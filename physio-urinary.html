Chapter 13: Urinary System

The Urinary System maintains fluid balance, electrolyte balance, acid–base balance, blood pressure, and removes metabolic waste.

1. Components of the Urinary System

The Mnemonic: KUUB

Kidneys, Ureters, Urinary bladder, B (Urethra)

2. Functions of the Urinary System

The Mnemonic: WEB RAP

Waste excretion, Electrolyte balance, Blood pressure regulation, RBC production (Erythropoietin), Acid-base balance, Plasma volume regulation

3. Functions of the Kidney

The Mnemonic: FILTER

Filtration, Ion regulation, Liquid balance, Toxin removal, Endocrine functions, Renin secretion

4. Layers of the Kidney

The Mnemonic: CCP

Capsule, Cortex, P (Medulla - remember as "Pyramids")

5. Parts of the Kidney

The Mnemonic: CHPUP

Capsule, Hilum, Pyramids, U (Renal pelvis), Papilla

6. Structures at the Renal Hilum

The Mnemonic: VAP (Front to Back)

Renal Vein, Renal Artery, Renal Pelvis (Ureter)

7. Functional Unit of Kidney

The Mnemonic: Nephron = Kidney Worker

The nephron is the structural and functional unit of the kidney.

8. Parts of the Nephron

The Mnemonic: BGPDTC

Bowman's capsule, Glomerulus, Proximal convoluted tubule (PCT), Descending limb, Thick ascending limb / Distal convoluted tubule (DCT), Collecting duct

9. Renal Corpuscle

The Mnemonic: BG

Bowman's capsule, Glomerulus

10. Stages of Urine Formation

The Mnemonic: FSR

Filtration, Selective reabsorption, Recretion (Secretion)

11. Site of Filtration

The Mnemonic: G Filters

Glomerulus performs filtration.

12. Maximum Reabsorption Occurs In

The Mnemonic: PCT = Plenty Comes Together

Most water, glucose, amino acids, and ions are reabsorbed in the PCT.

13. Loop of Henle

The Mnemonic: Down Water, Up Salt

Descending limb → Water leaves. Ascending limb → Na⁺, K⁺, Cl⁻ leave.

14. Distal Convoluted Tubule

The Mnemonic: DCT = Fine Tuning

Regulated by aldosterone and PTH.

15. Collecting Duct

The Mnemonic: ADH Acts Here

ADH increases water reabsorption.

16. Juxtaglomerular Apparatus (JGA)

The Mnemonic: MJG

Macula densa, Juxtaglomerular cells, Granular cells

17. Renin–Angiotensin–Aldosterone System

The Mnemonic: RAAS Raises Pressure

Renin, Angiotensin II, Aldosterone, Sodium and water retention

18. Kidney Hormones

The Mnemonic: RED

Renin, Erythropoietin, D (Active Vitamin D - Calcitriol)

19. Ureter Constrictions

The Mnemonic: PUV

Pelvi-ureteric junction, U (Crossing iliac vessels), Vesicoureteric junction

20. Layers of the Ureter

The Mnemonic: MMA

Mucosa, Muscularis, Adventitia

21. Parts of the Urinary Bladder

The Mnemonic: ABN

Apex, Body, Neck

22. Trigone of the Bladder

The Mnemonic: 2U + 1U

Two ureteric openings, One urethral opening

23. Urethra (Male)

The Mnemonic: PMS

Prostatic, Membranous, Spongy

24. Female Urethra

The Mnemonic: Short & Straight

Length is approximately 4 cm.

25. Normal Urine Characteristics

The Mnemonic: YACS

Yellow, Acidic (slightly), Clear, Sterile

26. Normal Urine pH

The Mnemonic: 6 is Normal

Average urine pH ≈ 6 (range 4.5–8).

27. Normal Urine Output

The Mnemonic: 1.5 Liters

Average adult urine output ≈ 1–2 L/day.

28. Blood Supply of the Kidney

The Mnemonic: RA → RV

Renal artery → Renal vein

29. Common Kidney Diseases

The Mnemonic: KSGN

Kidney stones, S (Renal failure), Glomerulonephritis, Nephrotic syndrome

30. Kidney Functions Summary

The Mnemonic: FREE

Filtration, Reabsorption, Excretion, Endocrine functions
