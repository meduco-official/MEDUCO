Subject: Dermatology

Master skin anatomy, genodermatoses, bullous diseases, histology, and pharmacology with these 45 high-yield mnemonics.

Anatomy & Inflammatory Disorders

1. Epidermal Layers (Superficial to Deep)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Come, Let's Get Sunburned

Corneum, Lucidum, Granulosum, Spinosum, Basale.

2. Primary Skin Lesions

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-P-N

Macule (Flat), Papule (Raised, small), Nodule (Raised, deep/large).

3. Lichen Planus Presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: The 6 P's

Purple, Polygonal, Planar (Flat-topped), Pruritic (Itchy), Papules, Plaques.

4. Koebner Phenomenon (Isomorphic Response)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Little Plans Kan Make Win Very Decent

Lichen planus, Psoriasis, Kaposi sarcoma, Molluscum contagiosum, Warts, Vitiligo, DLE (Discoid Lupus Erythematosus).

5. Dermatitis Herpetiformis (Dietary Restrictions)

🧠 The Mnemonic: BROW

Barley, Rye, Oat, Wheat.

Drug-Induced Conditions

6. Drugs Causing Pemphigus

🧠 The Mnemonic: PCR

Penillin / Penicillamine (Most common), Captopril, Rifampicin.

7. Drugs Causing Psoriasis Exacerbation

🧠 The Mnemonic: PLEB

Painkillers (NSAIDs), Lithium, Antimalarials / ACE inhibitors, Beta-blockers.

8. Drugs Causing SJS/TEN

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLEAR SOAP

Carbamazepine, Lamotrigine, Efavirenz, Allopurinol, Rifampicin, Sulfonamides, Oxicams (NSAIDs), Anticonvulsants (e.g., Phenytoin), Penicillin.

9. Drugs Causing Acne Vulgaris

🧠 The Mnemonic: PIMPLE

Phenytoin, Isoniazid (Most common in India), Methylcobalamin, Progesterone OCPs, Lithium, EGFR inhibitors.

10. Drug-Induced SLE

🧠 The Mnemonic: HIPP

Hydralazine, Isoniazid, Procainamide, Phenytoin.

Dermatopathology & Tumours

11. Herpes Virus Cytology (Tzanck Smear)

🧠 The Mnemonic: The 3 M's

Multinucleation, Molding (Nuclear), Margination of chromatin.

12. Neutrophils in Stratum Corneum

🧠 The Mnemonic: PTICS

Psoriasis, Tinea, Impetigo, Candida, Syphilis.

13. Subepidermal Split + Neutrophils in Papillae

🧠 The Mnemonic: PLAID

Pemphigoid (Bullous), Lupus (Bullous SLE), Acquisita (Epidermolysis Bullosa Acquisita), IgA (Linear IgA disease), Dermatitis herpetiformis.

14. Types of Tissue Necrosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: Life Can Get Complicated

Liquefactive, Coagulation, Gangrenous, Caseous.

15. Melanoma Warning Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDE

Asymmetry, Border irregularity, Color variation, Diameter (>6mm), Evolving/Changing.

16. Painful Skin Tumours

🧠 The Mnemonic: LEND AN EGG

Leiomyoma, Eccrine spiradenoma, Neuroma, Dermatofibroma, Angiolipoma, Neurilemmoma, Endometriosis, Granular cell tumor, Glomus tumor.

17. Generalized Hyperpigmentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPARED

Skin SPARED (Baseline), Pregnancy, Addison’s disease, Renal failure, Excess iron (Hemochromatosis), Drugs (Amiodarone, Minocycline).

18. Syphilis Manifestations

🧠 The Mnemonic: Cphilless

Painless genital ulcer, Less pain lymphadenopathy (Painless nodes).

19. Albright Syndrome

🧠 The Mnemonic: GAME OF

Giant melanosomes, Alopecia, Macules (Café au lait spots), Endocrine dysfunction, Osteomas, Fibrous dysplasia.

20. Nevus of Ota vs. Ito Location

🧠 The Mnemonic: O vs I

Ota = Ophthalmic/Ocular distribution.
Ito = Interscapular/Thoracic distribution.

Clinical Signs & Physical Exam

21. Pityriasis Rosea Distribution

🧠 The Mnemonic: CHRISTMAS TREE

Central trunk location, Herald patch occurs first, Rib-like parallel orientation, Intense itching occasionally, Scaling at borders (collarette).

22. Nikolsky Sign Positive Conditions

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-S-S

Pemphigus vulgaris, Scalded skin syndrome (SSSS), Stevens-Johnson Syndrome / TEN.

23. Acanthosis Nigricans Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-E-D-S

Malignancy (Gastric cancer), Endocrine (Diabetes, PCOS), Drugs (Steroids, Nicotinic acid), Syndromes (Down syndrome).

24. Erythema Nodosum Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-H-I-T S-U-I-T

Streptococcal infections, Histoplasmosis, IBD (Crohn's/UC), Tuberculosis, Sulfonamides, Unknown (Idiopathic), Indigestible things (Sarcoidosis), Travel vaccines.

25. Pyoderma Gangrenosum Associations

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-A-P-A

Pyoderma gangrenosum, Acne (Severe), Post-injection flare, Arthritis (Sterile).

26. Yellow Nail Syndrome Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: L-Y-N

Lymphedema, Yellow nails, Nonasthmatic respiratory disease (Pleural effusion).

27. Sweat Gland Distribution (Apocrine)

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-A-A

Axilla, Areola, Anus / Anogenital region.

Bacterial, Viral & Fungal Infections

28. Leprosy Types

🧠 The Mnemonic: T-T to L-L

Tuberculoid (High immunity, asymmetric) → Towards Borderline → Lepromatous (Low immunity, symmetric).

29. Tinea Versicolor Presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-P-A

Scales (Fine), Patchy discoloration, Aspaghetti and meatballs appearance (Microscopy).

30. Scabies Classic Distribution

🧠 The Mnemonic: W-A-V-E

Web spaces of fingers, Axillary folds, Volar wrists, External genitalia.

31. Rickettsial Pox Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: E-F-R

Eschar at bite site, Fever, Rash (Papulovesicular).

32. Erysipeloid Transmission Vector

🧠 The Mnemonic: F-F-P

Fish handlers, Farmers, Poultry workers.

33. Secondary Syphilis Rashes

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-A-M

Bilateral distribution, Affects palms and soles, Maculopapular appearance.

Genodermatoses & Pediatric Syndromes

34. Neurofibromatosis Type 1 (NF1)

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-A-F-E S-P-O-T

Café-au-lait macules, Axillary/Inguinal freckling, Fibromas (Neurofibromas), Eye Lisch nodules, Skeletal dysplasia, Pedigree (Autosomal Dominant), Optic glioma, Tumour risk increased.

35. Tuberous Sclerosis Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-S-H-L-E-A-F

Ash-leaf spots, Shagreen patches, Heart rhabdomyoma, Lung lymphangiomyomatosis, Epilepsy (Seizures), Angiomyolipoma (Renal), Facial angiofibromas.

36. Incontinentia Pigmenti Stages

🧠 The Mnemonic: V-V-H-A

Vesicular stage, Verrucous stage, Hyperpigmented stage, Atrophic/Hypopigmented stage.

37. Peutz-Jeghers Syndrome Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-O-P

Pigment around lips/mucosa, Organ polyps (Hamartomas), Prone to GI malignancy.

38. Gorlin Syndrome (Basal Cell Nevus)

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-O-B

BCCs at an early age, Odontogenic keratocysts, Bifid ribs.

39. Wiskott-Aldrich Syndrome

🧠 The Mnemonic: W-I-P-E

Wiskott-Aldrich, Immunodeficiency, Purpura (Thrombocytopenia), Eczema.

40. Job Syndrome (Hyper-IgE)

🧠 The Mnemonic: F-A-T-E

Facial features coarse, Abscesses (Cold, staphylococcal), Teeth retention (Primary teeth), Elevated IgE / Eczema.

Histopathology & Immunology

41. Lichen Planus Histology

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-A-W

Saw-tooth rete ridges, Acanthosis, Wedge-shaped hypergranulosis.

42. Psoriasis Pathology Changes

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-A-P-S

Munro microabscesses, Acanthosis, Parakeratosis, Spongiosis reduced.

43. Granuloma Annulare Character

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-A-L-E

Palisading histiocytes, Altered collagen, Lymphocytic infiltrate, Extracellular mucin.

44. Pemphigus Vulgaris Immunofluorescence

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-H-I-C-K-E-N

Chicken-wire appearance (IgG deposition in epidermis).

45. Bullous Pemphigoid Immunofluorescence

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-A-S-E

Basement membrane zone, Along the dermal-epidermal junction, Smooth, linear IgG/C3 deposition.
