Chapter 2: Cell

As a medical student, learning the human body means understanding thousands of physiological concepts and complex pathways. Remembering such a vast amount of information can feel challenging during exams. That's why using these simple memory techniques will transform difficult topics into concepts that are easy to recall with confidence.

1. Cell Membrane Functions

The Mnemonic: PRoTECTS

Protection, Receptors, Transport, Electrical activity, Cell communication, Tissue attachment, Selective permeability

2. Cell Organelles

The Mnemonic: My Rough Smooth Golgi Likes Pretty Tiny Peroxisomes

Mitochondria, Rough ER, Smooth ER, Golgi, Lysosome, Peroxisome, Transport vesicles

3. Functions of Rough ER

The Mnemonic: Rough = Ribosomes = Protein

Protein synthesis, Secretory proteins, Membrane proteins

4. Functions of Smooth ER

The Mnemonic: Smooth Makes Lipids & Steroids

Lipid synthesis, Steroid synthesis, Drug detoxification, Calcium storage

5. Golgi Apparatus Functions

The Mnemonic: MMPS

Modifies proteins, Matures proteins, Packages proteins, Secretes proteins

6. Lysosome Functions

The Mnemonic: Digest & Destroy

Intracellular digestion, Autophagy, Autolysis, Defense

7. Peroxisome Functions

The Mnemonic: PerOXisome removes OXidants

Breaks H₂O₂, Fatty acid oxidation, Detoxification

8. Cytoskeleton

The Mnemonic: MAI

Microtubules, Actin filaments, Intermediate filaments

9. Functions of Cytoskeleton

The Mnemonic: SHAPE

Support, Hold organelles, Active movement, Protein transport, Endocytosis

10. Passive Transport

The Mnemonic: No ATP

Simple diffusion, Facilitated diffusion, Osmosis, Filtration

11. Active Transport

The Mnemonic: ATP Always Pays

Needs ATP. Examples: Na⁺/K⁺ pump, Ca²⁺ pump, H⁺ pump

12. Sodium Potassium Pump

The Mnemonic: 3 OUT 2 IN

3 Na⁺ out, 2 K⁺ in (Uses 1 ATP). Extracellular fluid has higher solute concentration; Cytoplasm has lower solute concentration.

13. Diffusion Factors

The Mnemonic: CASA

Concentration gradient, Area, Solubility, Amount of Thickness (inverse)

14. Osmosis

The Mnemonic: Water Wants More Solute

Water moves from Low solute → High solute

15. Endocytosis

The Mnemonic: PEM

Phagocytosis, Endocytosis, Micropinocytosis

16. Types of Endocytosis

The Mnemonic: PRP

Phagocytosis, Receptor-mediated, Pinocytosis

17. Exocytosis

The Mnemonic: EXIT = Exocytosis

Moves substances outside cell.

18. Resting Membrane Potential

The Mnemonic: Neurons Love -70

Neuron = −70 mV

19. Causes of Resting Membrane Potential

The Mnemonic: PKL

Potassium leak, K⁺ permeability, Leakage via Na⁺/K⁺ pump

20. Cell Junctions

The Mnemonic: TGD

Tight junction, Gap junction, Desmosome

21. Tight Junction

The Mnemonic: Tight = No Leak

Prevents fluid from leaking between cells.

22. Gap Junction

The Mnemonic: Gap = Communication

Allows rapid signal transmission between cells.

23. Desmosome

The Mnemonic: Desmo = Strong

Anchors cells together strongly.

24. Membrane Proteins

The Mnemonic: IRC

Integral, Receptor, Carrier

25. Components of Plasma Membrane

The Mnemonic: PLC

Phospholipids, Lipids (cholesterol), Cell Proteins

26. Fluid Mosaic Model

The Mnemonic: Fluid + Floating Proteins

Proposed by Singer & Nicolson (1972).

27. Functions of Mitochondria

The Mnemonic: ATP POWER

ATP production, Phosphorylation (Oxidative), Heat generation, Calcium storage, Apoptosis

28. Cell Cycle

The Mnemonic: I Prefer Making Apples

Interphase, Prophase, Metaphase, Anaphase, Telophase

29. Interphase

The Mnemonic: GSG

G₁, S, G₂

30. ATP Production

The Mnemonic: Glycolysis → Krebs → ETC

The stages of cellular respiration: Glycolysis, Krebs cycle, Electron Transport Chain

31. Cell Death

The Mnemonic: ANA

Apoptosis, Necrosis, Autophagy

32. Apoptosis Features

The Mnemonic: SAFE

Shrinkage, Apoptotic bodies, Fragmentation of DNA, Energy dependent

33. Necrosis Features

The Mnemonic: SWELL

Swelling, Membrane rupture, Leakage, Inflammation
