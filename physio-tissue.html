Chapter 3: Tissue of Human Body

The human body is made up of 4 basic types of tissues. Use the high-yield memory techniques below to master their classifications, functions, and cellular structures.

1. Human Body Tissues

The Mnemonic: ECMN (or "Every Child Must Nurse")

Epithelial Tissue, Connective Tissue, Muscle Tissue, Nervous Tissue

2. Epithelial Tissue Functions

The Mnemonic: PADS

Protection, Absorption, Diffusion/Secretion, Sensation

3. Simple Epithelium Types

The Mnemonic: SCCCP

Simple Squamous, Simple Cuboidal, Simple Columnar, Ciliated Columnar, Pseudostratified Columnar

4. Stratified Epithelium Types

The Mnemonic: SCCC

Stratified Squamous, Stratified Cuboidal, Stratified Columnar, Transitional (Changeable)

5. Cell Junctions

The Mnemonic: TGD

Tight junction, Gap junction, Desmosome

6. Connective Tissue Components

The Mnemonic: CFG

Cells, Fibres, Ground substance

7. Types of Connective Tissue

The Mnemonic: PDSF

Proper connective tissue, Dense connective tissue, Specialized connective tissue, Fluid connective tissue

8. Connective Tissue Fibres

The Mnemonic: CRE

Collagen, Reticular, Elastic

9. Cells of Connective Tissue

The Mnemonic: FMPCAL

Fibroblast, Mast cell, Plasma cell, Macrophage (C for Macrophage equivalent), Adipocyte, Leukocyte

10. Types of Muscle Tissue

The Mnemonic: SSC

Skeletal, Smooth, Cardiac

11. Skeletal Muscle

The Mnemonic: VMS

Voluntary, Multinucleated, Striated

12. Smooth Muscle

The Mnemonic: NUS

Non-striated, Uninucleated, Involuntary (Smooth)

13. Cardiac Muscle

The Mnemonic: SBI

Striated, Branched, Involuntary

14. Nervous Tissue Components

The Mnemonic: NG

Neurons, Neuroglia

15. Parts of a Neuron

The Mnemonic: DAB

Dendrite, Axon, Cell Body

16. Neuroglia in CNS

The Mnemonic: MOAM

Microglia, Oligodendrocytes, Astrocytes, Ependymal cells (M for Macrophage-like equivalent)

17. Neuroglia in PNS

The Mnemonic: SS

Schwann cells, Satellite cells

High-Yield Comparison

Tissue Function Example
Epithelial Covers and lines surfaces Skin, intestine
Connective Supports and binds Bone, cartilage, blood
Muscle Movement Skeletal, cardiac, smooth muscle
Nervous Conducts impulses Brain, spinal cord, nerves
