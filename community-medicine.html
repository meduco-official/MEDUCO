🧠 The Mnemonic: RESPECCT

🧠 The Mnemonic: SNOB

12. Screening Test Attributes (Specificity)

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPIN

16. Iceberg Phenomenon Exceptions

🧠 The Mnemonic: VIP
