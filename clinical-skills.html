Subject: Psychiatry

Master the complete spectrum of DSM-5 diagnostic criteria, personality clusters, childhood disorders, and essential psychopharmacology.

Mood Disorders & Psychosis

1. Major Depressive Disorder Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: SIG E CAPS

Sleep changes, Interest loss (anhedonia), Guilt or worthlessness, Energy lack, Concentration difficulties, Appetite/weight changes, Psychomotor agitation or retardation, Suicidal ideation.

2. Mania Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: DIG FAST

Distractibility, Impulsivity/Indiscretion, Grandiosity, Flight of ideas, Activity increase, Sleep deficit (decreased need), Talkativeness (pressured speech).

3. Schizophrenia Symptoms & Rule-outs

🧠 The Mnemonic: DELUSIONS

Delusions, Emotional blunting (flat affect), Logic impairment, Unrealistic hallucinations, Social withdrawal, Illness onset > 6 months, Other organic causes ruled out, Negative symptoms, Speech disorganization.

Anxiety, Trauma & Personality Disorders

4. Generalized Anxiety Disorder Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: WATCHERS

Worry, Anxiety, Tension in muscles, Concentration loss, Hyperarousal, Energy loss (fatigue), Restlessness, Sleep disturbances.

5. Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder Core Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: STUD

Situation re-experienced (flashbacks/nightmares), Trauma-related stimuli avoided, Unable to function (negative mood/cognition), Distress or hyperarousal.

6. Cluster A Personality Disorders (Odd/Eccentric)

🧠 The Mnemonic: WILD

Weird (General description), Isolated (Schizoid), Lost in space (Schizotypal), Distrustful (Paranoid).

7. Cluster B Personality Disorders (Dramatic/Erratic)

🧠 The Mnemonic: BAM

Borderline, Antisocial, Mantles of drama (Histrionic & Narcissistic).

8. Cluster C Personality Disorders (Anxious/Fearful)

🧠 The Mnemonic: COWARD

Compulsive (Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder), Overly dependent (Dependent), Withdrawn and Anxious (Avoidant).

9. Borderline Personality Disorder Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: AM PREJUDICE

Abandonment fears, Mood instability, Paranoia/dissociation under stress, Relationship instability, Emptiness feelings, Judgment lack (impulsivity), Unstable self-image, Destructive behaviors (self-harm), Intense anger, Control issues, Empathy gaps during splitting.

10. Antisocial Personality Disorder Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: CORRUPT

Conformity to law lacking, Owning up absent (deceitfulness), Reckless disregard for safety, Unplanning (impulsivity), Remorse absent, Punishing aggression (irritability), Trustworthiness lacking (irresponsibility).

Substance Use & Medical Psychiatry

11. Alcohol Use Screening

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAGE

Cut down (Have you felt you should?), Annoyed (Have people annoyed you by criticizing?), Guilty (Felt bad or guilty?), Eye-opener (Drink first thing in the morning?).

12. Alcohol Withdrawal Stages (in order)

🧠 The Mnemonic: PAST

Psychomotor agitation/tremors (6-36 hours), Allucinosis (12-48 hours), Seizures (24-48 hours), Tremens - Delirium Tremens (48-96 hours).

13. Causes of Delirium

🧠 The Mnemonic: I'M DELIRIOUS

Infection, Metabolic changes, Drugs, Endocrine disorders, Lesions in the brain, Intoxication/withdrawal, Renal/hepatic failure, Inmanition (malnutrition/dehydration), Oxygen deficit (hypoxia), Uremia, Sensory deprivation/overload.

14. Suicide Risk Assessment

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAD PERSONS

Sex (Male), Age (<19 or >45), Depression, Previous attempt, Ethanol/substance use, Rational thinking loss, Social support lacking, Organized plan, No spouse, Sickness (chronic illness).

Psychopharmacology & Side Effects

15. Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: FEVER

Fever (hyperthermia), Encephalopathy (altered mental status), Vitals unstable (autonomic instability), Elevated enzymes (CPK), Rigidity ("lead-pipe" muscle rigidity).

16. Serotonin Syndrome Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: HARMED

Hyperthermia, Autonomic instability, Reflexes increased (hyperreflexia/clonus), Mental status changes, Enteric symptoms (diarrhea), Dilated pupils (mydriasis).

17. Antipsychotic Extrapyramidal Side Effects (Timeline Order)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Stuck In The Mud

Spasms (Acute Dystonia - hours to days), Inability to sit still (Akathisia - days to weeks), Tremors (Parkinsonism - weeks to months), Mouth movements (Tardive Dyskinesia - months to years).

18. Lithium Toxicity & Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: LITHIUM

Leukocytosis, Insipidus (Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus), Tremors/Teratogenesis (Ebstein's anomaly), Hypothyroidism, Increased weight, Unpleasant GI upset, Miscellaneous ECG changes.

19. MAOI Hypertensive Crisis Triggers (Tyramine Foods)

🧠 The Mnemonic: TYRANT

Tasty aged cheeses, Yeast extracts (Marmite), Red wine, Aged meats/salami, Never-fresh fish, Tofu/soy sauce.

20. Mental Status Examination Components

🧠 The Mnemonic: ASEPTIC

Appearance and behavior, Speech, Emotion (Mood and affect), Perception (Hallucinations/illusions), Thought content and process, Insight and judgment, Cognition.

Diagnosis & Clinical Criteria

21. Panic Attack Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: PANICS

Palpitations, Abdominal distress, Numbness (paresthesias), Intense fear of dying/going crazy, Chest pain or Choking sensation, Sweating, Shaking, or Shortness of breath.

22. PTSD Diagnostic Clusters

🧠 The Mnemonic: DREAMS

Disrupted sleep/Hyperarousal, Re-experiencing (flashbacks), Evading stimuli (avoidance), Affective changes (negative mood), Month or more of symptoms, Significant distress/functional impairment.

23. Agoraphobia Feared Situations

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPACE

Spaces that are open, Public transportation, Areas enclosed (shops/theatres), Crowds or standing in line, Exit from home alone.

24. Binge Eating Disorder Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: BEDS

Binge episodes without purging, Eating rapidly or until uncomfortably full, Distress regarding eating habits, Secretive eating due to embarrassment.

25. Anorexia Nervosa Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: REFUSE

Restriction of energy intake, Extreme fear of gaining weight, Fat body image distortion, Underweight status (low BMI), Severe restriction of types of food, Excessive exercise.

26. Bulimia Nervosa Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: PURGE

Purging behaviors (vomiting, laxatives), Uncontrolled binge eating episodes, Recurrent compensatory behaviors, Guilt and body shape obsession, Esteem overly influenced by weight.

27. Phobia Presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: DREAD

Disproportionate fear of an object/situation, Recurrent immediate anxiety response, Endured with intense distress, Avoidance of the trigger, Debilitating effect on daily life.

28. Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder Themes

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLEAN

Contamination doubts, Losing control fears, Exactness/symmetry needs, Aggressive or taboo thoughts, Neutralizing compulsions (washing, checking).

29. Intermittent Explosive Disorder Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: FIGHT

Frequent verbal or physical outbursts, Impulsive and unplanned aggression, Grossly disproportionate to the provocation, Harm or distress caused to self/others, Three months minimum frequency.

30. Kleptomania Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: THEFT

Tension build-up before stealing, Heist not done for monetary value, Emulation of relief/pleasure after the act, Failure to resist impulses to steal, Theft not done out of anger or vengeance.

31. Pyromania Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: FIRE

Fascinated by fire and its contexts, Intense tension/arousal before setting fire, Relief or gratification when witnessing fires, Exclusion of conduct disorder or manic episodes.

32. Gambling Disorder Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: GAMBLE

Gambling with increasing amounts of money, Anxious or irritable when trying to cut down, Mind preoccupied with gambling past/future, Bailing out financial crises using others, Lying to conceal involvement, Ejeep/jeopardizing relationships or career.

33. Adjustment Disorder Timing

🧠 The Mnemonic: SO FAR

Symptoms start within 3 months of stressor, Out of proportion to severity of stressor, Functioning is significantly impaired, Absent other psychiatric diagnosis, Resolves within 6 months once stressor stops.

34. Factitious Disorder / Munchausen

🧠 The Mnemonic: FABRICATED

Falsification of physical or psychological signs, Assuming the sick role intentionally, Behavior present even without external rewards, Repeatedly seeking medical interventions, Injury or disease induction, Compulsive medical shopping, Attention-seeking motivation, Truth hidden from medical staff, Extensive surgical history / scars, Deceptive clinical presentation.

35. Malingering

🧠 The Mnemonic: FRAUD

Fabricating symptoms for external gain, Rewards are clear (money, avoiding work/jail), Anti-social personality traits often present, Uncooperative with medical evaluations, Discrepancy between subjective complaints and objective findings.

Personality Disorders & Specific Traits

36. Paranoid Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: GET SAFE

Grudges held for a long time, Exploitation expected from others, Trust issues with friends/family, Spousal infidelity suspected without proof, Attacks on character perceived quickly, Fear of confiding in others, Everything read as hidden demeaning meanings.

37. Schizoid Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: DISTANT

Detached or flat affect, Indifferent to criticism or praise, Sexual experiences hold little interest, Tasks performed alone, Absence of close friends, Neither desires nor enjoys close relationships, Takes pleasure in few activities.

38. Schizotypal Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: ME ECCENTRIC

Magical thinking or odd beliefs, Experiences unusual perceptual illusions, Eccentric behavior or appearance, Constricted or inappropriate affect, Cognitive/perceptual distortions, Excessive social anxiety, No close friends, Thoughts and speech are odd, Referential thinking, Interpersonal deficits, Cusp of schizophrenia.

39. Antisocial Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: CALLOUS

Conformity to social norms lacking, Aggressive and irritable, Legal problems frequently recurring, Lying and deceitfulness, Obligatory debts/responsibilities ignored, Unconcerned for safety, Shame/remorse completely absent.

40. Borderline Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: DESPERATE

Desperate efforts to avoid abandonment, Emptiness felt chronically, Suicidal behaviors or self-mutilation, Paranoia or dissociation, Empathic splitting (idealization vs devaluation), Rage that is intense, Affective instability, Targetless or unstable self-image, Erratic impulsivity.

41. Histrionic Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: PRAISE ME

Provocative or sexually seductive behavior, Relationships considered more intimate than they are, Attention-seeking, Influenced easily by others, Speech is impressionistic, Emotional expression is shallow, Makeup/physical appearance used to draw attention, Exaggerated emotions.

42. Narcissistic Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPECIAL

Special/unique belief about self, Preoccupied with fantasies of unlimited success, Entitlement sense, Arrogant behaviors, Conceited/grandiose self-importance, Interpersonally exploitative, Admiration required excessively, Lacks empathy.

43. Avoidant Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: CRINGE

Criticism or rejection preoccupies thoughts, Restrained in intimate relationships, Inhibited in new interpersonal situations, New activities avoided, Gets close to people only if certain of being liked, Embarrassment feared.

44. Dependent Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: RELIANT

Reassurance required for everyday decisions, Expression of disagreement is difficult, Lacks initiative, Individual needs subordinated to nurturance, Alone and helpless feelings, New relationship sought immediately, Tasks shifted to others.

45. Obsessive-Compulsive Personality Disorder

🧠 The Mnemonic: LAW FIRM

Loses the point of an activity due to detail obsession, Ability to finish tasks compromised, Work-devoted at the expense of leisure, Flexibility lacking, Inability to discard worthless items, Reluctant to delegate, Miserly spending style.

Substance Use, Abuse & Withdrawal

46. Cholinergic Crisis / Organophosphate Poisoning

🧠 The Mnemonic: SLUDGE

Salivation, Lacrimation, Urination, Defecation, GI upset (cramps), Emesis.

47. Methanol Poisoning Symptoms/Findings

🧠 The Mnemonic: MATH

Metabolic acidosis, Abdominal pain, Tremors/altered mental status, Hazy vision (snowstorm vision).

48. Cocaine/Amphetamine Withdrawal

🧠 The Mnemonic: CRASH

Craving intensely, Retarded psychomotor movements or agitation, Anhedonia and severe dysphoria, Sleep changes (hypersomnia), Hunger increased (hyperphagia).

49. Opioid Overdose Triage

🧠 The Mnemonic: ARREST

Altered mental status (coma), Respiratory depression, Rate of heart dropping (bradycardia), Eyes showing miosis (pinpoint pupils), Skin is cool and clammy, Treatment needed instantly (Naloxone).

50. Thyroid Storm vs. Sympathomimetic Toxicity Differentiation

🧠 The Mnemonic: STORM

Sweating profusely, Tachycardia, Out of control fever, Restlessness and agitation, Mental status changes (delirium).

51. Wernicke-Korsakoff Syndrome Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: WET BERI

Wernicke encephalopathy (acute), Eye findings (ophthalmoplegia/nystagmus), Trunk ataxia, B1 deficiency (Thiamine), Evident confabulation (Korsakoff), Retrograde/Anterograde amnesia, Irreversible if untreated.

52. Benzodiazepine/Barbiturate Intoxication

🧠 The Mnemonic: BUZZ

Behavioral changes (disinhibition), Unsteady gait (ataxia), Zipped speech (slurred), Zoned out (somnolence/stupor).

53. Sedative-Hypnotic Withdrawal

🧠 The Mnemonic: SHAKES

Sweating and tachycardia, Hand tremors, Anxiety and agitation, Kicking reflexes (hyperreflexia), Eptic seizures (grand mal), Seeing things (illusions/hallucinations).

54. Opioid Withdrawal Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: WIND

Pre-Anesthetic Assessment & Preparation

1. Standard Room Setup

🧠 The Mnemonic: SOAPM

Suction setup and working, Oxygen and backup supplies, Airway equipment, Pharmacy (induction agents/emergency drugs), Monitors (ECG, SpO2, NIBP, EtCO2).

2. Patient History

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAMPLE

Signs and symptoms, Allergies, Medications, Past medical history, Last oral intake (NPO status), Events leading up to anesthesia.

3. Airway Visualisation Score

🧠 The Mnemonic: MALAMPATI

Mouth opening, Anatomy visible, Look for tonsils/pillars, Access difficulty estimation, Mallampati classification (Class I to IV).

Difficult Airway Management

4. Predicting Difficult Intubation

🧠 The Mnemonic: LEMON

Look externally (facial trauma, small jaw), Evaluate 3-3-2 rule, Mallampati score, Obstruction (epiglottitis, abscess), Neck mobility (cervical spine issues).

5. Predicting Difficult Extraglottic Device / LMA Placement

🧠 The Mnemonic: RODS

Restriction (high airway pressures), Obstruction (upper airway masses), Disrupted or distorted airway, Stiff lungs or cervical spine.

6. Predicting Difficult Mask Ventilation

🧠 The Mnemonic: MOANS

Mask seal (beards, facial trauma), Obesity or Obstruction, Age (> 55 years), No teeth (edentulous), Stiff lungs (asthma, COPD, ARDS).

7. Predicting Difficult Mask Ventilation (Alternative)

🧠 The Mnemonic: BONES

Beard, Obesity, No teeth, Elderly, Snorer / Sleep Apnea.

8. Predicting Difficult Surgical Airway / Cricothyroidotomy

🧠 The Mnemonic: SMART

Surgery (previous neck scars), Mass (tumor, hematoma), Access/Anatomy (obesity, short neck), Radiation (prior neck radiation), Trauma (laryngeal fractures).

9. Predicting Difficult Surgical Airway (Alternative)

🧠 The Mnemonic: SHORT

Surgery (previous neck scars), Hematoma or infection, Obesity, Radiation history, Tumor or trauma.

Equipment & Checklist Protocols

10. Standard Room Setup Checklist

🧠 The Mnemonic: MSMAID

Machine check, Suction, Monitors, Airway tools, IV access supplies, Drugs (induction, paralytics).

11. Endotracheal Tube Placement Verification

🧠 The Mnemonic: STOP

See the tube pass through cords, Tomography (EtCO2 wave trace presence), Outflow (fogging in the tube), Pulmonary sounds (bilateral).

12. Troubleshooting Sudden Intubated Hypoxia

🧠 The Mnemonic: DOPE

Displacement of the tube, Obstruction (kinked tube, mucus plug), Pneumothorax, Equipment failure.

13. Airway Visualisation

14. Complications of Laryngoscopy

🧠 The Mnemonic: MESH

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDE

🧠 The Mnemonic: STRETCH

Size (Tidal volume), Trigger sensitivity, Rate (Respiratory rate), End-expiratory pressure (PEEP), Time (I:E ratio), Concentration (FiO2), High pressure limits.

Emergency Codes & Crisis Management

19. Reversible Causes of Cardiac Arrest / PEA (Hs)

🧠 The Mnemonic: 6 Hs

Hypovolemia, Hypoxia, Hydrogen ion excess (Acidosis), Hypo- / Hyperkalemia, Hypothermia, Hypoglycemia.

20. Reversible Causes of Cardiac Arrest / PEA (Ts)

🧠 The Mnemonic: 5 Ts

Tension pneumothorax, Tamponade (Cardiac), Toxins (Drug overdose, LAST), Thrombosis (Pulmonary embolus), Thrombosis (Coronary MI).

21. Malignant Hyperthermia Quick Interventions

🧠 The Mnemonic: FAST

Freeze (stop all volatile agents), Act (hyperventilate with 100% O2), Sodium Dantrolene immediately (2.5 mg/kg), Temperature control (cool the patient).

Thrombolytics / Anticoagulated patient, Infection at needle site, Lack of patient consent, True allergy to local anesthetics.

Cardiovascular & Hemodynamics

27. Management of Intraoperative Anaphylaxis

🧠 The Mnemonic: O SHIT

Oxygen (100%), Stop the triggering agent, Hypotension management (IV fluids), IV Epinephrine, Therapy (Steroids, Antihistamines).

28. Causes of Intraoperative Bradycardia

🧠 The Mnemonic: SICK

Surgical stimulation (Vagal reflex), Ischemia (Myocardial infaction), Cholinomimetics / Drugs, Potassium abnormalities.

29. Inotropes vs Vasopressors

🧠 The Mnemonic: PUSH

Phenylephrine (Pure alpha), Ultimate squeeze (Norepinephrine), Speed up (Isoproterenol), Heart squeeze (Dobutamine).

30. Aortic Stenosis Classic Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAD

Syncope, Angina, Dyspnea.

31. Managing Nitrous Oxide and Air Filled Cavities

🧠 The Mnemonic: PRELOAD

Pneumothorax, Retinal surgery (gas bubble), Embolism (Air), Liddle (Middle) ear surgery, Obstructed bowel → Avoid Delivery of N2O.

32. Perioperative Stroke/Anticoagulation Risk Stratification

🧠 The Mnemonic: CHADS2

Congestive heart failure, Hypertension, Age ≥ 75, Diabetes mellitus, Stroke or TIA history (2 points).

33. Systemic Inflammatory Response Syndrome (SIRS)

🧠 The Mnemonic: WARM

WBC count (<4k or >12k), Atmosphere/Temp (<36°C or >38°C), Respiratory rate (>20), Motor/Heart rate (>90).

34. Drugs Given via Endotracheal Tube (if IV access lost)

🧠 The Mnemonic: LEAF

Lidocaine, Epinephrine, Atropine, Flumazenil / Naloxone.

35. Transthoracic Echo Focus Areas in Trauma/Shock

🧠 The Mnemonic: FAST

Focused Assessment with Sonography for Trauma (Pericardial, Perihepatic, Perisplenic, Pelvic views).

Pharmacology & Anesthetic Drugs

36. Intravenous Induction Agents

🧠 The Mnemonic: PAPA

Propofol, Alfaxalone / Etomidate, Pentobarbital / Thiopental, Airway reflexes preserved (Ketamine).

37. Risk Factors for Post-Operative Nausea and Vomiting (PONV)

🧠 The Mnemonic: VOMIT

Vocal cords / ENT surgeries, Opioid use post-operatively, Motion sickness history, Inhalation anesthetics, Tobacco non-user.

38. Local Anesthetic Systemic Toxicity Treatment (LAST)

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCD

Airway management, Bolus of 20% Lipid Emulsion, Continuous infusion of Lipid Emulsion, Decline vasopressin and local anesthetics.

39. Opioid Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: I AM OPIUM

Itching, Altered mental status, Miosis, Orthostatic hypotension, Pulmonary depression, Ileus, Urinary retention, Muscle rigidity.

40. Factors Increasing Local Anesthetic Toxicity Risk

🧠 The Mnemonic: PUMP

Protein binding low, Un-ionized fraction high, Metabolism slow, Perfusion high at injection site.

41. Pre-treatment for Succinylcholine fasciculations

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCD

Atracurium, Cisatracurium, Curare, Defasciculating dose (1/10th of standard non-depolarizing blocker dose).

42. Physiological Changes Prolonging Neuromuscular Blockade

🧠 The Mnemonic: STILE

Severe hypothermia, Thyroid deficiency, Ion imbalance (HypoK, HypoCa, HyperMg), Liver failure, Encephalopathy / Acidosis.

43. Drugs causing CYP450 Enzyme Inhibition

🧠 The Mnemonic: SICK

Sodium valproate, Isoniazid, Cimetidine, Ketoconazole.

44. Drugs causing CYP450 Enzyme Induction

🧠 The Mnemonic: CARS

Carbamazepine, Alcohol (Chronic use), Rifampicin, Smoking / St. John's Wort.

🧠 The Mnemonic: TIME

Tissue solubility, Inspired gas concentration, Minute ventilation, Effective cardiac output.

🧠 The Mnemonic: PROP

Prostration / Heart failure, Rhabdomyolysis, Oxidative phosphorylation uncoupling, Prenal (Renal) failure / Hyperkalemia.

47. Hourly Maintenance Fluid Calculation

🧠 The Mnemonic: 4-2-1 Rule

4 mL/kg/hr for first 10 kg.
2 mL/kg/hr for next 10 kg.

🧠 The Mnemonic: C BIG K DROP

51. Causes of Hypercalcemia

🧠 The Mnemonic: CHIMPANZEES

Calcium excess, Hyperparathyroidism, Immobilization, Malignancy, Parathyroid hyperplasia, Alcoholism, Neoplasm, Zollinger-Ellison, Excess Vitamin D, Excess Vitamin A, Sarcoidosis.

🧠 The Mnemonic: SWOLLEN

Skin flushed, Weakness / Restlessness, Oedema, Low-grade fever, Lethargy, Extreme thirst, Neurological dysfunction.

53. Plasma volume expansion ratios

🧠 The Mnemonic: MD

Maximum 1:3 ratio for Crystalloids.
Direct 1:1 ratio for Colloids / Blood products.

54. Transfusion-Related Acute Lung Injury Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: TRALI

Transfusion within 6 hours, Respiratory distress, Acute onset, Lung infiltrates bilaterally, Idiopathic (No prior ALI).

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAFE

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPLAT

Sedatives, Paracetamol / Pain relievers, Local anesthetics, Atropine, Thiopental. (Neuromuscular blockers do NOT cross easily).

🧠 The Mnemonic: HELLP

🧠 The Mnemonic: BOHR

🧠 The Mnemonic: GAS

🧠 The Mnemonic: WIND

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAM

Subject: Radiology

Master imaging patterns, broad differentials, anatomical variants, and classic radiological signs across all imaging modalities.

General & Fundamentals

1. Chest X-ray Interpretation

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDE

Airway (trachea, bronchi), Breathing (lungs, pleura), Circulation (heart size, mediastinum), Disability/Bone (fractures, lytic lesions), Everything else/Lines (tubes, drains, foreign bodies).

2. Lucent Bone Lesions in Patients Under 30

🧠 The Mnemonic: LIPI

Langerhans cell histiocytosis (LCH), Infection (Osteomyelitis), Parasitic/Fibrous dysplasia, Infarction (Bone infarct).

3. Lytic Bone Lesions - Classic Broad Differential

🧠 The Mnemonic: FEGONOMICS

Fibrous dysplasia, Enchondroma / Eosinophilic granuloma, Giant cell tumor, Osteoblastoma, Non-ossifying fibroma (NOF), Osteoid osteoma, Metastasis / Myeloma, Infection (Osteomyelitis), Chondromyxoid fibroma / Cysts, Sarcoma (Osteosarcoma, Ewing).

4. Anterior Mediastinal Masses

🧠 The Mnemonic: 4 Ts

Thymoma, Teratoma (and other germ cell tumors), "Terrible" Lymphoma, Thyroid (retrosternal goiter).

5. Neurofibromatosis Type 2 Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: MISME

Multiple, Inherited, Schwannomas, Meningiomas, Ependymomas.

6. Basal Ganglia Calcification

🧠 The Mnemonic: STITCH

Stride-Dukes disease, Toxoplasmosis / Tuberous sclerosis, Idiopathic (Fahr disease), Trauma (Anoxia / CO poisoning), Congenital (TORCH), Hyperparathyroidism (and Hypo).

7. Ring-Enhancing Brain Lesions

🧠 The Mnemonic: MAGIC DR

Metastasis, Abscess, Glioblastoma multiforme (GBM), Infarct (subacute phase), Contusion, Demyelinating disease (Tumefactive MS), Radiation necrosis.

8. Pulmonary Embolism on V/Q Scan

🧠 The Mnemonic: PIOPED

Perfusion Intact Only Pulmonary Embolism Diagnosed (High probability means ≥ 2 mismatched segment defects).

9. Adrenal Mass Washout

🧠 The Mnemonic: Adenomas lose it fast

Absolute Washout ≥ 60% at 15 minutes indicates a benign adrenal adenoma.
Relative Washout ≥ 40% at 15 minutes also indicates an adenoma.

10. Causes of Ivory Vertebra

🧠 The Mnemonic: I AM SUICIDAL

Idiopathic, Avascular necrosis, Metastasis (Blastic), Sarcoma (Osteosarcoma), Unicameral bone cyst, Infection (chronic osteomyelitis), Carcinoid, Irradiation, Dish (DISH), Albers-Schönberg (Osteopetrosis), Lymphoma / Lymphangioma.

11. Pediatric Physeal Fractures

🧠 The Mnemonic: SALTER-Harris

Straight across (Type I), Above (Type II), Lower (Type III), Through (Type IV), ERasure of growth plate (Type V).

12. General Radiology Search Patterns

🧠 The Mnemonic: MINT

Metabolic / Mechanical, Inflammatory / Infectious, Neoplastic, Traumatic / Congenital.

13. Causes of Splenomegaly

🧠 The Mnemonic: VACCINES

Vascular (Portal HTN), Autoimmune (RA, SLE), Cellular proliferation (Leukemia), Congenital (Gaucher), Infectious (EBV, Malaria), Neoplastic (Hemangioma), Endocrine (Hyperthyroidism), Storage diseases (Amyloidosis).

14. Basilar Artery Occlusion Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: BATMAN

Blindness (cortical), Ataxia, Tetraplegia / Tetra-sensory loss, Muscle spasm / Myoclonus, Altered mental status, Nystagmus.

15. Paranasal Sinus Drainage

🧠 The Mnemonic: Water Travels Down Hill

Watershed (Frontal), Anterior Ethmoid, Maxillary → All drain into the Middle Meatus (except posterior ethmoid/sphenoid).

16. Ovarian Mucinous Cystadenoma Appearance

🧠 The Mnemonic: Paddle Wheel

Pseudomyxoma, Wheel-like multilocular cystic appearance on Ultrasound/CT.

17. Normal Esophageal Impressions

🧠 The Mnemonic: 3 Ds

Deformity by Aortic arch, Deformity by Left main bronchus, Deformity by Left atrium.

18. Clinical History Review Before Dictation

🧠 The Mnemonic: Old Carts

Onset, Location, Duration, Character, Aggravating factors, Radiating, Timing, Severity.

19. Reversible Ischemic Neurological Deficit

🧠 The Mnemonic: RIND

Reversible Ischemic Neurological Deficit (resolves within 24 hours to 3 weeks, distinct from TIA and stroke).

20. Intercostal Neurovascular Bundle Order

🧠 The Mnemonic: VAN

Vein (Highest), Artery (Middle), Nerve (Lowest, most vulnerable). Always place needles over the top of the lower rib to avoid this bundle.

Chest & Cardiothoracic

21. Causes of Peripheral Pulmonary Opacities

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPLIT

Sarcoidosis, Pulmonary infarction, Lymphoma, Infection (organizing pneumonia), Tumor (bronchioalveolar carcinoma).

22. Causes of Eggshell Calcification of Hilar Nodes

🧠 The Mnemonic: SHAVER

Silicosis, Histoplasmosis, Amyloidosis, Vital infections (treated Lymphoma), Ecchinococcosis, Radiation therapy.

23. Causes of Upper Lobe Pulmonary Fibrosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-A-S-T-A

Pneumoconiosis (Silicosis/Coal worker's), Ankylosing spondylitis, Sarcoidosis, TB (Tuberculosis), Allergic bronchopulmonary aspergillosis (ABPA).

24. Causes of Lower Lobe Pulmonary Fibrosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-A-D-G-E-R

Bronchiectasis, Asbestosis, Drug-induced (Amiodarone, Bleomycin), G-I reflux (chronic aspiration), Eosinophilic pneumonia, Rheumatoid arthritis / Scleroderma.

25. Causes of Cavitary Lung Lesions

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-A-V-I-T-Y

Cancer (Squamous cell), Autoimmune (Granulomatosis with polyangiitis), Vascular (Pulmonary emboli), Infection (Abscess, TB, Fungal), Trauma (Pneumatocele), Youth (Congenital cysts, CCAM).

26. Posterior Mediastinal Masses

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-E-D-I-A-S-T-I-N-U-M

Metastasis, Extramedullary hematopoiesis, Diverticulum (Zenker), Inflammation (Abscess), Aortic aneurysm, Sequestration, Thoracic duct cyst, Infection (Spine), Neurogenic tumors, Unknown vascular anomalies, Meningocele.

27. Aortic Coarctation Chest X-Ray Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: 3 Signs of Coarctation

3: "Figure of 3" sign on PA view.
E: Reverse "E" sign on barium swallow.
R: Rib notchings (3rd to 8th ribs from collateral flow).

28. Garland Triad of Sarcoidosis Adenopathy

🧠 The Mnemonic: 1-2-3 Sign

1: Right paratracheal lymphadenopathy.
2: Right hilar lymphadenopathy.
3: Left hilar lymphadenopathy.

29. Causes of High-Output Heart Failure

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-A-R-D-I-A-C

Chronic severe anemia, AV fistulas, Runaway metabolism (Thyrotoxicosis), Dermatological lesions (Psoriasis), Infection (Sepsis), Alcoholism (Wet beriberi), Carcinoid syndrome.

30. Pulmonary Sequestration Types

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-N-O-W

Systemic arterial supply, No airway connection, Outside normal pleura (Extralobar), Within normal pleura (Intralobar).

Musculoskeletal (MSK)

31. General Bone Lesion Differential Framework

🧠 The Mnemonic: V-I-N-D-I-C-A-T-E

Vascular (Aneurysmal cyst), Infectious (Osteomyelitis), Neoplastic, Degenerative, Intoxication / Drugs, Congenital (Osteopetrosis), Autoimmune, Trauma, Endocrine (Hyperparathyroidism).

32. Epiphyseal Bone Lesions

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-I-L-E-S

Metastasis / Myeloma (rare), Infection (Brodie abscess), Langerhans cell histiocytosis, Enchondroma / Eosinophilic granuloma, Schwannoma / Subchondral cyst / Giant Cell Tumor.

33. Vertebral Body Hemangioma Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-O-S-T

Polka-dot appearance on axial CT, Old age predisposition, Spastic/Striated corduroy appearance on sagittal, T1 and T2 hyperintensity on MRI.

34. Lucent Lesions of the Patella

🧠 The Mnemonic: G-O-A-L

Giant cell tumor, Osteoblastoma, Abscess (Brodie's), Lesion of Brown tumor (Hyperparathyroidism).

35. Hand Changes in Hyperparathyroidism

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-H-O-P

Calcification (Chondrocalcinosis), Hawk-beak (Acro-osteolysis), Osteopenia, Periosteal bone resorption (radial aspect of middle phalanges).

36. Causes of AVN / Avascular Necrosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: F-A-M-I-N-E

Fracture / Trauma, Alcohol abuse, Myeloproliferative disorders, Idiopathic (Legg-Calvé-Perthes) / Infection, Nitrogen bubbles (Caisson disease), Exogenous steroids.

37. Causes of Acro-osteolysis

🧠 The Mnemonic: T-I-P-S

Trauma (Thermal burns / Frostbite), Injury/Infection (Leprosy), Psoriatic arthritis / Hyperparathyroidism, Scleroderma.

38. Rotator Cuff Muscles Insertions

🧠 The Mnemonic: R-O-T-A

S-R-atus (Supraspinatus - superior insertion), Outer posterior insertion (Infraspinatus), Teres minor (Lower posterior insertion), SubscapulAris (Anterior insertion).

39. Carpal Bone Dislocations

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-I-E

Perilunate dislocation, Intermediate stage, Lunate dislocation ("Spilled teacup" sign).

40. Osteopoikilosis Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-O-A-T

Benign condition, Osseous spots (Multiple small radiopaque foci), Asymptomatic incidental finding, Tends to cluster around joints symmetry.

Neuroradiology

41. Suprasellar / Sellar Masses

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-A-M-P-L-E

Sellar aneurysm, Adenoma (Pituitary), Meningioma / Metastasis, Pouch origin (Craniopharyngioma), LH / Glioma (Hypothalamic/Chiasmatic), Epidermoid cyst / Dermoid cyst.

42. Cerebral Dural Arteriovenous Fistula Types

🧠 The Mnemonic: T-O-A-S-T

Type I: Benign anterograde sinus drainage.
Type II: Retrograde without cortical reflux.
Type III: Cortical venous reflux only (High hemorrhage risk).
Type IV: Cortical venous reflux with ectasia.
Type V: Spinal perimedullary drainage.

43. Cerebral Microbleeds on SWI/Gradient Echo MRI

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-L-U-E-S

Brain trauma (diffuse axonal injury), Leukoencephalopathy (CADASIL), Underlying amyloid angiopathy (lobar), Essential hypertension (basal ganglia/pons), Septic emboli.

44. Intra-axial Brain Tumors that Cross the Midline

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-P-A-C-E-S

Suspected Glioblastoma (Butterfly glioma), Primary CNS Lymphoma, Astrocytoma (Anaplastic), Corpus callosum lipoma, Ependymoma, Stroke (non-neoplastic mimic).

45. Cerebral Atrophy Patterns

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-O-P-D

Central atrophy (Ventriculomegaly), Occipital / Parietal sparing (Alzheimer's), Pick's disease (Frontotemporal focal), Diffuse cortical atrophy (Senile involution).

46. Structures passing through the Internal Acoustic Meatus

🧠 The Mnemonic: 7 S's

7 (Cranial Nerve VII), Superior vestibular nerve, Sub-vestibular nerve, Singular nerve, Schwann cells, Solitary internal auditory artery, Secretory nervus intermedius.

47. Features of Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus

🧠 The Mnemonic: G-A-S-P

Gait apraxia ("Magnetic gait"), Atrophy lacking relative to ventricular dilation, Smartness reduced (Dementia), Peeing frequently (Urinary incontinence).

48. Arvenous Malformations on Angiography

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-O-L-D

Compact nidus, Overly dilated draining veins, Late-phase arterial blushing absence, Early-phase Drainage (Pathognomonic feature).

49. Leptomeningeal vs Pachymeningeal Enhancement

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-A-G

Brain surface outline (Leptomeningeal: follows sulci and gyri like a Blanket), All dura mater (Pachymeningeal: thick, smooth, doesn't enter sulci), Gyral patterns seen only in Leptomeningeal type.

50. Brain Tumors presenting with Cyst and Mural Nodule

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-A-F-E

Sponge-like cyst (Pilocytic Astrocytoma), Adult age group (Hemangioblastoma), Focal dural tail (Pleomorphic Xanthoastrocytoma), Epilepsy-associated (Ganglioglioma).

Gastrointestinal & Abdominal (GI)

51. Strictures of the Esophagus

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-A-R-I-U-M

Benign peptic stricture, Adenocarcinoma, Radiation stricture, Infectious esophagitis (Candida/HSV), Ulcerative lye ingestion, Motility-induced spasm.

52. Splenic Infarct Causes on CT

🧠 The Mnemonic: L-E-F-T

Leukemia / Lymphoma, Emboli (Infective endocarditis), Fibrosis / Sickle cell crisis, Torsion (Wandering spleen).

53. Causes of Pneumoperitoneum

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-A-P-U-T

Cholecystitis with perforation, Appendicitis with rupture, Peptic ulcer disease perforation, Ulcerative colitis / Megacolon rupture, Trauma / Iatrogenic.

54. Echogenic Liver on Ultrasound

🧠 The Mnemonic: F-A-T-T-Y

Fatty liver infiltration (Steatosis), Alcohol abuse, Total parenteral nutrition (TPN), Toxicity (Amiodarone, Methotrexate), Youth obesity / Type II diabetes.

55. Normal Bowel Calibre Limits

🧠 The Mnemonic: 3-6-9 Rule

3: Small bowel should measure < 3 cm.
6: Large bowel should measure < 6 cm.
9: Cecum should measure < 9 cm.

56. Hypervascular Liver Metastases

🧠 The Mnemonic: L-I-V-E-R

Leiomyosarcoma, Islet cell tumors (Neuroendocrine), Von Hippel-Lindau lesions (RCC), Endocrine (Thyroid carcinoma), Retinal/Cutaneous (Melanoma).

57. Imaging Signs of Crohn's Disease

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-R-O-H-N

Cobblestone appearance, Rose-thorn ulcers, Obstruction / Strictures ("String sign"), Hyperemia of vasa recta ("Comb sign"), Non-continuous disease (Skip lesions).

58. Causes of Gas in the Portal Vein

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-U-F-F

Perforated viscus, Ulcerative/Ischemic bowel necrosis, Focal abdominal abscess, Fecal impaction with megacolon.

59. Intussusception Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: T-A-R-G-E-T

Target sign on axial view, Alternating concentric rings of fluid/fat, Recurrent colicky pain history, Intussusceptum inside intussuscipiens, Ultrasound "Pseudokidney" sign, Telescope mechanism.

60. True vs False Diverticula

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-A-C-K

Serosa intact (True), All layers of the bowel wall present (True), Congenital origin (True - e.g., Meckel's), Kinetic pressure outpouching through muscle defects only (False).

Genitourinary & Pelvic (GU)

61. Causes of Nephrocalcinosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: H-U-M-A-N

Hyperparathyroidism, Urinary tract infection (chronic), Medullary sponge kidney, Acidosis (Distal Renal Tubular Acidosis), Nephrotoxicity (Furosemide use in infants).

62. Retroperitoneal Structures

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-H-I-E-L-D

Suprarenal (adrenal) glands, Heavy vessels (Aorta & IVC), Isthmus/Duodenum (2nd, 3rd, 4th parts), Esophagus (abdominal portion), L-Kidneys and ureters, Descending/Ascending Colon & Pancreas (except tail).

63. Causes of Asymmetric Kidney Size

🧠 The Mnemonic: T-W-I-N-S

Thrombosis (Renal vein thrombosis), Wilms tumor / Malignancy, Ischemia (Renal artery stenosis), Nephritis (Chronic pyelonephritis scarring), Stones (Obstructive hydronephrosis).

64. Cystic Renal Masses - Bosniak Classification Guidance

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-E-S-H

Minimal septations / Thin calcification (Bosniak II), Equivocal / Thick irregular nodular septations (Bosniak III), Simple benign water-density cyst (Bosniak I), High attenuation / Soft tissue components enhancing (Bosniak IV).

65. Uterine Fibroid Degeneration Types

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-P-I-D-E-R

Subserosal/Submucosal/Intramural forms, Painful Pregnancy complication (Red/Carneous), Ischemic Hyaline degeneration (Most common), Dropsical/Cystic fluid-filled spaces, End-stage Fatty degeneration, Rigid Calcific degeneration ("Womb stone").

66. Bladder Wall Thickening Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-L-O-A-C-A

Cystitis (Infectious/Radiation), Low compliance bladder (Neurogenic), Outflow tract obstruction (BPH / Urethral stricture), Adenocarcinoma / TCC of bladder, Chronic schistosomiasis, Amyloidosis.

67. Testicular Masses Ultrasound Differential

🧠 The Mnemonic: T-O-E

Tumor (Seminoma / Non-seminomatous germ cell), Orchitis / Epididymitis (Hypervascular on Doppler), Extratesticular lesion misidentified (Hydrocele/Spermatocele).

68. Ovarian Teratoma/Dermoid Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-O-U-C-H

Rokitansky Protuberance (Dermoid plug), Ultrasound "Tip of the Iceberg" sign, Osseous / Dental elements (Teeth/Bone on X-ray), Chemical peritonitis if ruptured, Hair-fluid levels.

69. Prune Belly Syndrome Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-A-L-E

Muscle deficiency of anterior abdominal wall, Abnormalities of urinary tract (Megaueter/Megaurethra), Cryptorchidism (Undescended testicles), Exclusively in males.

70. Relationship of Ureter to Uterine Artery / Vas Deferens

🧠 The Mnemonic: Water under the bridge

Water = Ureter (carries urine).
Bridge = Uterine artery (females) or Vas Deferens (males).
The ureter runs posterior/inferior to these structures.

Pediatrics, Vascular & Artifacts

71. Duodenal Atresia Findings

🧠 The Mnemonic: D-O-Double G

Duodenal narrowing, Obstruction early in neonatal life, Double Bubble sign on plain film, Genetic link to Down Syndrome (Trisomy 21).

72. Paediatric Abdominal Masses

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-H-O-M-P

Choledochal cyst, Hepatoblastoma, Over-sized Hydronephrosis, Meconium ileus/pseudocyst, Neuroblastoma / Wilms Tumour.

73. Ultrasound Signs of Fetal Death

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-P-I-N-E

Spaulding's sign (Overlapping of fetal skull bones), Pneumatosis (Gas in fetal circulation), Inversion of normal spinal curvature, No fetal heart motion, Edema of scalp skin ("Deuel's halo sign").

74. Causes of Microcardia / Small Heart on Chest Film

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-A-G-I-C

Malnutrition / Cachexia, Addison's disease, Great emphysema (Severe hyperinflation), Improper inspiration (False mimic), Constrictive pericarditis.

75. High Resistance vs Low Resistance Doppler Waveforms

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-L-U-E

Brain, Liver, Kidneys, Uterus (Pregnant) = Low Resistance (Continuous forward flow).
Limbs, Face, External Carotid = High Resistance (Sharp systolic peak).

76. Aortic Arch Variants

🧠 The Mnemonic: R-I-G-H-T

Right-sided aortic arch with aberrant left subclavian, Isolation of left subclavian artery, Great vessel mirror image branching, High incidence of congenital heart disease with mirror image types, Tracheoesophageal compression by vascular rings.

77. Fibromuscular Dysplasia Arterial Appearance

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-M-A-L-L

"String of beads" sign, Medial fibroplasia type (Most common), Aneurysmal dilatation alternating with stenosis, Young to middle-aged female predilection, Locates primarily in Renal and Internal Carotid arteries.

78. Aortic Dissection Stanford Classification

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-A-D

Stanford type A = Involves Ascending aorta (Surgical emergency).
Stanford type B = Descending aorta only (Managed Medically).
DeBakey system matches Type 1 & 2 to Stanford A, Type 3 to Stanford B.

79. Subclavian Steal Syndrome Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-T-E-A-L

Subclavian artery stenosis, Total or partial reversal of flow in vertebral artery, Exercising ipsilateral arm worsens symptoms, Arm blood pressure discrepancy (>20 mmHg lower), Left side affected far more commonly.

80. Reporting Checkpoints for Critical Findings

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-I-S-S

Mistake or discrepancy found on prior shift films, Identification mismatch checked, Systemic life-threatening critical results (e.g., tension pneumothorax), Sign-off communication directly recorded.

81. Features of Malignant Microcalcifications on Mammography

🧠 The Mnemonic: H-I-G-H

Heterogeneous / Pleomorphic shape, Intermittent or branching/linear distribution, Grouped / Clustered (>5 calcifications in 1 cm³), High density relative to size.

82. Popliteal Artery Entrapment Anatomy

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-A-T

Course deviation of the popliteal artery, Anomalous attachment of the medial head of gastrocnemius muscle, Trauma / Functional occlusion during plantarflexion.

83. Carotid Artery Dissection Signs on MRI

🧠 The Mnemonic: F-I-S-H

Crescentic wall hematoma (Hyperintense T1 fat-sat), Intimal flap visualization, Stenosis of the true lumen ("String sign"), High-lying tapering occlusion ("Flame sign").

84. Perianal Fistula Parks Classification

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-E-R-I

Inter-sphincteric (Between sphincters), Trans-sphincteric (Crosses external sphincter), Supra-sphincteric (Loops over levator ani), Extra-sphincteric (Outside sphincter complex).

85. Ultrasound Artifacts

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-K-I-N

Shadowing (Clean behind bone, dirty behind gas), Kinetic movement anomalies (Acoustic enhancement), Image duplication (Mirror image artifact), Null echoes / Reverberation (Comet-tail artifact).
