Chapter 11: Nervous System

The Nervous System controls and coordinates all body activities through electrical and chemical signals.

1. Divisions of the Nervous System

The Mnemonic: CPA

Central Nervous System (CNS), Peripheral Nervous System (PNS), Autonomic Nervous System (ANS)

2. Components of CNS

The Mnemonic: BS

Brain, Spinal cord

3. Components of PNS

The Mnemonic: CN + SN

Cranial nerves, Spinal nerves

4. Divisions of ANS

The Mnemonic: PS

Parasympathetic, Sympathetic

5. Functions of the Nervous System

The Mnemonic: SIR

Sensory input, Integration, Response (Motor output)

6. Parts of a Neuron

The Mnemonic: DAB

Dendrite, Axon, Cell Body

7. Types of Neurons

The Mnemonic: SAM

Sensory, Association (Interneuron), Motor

8. Neuroglial Cells (CNS)

The Mnemonic: MOAM

Microglia, Oligodendrocytes, Astrocytes, M (Ependymal cells)

9. Neuroglial Cells (PNS)

The Mnemonic: SS

Schwann cells, Satellite cells

10. Meninges

The Mnemonic: DAP

Dura mater, Arachnoid mater, Pia mater

11. Brain Divisions

The Mnemonic: CDBC

Cerebrum, Diencephalon, Brainstem, Cerebellum

12. Brainstem Parts

The Mnemonic: MPM

Midbrain, Pons, Medulla oblongata

13. Cerebrum Lobes

The Mnemonic: FPOT

Frontal, Parietal, Occipital, Temporal

14. Cranial Nerves (I–XII)

The Mnemonic: Oh Oh Oh To Touch And Feel Very Good Velvet, AH!

Olfactory, Optic, Oculomotor, Trochlear, Trigeminal, Abducens, Facial, Vestibulocochlear, Glossopharyngeal, Vagus, Accessory, Hypoglossal.

15. Cranial Nerves – Sensory/Motor/Both

The Mnemonic: Some Say Marry Money, But My Brother Says Big Brains Matter More

Sensory, Motor, Both. Order (I to XII): S, S, M, M, B, M, B, S, B, B, M, M.

16. Ventricles of the Brain

The Mnemonic: 1-2-3-4

Right lateral ventricle (1), Left lateral ventricle (2), Third ventricle (3), Fourth ventricle (4).

17. CSF Flow

The Mnemonic: LAMAT

Lateral ventricles, Aqueduct (after third ventricle), Median/Lateral apertures, Arachnoid villi, Transverse (venous) sinus

18. Spinal Cord Regions

The Mnemonic: CTLSC

Cervical, Thoracic, Lumbar, Sacral, Coccygeal

19. Number of Spinal Nerves

The Mnemonic: 8-12-5-5-1

8 Cervical, 12 Thoracic, 5 Lumbar, 5 Sacral, 1 Coccygeal

20. Ascending Tracts

The Mnemonic: SPIN

Spinothalamic, Posterior column, Inocerebellar (Spinocerebellar), N (Remember major sensory pathways)

21. Descending Tracts

The Mnemonic: CRVT

Corticospinal, Rubrospinal, Vestibulospinal, Tectospinal

22. Reflex Arc

The Mnemonic: SRINE

Sensory receptor, Receptor neuron (Sensory neuron), Interneuron, Neuro-motor (Motor neuron), Effector

23. Sympathetic Nervous System

The Mnemonic: Fight or Flight

Effects: Increased heart rate, Dilated pupils, Bronchodilation, Reduced digestion.

24. Parasympathetic Nervous System

The Mnemonic: Rest and Digest

Effects: Slower heart rate, Increased digestion, Constricted pupils.

25. Neurotransmitters

The Mnemonic: A GAD

Acetylcholine, Glutamate, A (GABA), Dopamine

26. Basal Ganglia

The Mnemonic: CPSP

Caudate nucleus, Putamen, Subthalamic nucleus, Pallidus (Globus Pallidus)

27. Cerebellum Functions

The Mnemonic: BAM

Balance, Accuracy of movement, Muscle coordination

28. Limbic System

The Mnemonic: HEA

Hippocampus, Emotional processing (Amygdala), Amygdala

29. Major Brain Blood Supply

The Mnemonic: ICA + VBA

Internal Carotid Artery, Vertebrobasilar Artery

30. Circle of Willis

The Mnemonic: ICAMPC

Internal carotid, C (Anterior cerebral), Anterior communicating, Middle cerebral, Posterior cerebral, C (Posterior communicating)

31. Common Neurological Disorders

The Mnemonic: SPAM

Stroke, Parkinson disease, Alzheimer's disease, Multiple sclerosis
