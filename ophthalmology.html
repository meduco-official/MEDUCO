Subject: Ophthalmology

Master ocular anatomy, glaucoma pathophsysiology, retinal disorders, and neuro-ophthalmology with crystal-clear memory hooks.

1. Corneal Stromal Dystrophies & Staining

🧠 The Mnemonic: Marilyn Monroe Always Gets Her Men in L.A. County

Macular = Mucopolysaccharide (Alcian blue) | Granular = Hyaline (Masson trichrome) | Lattice = Amyloid (Congo red)

2. Layers of the Cornea (Anterior to Posterior)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Every Body Sleeps During Eye class

Epithelium, Bowman's layer, Stroma, Descemet's membrane, Endothelium.

3. Extraocular Muscle Innervation

🧠 The Mnemonic: Formula LR₆SO₄AO₃

Lateral Rectus = CN 6 (Abducens) | Superior Oblique = CN 4 (Trochlear) | All Others = CN 3 (Oculomotor).

4. Extraocular Muscle Actions (Obliques vs. Recti)

🧠 The Mnemonic: RAD and SIN

Recti Adduct (RAD) — All rectus muscles adduct, except lateral rectus.
Superiors Intort (SIN) — Both superior muscles cause intorsion.

5. Angle Structures on Gonioscopy (Posterior to Anterior)

🧠 The Mnemonic: I Can See The Schwalbe's Line

Iris root, Ciliary body band, Scleral spur, Trabecular meshwork, Schwalbe's Line.

6. Visual Field Defects (Quadrantanopias)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Pie in the Sky vs. Pie on the Floor

Pie in the Sky: Temporal lobe lesion (Superior quadrantanopia).
Pie on the Floor: Parietal lobe lesion (Inferior quadrantanopia).

7. Direct Ophthalmoscopy Image Properties

🧠 The Mnemonic: DEV 15

Direct ophthalmoscopy yields an Erect, Virtual image that is magnified 15 times.

8. Causes of Optic Atrophy

🧠 The Mnemonic: ICING

Ischemia, Compression, Intracranial pressure (chronic papilledema), Neuritis, Glaucoma.

9. Thyroid Eye Disease Muscle Involvement (Order of Frequency)

🧠 The Mnemonic: I M Slow

Inferior rectus (Most common), Medial rectus, Superior rectus, Lateral rectus (Least common).

10. Etiology of Angioid Streaks

🧠 The Mnemonic: PEPSI HAM

Pseudoxanthoma elasticum, Ehlers-Danlos, Paget's disease, Sickle cell, Idiopathic, Homocystinuria/Hypopituitarism, Aeroneurosis, Marfan.

11. Layers of the Retina (Outer to Inner)

🧠 The Mnemonic: In New Generation I Only Imitate Our Extraordinary Beautiful Eyes

Internal limiting membrane, Nerve fiber, Ganglion cell, Inner plexiform, Inner nuclear, Outer plexiform, Outer nuclear, External limiting, Bacillary (Photoreceptors), RPE.

12. Pathological Signs of Dry AMD

🧠 The Mnemonic: DRY DRUSEN

Dry AMD is primarily characterized by Drusen (yellow cellular debris deposits under retina).

13. High-Risk Features of PDR

🧠 The Mnemonic: NVD / NVE with VH

NVD > 1/4 to 1/3 disc area or any NVD with Vitreous Hemorrhage | NVE > 1/2 disc area with VH.

14. Cherry Red Spot Differential Diagnosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: Cherry Trees May Never Grow Stall In Mud

CRAO, Tay-Sachs, Metachromatic leukodystrophy, Niemann-Pick, Gaucher's, Sialidosis, Interferon toxicity, Mucopolysaccharidosis.

15. Risk Factors for Choroidal Melanoma Growth

🧠 The Mnemonic: To Find Small Ocular Melanoma Using Heartful Review

Thickness > 2mm, Fluid, Symptoms, Orange pigment, Margin within 3mm, Ultrasound hollowness, Halo absence, Retrapapillary location.

16. Primary Angle-Closure Glaucoma Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: Old Female Hyperopes Fear Close Angles

Old age, Female gender, Hyperopia, Family history, Chinese/Asian ethnicity.

17. Secondary Open-Angle Glaucoma Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: Pigments Exfoliate Slowly Under Trauma

Pigmentary, Exfoliation syndrome, Steroid-induced, Uveitic, Traumatic (Angle recession).

18. Beta-Blocker Contraindications (Glaucoma Drops)

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDE

Asthma, Bradycardia, COPD/Heart failure, Depression, Electrical heart block (2nd/3rd degree).

19. Prostaglandin Analog Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: LASH

Lash growth, Activation of HSV / Iris pigmentation darkening, Sunken eyes, Hyperemia.

20. Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitor (CAI) Contraindications

🧠 The Mnemonic: SULFA

Sulfa allergy (CAIs are sulfonamide derivatives).

21. Horner’s Syndrome Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: PAM is Horny

Ptosis (mild), Anhidrosis, Miosis.

22. Argyll Robertson Pupil Characteristics

🧠 The Mnemonic: ARP / PRA

Accommodation Reflex Present, Pupillary Reflex Absent (Light reflex absent).

23. Marcus Gunn Pupil (RAPD) Etiology

🧠 The Mnemonic: RAPD

Retinal detachment, Artery/Vein occlusion, Papilledema, Demyelinating disease (Optic Neuritis).

24. Optic Disc Edema Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: VINDICATE

Vascular, Infectious, Neoplastic, Demyelinating, Idiopathic (IIH), Congenital, Autoimmune, Toxic/Nutritional, Endocrine.

25. Pseudotumor Cerebri Profile

🧠 The Mnemonic: 5 F's

Female, Fat (Obese), Fertile, Forty, First-line drugs (Tetracyclines, Retinoids).

26. Internuclear Ophthalmoplegia (INO) Lesion Location

🧠 The Mnemonic: MLF

Medial Longitudinal Fasciculus lesion. (Ipsilateral eye cannot adduct).

27. Acanthamoeba Keratitis Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: Swimming Taps Contact Lenses

Swimming in contaminated water, Tap water rinse, Contact Lens wear.

28. Keratoconus Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: Munson's Vogt Fleischer

Munson's sign, Vogt's striae, Fleischer ring.

29. Interstitial Keratitis Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: The Little Herpes Monster

Tuberculosis, Leprosy, Herpes Simplex/Zoster, Malaria/Cogan syndrome.

30. Band Keratopathy Etiologies

🧠 The Mnemonic: CALCIUM

Chronic uveitis, Age-related, Long-standing glaucoma, Cornea trauma, Idiopathic, Uremia, Milk-alkali syndrome.

31. Congenital Cataract TORCH Infections

🧠 The Mnemonic: TORCH

Toxoplasmosis, Other (Syphilis, Varicella), Rubella (pearley cataract), CMV, HSV.

32. Leucocoria Differential

🧠 The Mnemonic: Return Carefully Past The Other Doctors

Retinoblastoma, Cataract, PHPV, Toxocariasis, Coates' Disease, Detachment of retina (ROP).

33. Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: OXY LOW

Oxygen supplementation (high/unregulated), Low birth weight (< 1500g), Low gestational age (< 30 wks).

34. Duane Retraction Syndrome Types

🧠 The Mnemonic: Duane 1, 2, 3 = Ab, Ad, Both

Type 1: Abduction limit | Type 2: Adduction limit | Type 3: Both.

35. Overacting Superior Oblique (A-Pattern Strabismus)

🧠 The Mnemonic: As Superior Oblique Diverges

A-pattern esotropia/exotropia caused by overacting SO because it Diverges eyes on down-gaze.

36. Orbital Blowout Fracture Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: I Really Entrap Nerves

Infraorbital nerve anesthesia, Restricted up-gaze, Enophthalmos, Nasal bleeding.

37. Chlamydial Conjunctivitis Serotypes

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-C = African Blindness; D-K = Dirty Kids

A-C: Trachoma | D-K: Inclusion Conjunctivitis.

38. Scleritis vs. Episcleritis Phenylephrine Test

🧠 The Mnemonic: Episcleritis Evaporates

Episcleritis vessels blanch with Phenylephrine 2.5%. Scleritis vessels do not.

39. Uveitis HLA-B25/B27 Associated Conditions

🧠 The Mnemonic: PAIR

Psoriatic arthritis, Ankylosing spondylitis, IBD, Reiter’s syndrome.

40. Pre-septal vs. Orbital Cellulitis Differentiation

🧠 The Mnemonic: PROs View Pain

Orbital cellulitis presents with: Proptosis, Restricted motility, Ophthalmoplegia, Vision loss, Pain with movement.

41. Pilocarpine Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: My Brow Hurts Real Bad

Miosis, Brow ache, Hyperemia, Retinal detachment, Blood-aqueous breakdown.

42. Steroid-Induced Side Effects in the Eye

🧠 The Mnemonic: Please Give Steroid Insight

Posterior subcapsular cataract, Glaucoma, Secondary infection activation, Interrupted wound healing.

43. Systemic Drugs Causing Vortex Keratopathy

🧠 The Mnemonic: CHAI-T

Chloroquine/Hydroxychloroquine, Hydroxyurea, Amiodarone, Indomethacin, Tamoxifen.

44. Drugs Causing Toxic Optic Neuropathy

🧠 The Mnemonic: Every Medication Is Lethal

Ethambutol, Methanol, Isoniazid, Linezolid.

45. Mydriatics vs. Miotics Mechanism

🧠 The Mnemonic: Dilate = Dilator; Sphincter = Shrink

Dilator contraction = Mydriasis | Sphincter contraction = Miosis.

46. Fuchs Heterochromic Iridocyclitis (FHI) Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: Keratic Precipitates, Cataract, Heterochromia

Fine KPs, PSC cataract, Lighter affected eye.

47. Vogt-Koyanagi-Harada (VKH) Syndrome Stages

🧠 The Mnemonic: Please Always Cure Chronic

Prodromal, Acute uveitic, Convalescent, Chronic recurrent.

48. Behçet’s Disease Ocular Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: Hypopyon, Retinal Vasculitis, Ulcers

Hypopyon, Retinal vasculitis ("blood and thunder"), Aphthous/genital ulcers.

49. Sarcoidosis Ocular Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: Mutton Fat Candlewax

Mutton-Fat KPs, Candlewax drippings.

50. White Dot Syndromes affecting Young Myopic Females

🧠 The Mnemonic: PIC ME

Punctate Inner Choroidopathy (PIC), Multifocal Choroiditis and Panuveitis (MCP).

51. Reis-Bücklers vs. Thiel-Behnke Dystrophy Sheet Types

🧠 The Mnemonic: Reis = Rod; Thiel = Twisted

Reis-Bücklers = Rod-like | Thiel-Behnke = Twisted/honeycomb.

52. Wilson’s Disease Corneal Ring Location

🧠 The Mnemonic: Kayser-Fleischer = Descemet's

Occurs specifically in Descemet’s membrane.

53. Mooren’s Ulcer vs. Terrien’s Marginal Degeneration

🧠 The Mnemonic: Mooren's = Melting; Terrien's = Thins

Mooren's: Painful melting | Terrien's: Asymptomatic lipid thinning with intact epithelium.

54. Layers of the Tear Film

🧠 The Mnemonic: Many Aquatic Monsters

Mucin (inner), Aqueous (middle), Meibomian/Lipid (outer).

55. Dry Eye Disease Classification

🧠 The Mnemonic: Aquated Evaporative

Aqueous deficient vs. Evaporative (MGD).

56. Mechanisms of Angle Closure

🧠 The Mnemonic: Push from Behind, Pull from Ahead

Push = Pupillary block | Pull = Synechiae (PAS).

57. Target IOP Reduction Rule of Thumb

🧠 The Mnemonic: 20-30-40 Rule

20% (early), 30% (moderate), 40% (advanced).

58. Risk Factors for Normal Tension Glaucoma (NTG)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Migraine, Myopia, Raynaud's

Migraines, High Myopia, Raynaud's phenomenon.

59. ICE Syndrome Variants

🧠 The Mnemonic: Charlie Meets Cogs

Chandler, Cogan-Reese (Medial Iris nevus), Essential Iris atrophy.

60. Posner-Schlossman Syndrome Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: One Unilateral Uveitic Outburst

Unilateral high IOP (>40-50 mmHg) with minimal uveitis; recurrent and self-limiting.

61. Cranial Nerve 3 Palsy: Pupil Involving vs. Sparing

🧠 The Mnemonic: Pupil = Pressure; Sparing = Sugar

Pupil involving = Aneurysm pressure | Sparing = Microvascular ischemia (Diabetes).

62. Cranial Nerve 4 Palsy Compensatory Head Tilt

🧠 The Mnemonic: Tilt to the Topposite

Tilt head to Opposite side of lesion to minimize diplopia.

63. Foster Kennedy Syndrome Clinical Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: Optic Atrophy Papilledema Anosmia

Optic atrophy ipsilateral, Papilledema contralateral, Anosmia (frontal tumor).

64. Optic Neuritis Clinical Trial (ONTT) Takeaway

🧠 The Mnemonic: Iravenous Improves; Oral Outbursts

IV steroids improve recovery speed. Oral steroids alone increase recurrence rates.

65. Parinaud’s Dorsal Midbrain Syndrome Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: Light Convergence Retraction

Light-near dissociation, Convergence-retraction nystagmus, Up-gaze restriction.

66. Central Retinal Vein Occlusion (CRVO) Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: 90 Day Glaucoma

Neovascular glaucoma typically peaks around 90 days post-ischemic CRVO.

67. Retinitis Pigmentosa ERG Finding

🧠 The Mnemonic: RP = Reduced / Ring

Marked reduction/extinction of scotopic responses; clinical ring scotoma.

68. Fundus Fluorescein Angiography (FFA) Phases

🧠 The Mnemonic: Charlie Runs Around Various Loopings

Choroidal, Retinal Arterial, Arteriovenous, Venous, Late phase.

69. Coats' Disease Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: Coats = Child, Capillary exudates

Young boys, capillary telangiectasias with massive lipid exudation, unilateral yellow subretinal fluid.

70. Purtscher Retinopathy Presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: Purtscher = Pang (Trauma) + Pancreas

Caused by compressive trauma or acute pancreatitis.

71. Worth 4-Dot Test Outcomes

🧠 The Mnemonic: 2 Red = Left; 3 Green = Right; 5 = Diplopia

Red lens over right eye: 2 red = left suppression | 3 green = right suppression | 5 dots = diplopia.

72. Retinoscopy Motion Rules

🧠 The Mnemonic: Same Hyperope; Opposite Myope

With motion = Plus lenses (Hyperopia) | Against motion = Minus lenses (Myopia).

73. Lens Induced Glaucomas

🧠 The Mnemonic: PhacoLytic = Leaking; PhacoMorphic = Massive

Phacolytic = leaking proteins | Phacomorphic = massive lens size causing pupillary block.

74. Spherical Aberration Management

🧠 The Mnemonic: Cornea Periphery Flattening

The cornea counteracts spherical aberration by flattening at its periphery.

75. Prism Diopter Deviation

🧠 The Mnemonic: 1 Prism = 0.5 Degrees

1 prism diopter deviates light by approximately 0.5 degrees of arc.

76. Basal Cell Carcinoma Eyelid Sites

🧠 The Mnemonic: Lower Medial Upper Lateral

Lower lid > Medial canthus > Upper lid > Lateral canthus.

77. Optic Nerve Meningioma Radiographic Sign

🧠 The Mnemonic: Tram-Track

Tram-Track calcification or enhancement on CT/MRI.

78. Optic Nerve Glioma Association

🧠 The Mnemonic: Glioma = NF-1

Highly associated with Neurofibromatosis Type 1.

79. Retinoblastoma Histology Rosettes

🧠 The Mnemonic: Flexner = Flexible lumen; Homer = Hollow Center

Flexner-Wintersteiner = true lumen | Homer-Wright = neuropil center.

80. B-Scan Ultrasound Diagnostic Principle

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-scan = Brightness

Translates echo spikes into structural Brightness dots for a 2D cross-sectional view.
