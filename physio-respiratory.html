Chapter 8: Respiratory System

The Respiratory System is responsible for breathing, gas exchange, regulation of blood pH, voice production, and smell.

1. Functions of the Respiratory System

The Mnemonic: BGVAS

Breathing, Gas exchange, Voice production, Acid-base balance, Smell (Olfaction)

2. Parts of the Respiratory System

The Mnemonic: PNLTBLA

P (Pharynx/Nose), Nasal cavity, Larynx, Trachea, Bronchi, Lungs, Alveoli

3. Upper Respiratory Tract

The Mnemonic: NNLP

Nose, Nasal cavity, Larynx, Pharynx

4. Lower Respiratory Tract

The Mnemonic: TBLA

Trachea, Bronchi, Lungs, Alveoli

5. Parts of the Pharynx

The Mnemonic: Never Oral Lies

Nasopharynx, Oropharynx, Laryngopharynx

6. Cartilages of the Larynx

The Mnemonic: TECCCA

Thyroid, Epiglottis, Cricoid, Corniculate, Cuneiform, Arytenoid

7. Trachea

The Mnemonic: C-Shaped Cartilage

16–20 C-shaped hyaline cartilage rings

8. Bronchial Tree

The Mnemonic: TBBSA

Trachea, Bronchi, Bronchioles, Secondary bronchioles, Alveoli

9. Right vs Left Main Bronchus

The Mnemonic: Right is Short, Straight and Wide

Right bronchus is Shorter, Wider, and More vertical. (Aspiration is more common on the right.)

10. Lobes of the Lungs

The Mnemonic: Right Has 3, Left Has 2

Right lung = 3 lobes | Left lung = 2 lobes

11. Lobes of Right Lung

The Mnemonic: SUM

Superior, U (Middle), M (Inferior) / Superior, Middle, Inferior

12. Lobes of Left Lung

The Mnemonic: SI

Superior, Inferior

13. Fissures of the Lungs

The Mnemonic: Right = HOT, Left = O

Right lung → Horizontal + Oblique fissures | Left lung → Oblique fissure only

14. Root of Lung

The Mnemonic: BAVLN

Bronchus, Pulmonary Artery, Pulmonary Veins, Lymphatics, Nerves

15. Pleura

The Mnemonic: PV

Parietal pleura, Visceral pleura

16. Parts of Parietal Pleura

The Mnemonic: CCDM

Costal, Cervical, Diaphragmatic, Mediastinal

17. Alveolar Cells

The Mnemonic: 12M

Type 1 pneumocytes, Type 2 pneumocytes, Macrophages

18. Type II Pneumocytes

The Mnemonic: Type II = Surfactant

Produces pulmonary surfactant.

19. Functions of Surfactant

The Mnemonic: SAFE

Surface tension decreases, Alveoli stay open, Facilitates breathing, Easy lung expansion

20. Respiratory Muscles

The Mnemonic: DIE

Diaphragm, Intercostal muscles, External accessory muscles

21. Inspiration

The Mnemonic: In = Diaphragm Down

Diaphragm contracts and moves downward.

22. Expiration

The Mnemonic: Ex = Relax

Normally a passive process of relaxation.

23. Gas Exchange

The Mnemonic: O₂ In, CO₂ Out

Simple diffusion across the alveolar-capillary membrane.

24. Respiratory Membrane

The Mnemonic: AEC

Alveolar epithelium, Endothelium, Capillary

25. Oxygen Transport

The Mnemonic: 98 + 2

98% bound to hemoglobin, 2% dissolved in plasma

26. Carbon Dioxide Transport

The Mnemonic: 70-23-7

70% as bicarbonate, 23% as carbaminohemoglobin, 7% dissolved in plasma

27. Respiratory Centers

The Mnemonic: PM

Pons, Medulla

28. Cranial Nerve for Smell

The Mnemonic: I Smell

CN I = Olfactory nerve.

29. Clinical Conditions

The Mnemonic: AACT

Asthma, Atelectasis, COPD, Tuberculosis

30. Lung Volumes

The Mnemonic: TV IRV ERV RV

Tidal Volume, Inspiratory Reserve Volume, Expiratory Reserve Volume, Residual Volume
