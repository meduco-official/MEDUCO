Subject: Pharmacology

Turn the alphabet soup of drugs into unforgettable patterns. Master mechanisms, adverse effects, and drug classifications instantly.

🔥 Top 10 High-Yield Basics

1. Bacteriostatic Drugs

🧠 The Mnemonic: ECSTaTiC

Erythromycin | Chloramphenicol | Sulfonamides | Tetracyclines | Trimethoprim | Clindamycin
💡 These inhibit bacterial growth but don’t kill directly.

2. Beta-Blocker Classification (Cardio-Selective Beta-1)

🧠 The Mnemonic: MAN BABE

Metoprolol | Atenolol | Nebivolol | Bisoprolol | Acebutolol | Betaxolol | Esmolol

3. CYP450 Inducers

🧠 The Mnemonic: CRAP GPS

Carbamazepine | Rifampicin | Alcohol (chronic) | Phenytoin | Griseofulvin | Phenobarbital | Sulfonylureas
💡 These increase drug metabolism — leading to decreased efficacy of co-administered drugs.

4. CYP450 Inhibitors

🧠 The Mnemonic: SICKFACES.COM

Sodium valproate | Isoniazid | Cimetidine | Ketoconazole | Fluconazole | Amiodarone | Chloramphenicol | Erythromycin | Sulfonamides | .COM (Ciprofloxacin, Omeprazole, Metronidazole)
💡 They slow down drug metabolism — increasing plasma levels and toxicity risk.

5. ACE Inhibitors

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAP your ACE

Captopril | Analapril (Enalapril) | Perindopril
💡 ACE inhibitors end with “-pril”; used in hypertension and heart failure.

6. Parkinson’s Disease Drugs

🧠 The Mnemonic: BALSA

Bromocriptine | Amantadine | Levodopa + Carbidopa | Selegiline | Antimuscarinics (Benztropine)
💡 These balance dopamine-acetylcholine levels in Parkinson’s.

7. Anticholinergic Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: Can’t See, Can’t Pee, Can’t Spit, Can’t 💩

Blurred vision, urinary retention, dry mouth, constipation.

8. Anti-Tubercular Drugs (First-Line)

🧠 The Mnemonic: RIPE

Rifampicin | Isoniazid | Pyrazinamide | Ethambutol
💡 The four first-line drugs against TB.

9. Sulfonamide Mechanism

🧠 The Mnemonic: Folate Blockers Stop Bacterial Growth

💡 They inhibit folic acid synthesis — crucial for DNA replication.

10. Loop Diuretics

🧠 The Mnemonic: Loop Lose Na, K, Ca, Cl

💡 Mnemonic to remember their ion loss pattern — Furosemide, Bumetanide, Torsemide.

⚙️ General Pharmacology

11. Drugs with High First-Pass Metabolism

🧠 The Mnemonic: Nitrates Have Large Pre Systemic Metabolism

Nitrates, Hydrocortisone, Lignocaine, Propranolol, Salbutamol, Morphine.

12. Protein Binding Significance

🧠 The Mnemonic: DDDD

Duration of action, Distribution, Displacement, Dialysis.

13. Dialysis Ineffective in Toxicity

🧠 The Mnemonic: AVOID Dialysis

Amphetamines, Verapamil, Organophosphates/opioids, Imipramine, Digitalis, Diazepam.

14. Prodrugs

🧠 The Mnemonic: All Prefer Doing MD In Clinical Subjects

ACE inhibitors, Prednisone/Proguanil/PPIs, Dipivefrine, Mercaptopurine/Methyldopa/Minoxidil, L-Dopa, Irinotecan/INH, Cyclophosphamide/Carbimazole/Clopidogrel, Sulfasalazine.

15. Enzyme Inducers

🧠 The Mnemonic: GPRS Cell Phone

Griseofulvin, Phenytoin, Rifampicin, Smoking/Nicotine, Carbamazepine, Phenobarbitone.

16. Enzyme Inhibitors

🧠 The Mnemonic: Vitamin K Cannot Cause Enzyme Inhibition

Valproate, Ketoconazole, Cimetidine, Ciprofloxacin, Erythromycin, INH.

🫀 Cardiovascular

17. Non-Selective Beta-Blockers Contraindicated In

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCD

Asthma, Block (AV block), CHF, DM.

18. Cardioselective Beta-Blockers

🧠 The Mnemonic: New Beta Blockers Acting Exclusively At Myo Cardium

Nebivolol, Betaxolol, Bisoprolol, Acebutolol, Esmolol, Atenolol, Metoprolol, Celiprolol.

19. Beta-Blockers Safe in Bradycardia

🧠 The Mnemonic: Contain Partial Agonistic Activity

Celiprolol, Oxprenolol, Pindolol/Penbutolol, Alprenolol, Acebutolol.

20. Beta-Blockers with Membrane-Stabilizing Property

🧠 The Mnemonic: Possess Membrane stabilizing or Local Anesthetic Property

Propranolol, Metoprolol, Labetalol, Acebutolol, Pindolol.

21. Lipid-Insoluble Beta-Blockers

🧠 The Mnemonic: Not Soluble ABC

Nadolol, Sotalol, Atenolol/Acebutolol, Betaxolol/Bisoprolol, Celiprolol.

22. ACE Inhibitor Properties & Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAPTOPRIL

Cough, Angioedema, Prodrugs, Taste disturbances, Orthostatic hypotension, Pregnancy contraindicated, Renal artery stenosis contraindicated, Increased K+ (Hyperkalemia), Lower BP.

🩸 Autacoids & CNS

23. Anticholinergic Uses of Antihistamines

🧠 The Mnemonic: Possess Anti Cholinergic Properties

Parkinsonism, Acute muscular dystonia, Common cold, Prophylaxis of motion sickness.

24. Uses of Colchicine

🧠 The Mnemonic: SLUM

Sarcoid arthritis, Liver cirrhosis, Uric acid elevation, Mediterranean fever.

25. Drugs Unsafe in Porphyria

🧠 The Mnemonic: SEVERE PORPHYRiA

Sulfonamides, Erythromycin, Valproate, Ergot alkaloids, Rifampicin, Estrogens, Phenobarbitone, Oral contraceptives, Phenytoin, Hydralazine, Alcohol.

26. Short-Acting Benzodiazepines

🧠 The Mnemonic: LOT

Lorazepam, Oxazepam, Temazepam (and Triazolam).

27. Phenytoin Adverse Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: MILK OATH

Megaloblastic anemia, Inhibition of insulin release, Lymphadenopathy, K (Vitamin K deficiency), Osteomalacia, Ataxia/arrhythmias, Teratogenicity, Hirsutism/gum hypertrophy.

28. Valproate Adverse Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: VALPROATE

Vomiting, Alopecia, Liver damage, PCOD/pancreatitis, Rash, Obesity, Agranulocytosis, Tremors, Epigastric pain.

29. Antipsychotic Uses

🧠 The Mnemonic: ANTI PSY-CHO-TIC

Anti-emetic, Psychosis, Chorea, Tic disorders.

30. Antidepressants Uses

🧠 The Mnemonic: DEPRESSION

Depression, Enuresis, Phobia, Recurrent panic attacks, Eating disorder, Smoking cessation, Stress disorder, Impulse disorder, OCD, Neuropathic pain.

🦠 Antibiotics & Antimicrobials

31. Safe in Renal Dysfunction

🧠 The Mnemonic: DANCER

Doxycycline, Ampicillin, Nafcillin, Cefoperazone/Ceftriaxone, Erythromycin, Rifampicin.

32. Penicillins (Acid-Resistant vs. Anti-Pseudomonal vs. Pen G)

🧠 The Mnemonic: VODKA | CT-MAP | LASTMAn

Acid-Resistant (VODKA): Penicillin V, Oxacillin, Dicloxacillin, Cloxacillin (Kloxacillin), Amoxicillin/Ampicillin.
Anti-Pseudomonal (CT-MAP): Carbenicillin, Ticarcillin, Mezlocillin, Azlocillin, Piperacillin.
Penicillin G uses (LASTMAn): Leptospira, Actinomyces, Streptococcus/Staphylococcus, Treponema/Tetanus, Meningococcus, Anthrax.

33. Tetracyclines (Uses & Adverse Effects)

🧠 The Mnemonic: RBC In PLASMA | KAPIL DEV

Uses (RBC In PLASMA): Rickettsia, Brucellosis, Cholera/Chlamydia, Inguinale, Plague/Peptic ulcer, LGV, Atypical pneumonia, SIADH, Malaria, Amoebiasis.
Adverse Effects (KAPIL DEV): Kidney failure, Antianabolic effect, Photosensitivity, Insipidus, Liver toxicity, Dentition/bone defects, Expired drugs → Fanconi syndrome, Vestibular dysfunction.

34. Macrolides (Uses & Adverse Effects)

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLAW | CHAT | MACRO

General Uses (CLAW): Chancroid/Corynebacterium, Legionella, Atypical pneumonia, Whooping cough.
Azithro/Clarithro uses (CHAT): Chlamydia, H. influenzae, Avium complex, Toxoplasma.
Adverse Effects (MACRO): Motilin receptor agonism, Allergy, Cholestasis, Reversible Ototoxicity.

35. Clindamycin, Co-trimoxazole, Sulfonamides

🧠 The Mnemonic: PACT | PNB | ABC RASH

Clindamycin uses (PACT): Pneumocystis/Plasmodium, Anaerobes, Cocci, Toxoplasma.
Co-trimoxazole (Punjab National Bank): Pneumocystis, Nocardia, Burkholderia cepacia.
Sulfonamide Adverse Effects (ABC RASH): Aplastic anemia, Bilirubin displacement, Crystalluria, Rash, Acetylation → SLE, Hemolysis.

36. Anaerobe Coverage

🧠 The Mnemonic: Cyto Megalo Virus

Clindamycin/Cefmetazole/Cefotetan/Cefoxitin/Chloramphenicol, Moxifloxacin/Metronidazole, Vancomycin.

37. Antitubercular & Antimalarial Drugs

🧠 The Mnemonic: SACK | Mango ACHAR | REDMI-LPG

Aminoglycoside TB drugs (SACK): Streptomycin, Amikacin, Capreomycin, Kanamycin.
Fast-acting Schizonticides (Mango ACHAR): Mefloquine, Halofantrine, Atovaquone/Amodiaquine, Artemisinins, Chloroquine, Quinine.
Chloroquine Uses (REDMI-LPG): Rheumatoid arthritis, Extraintestinal amoebiasis, Discoid LE, Malaria, Infectious mononucleosis, Lepra reaction, Photogenic reactions, Giardiasis.

38. Metronidazole Uses

🧠 The Mnemonic: GUPTA

Giardiasis/Gardnerella, Ulcer (H. pylori), Pseudomembranous colitis, Trichomoniasis, Amoebiasis/Anaerobes.

💉 Antifungals & Oncology

39. Amphotericin B Adverse Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: AMPHOTERICIN-B

Anemia, Muscle spasms, Phlebitis, Headache/Hypotension, Ototoxicity, Thrombocytopenia, Emesis, Respiratory stridor, Increased temperature, Chills, Immediate hypersensitivity, Nephrotoxicity, Bronchospasm.

40. Methotrexate Uses

🧠 The Mnemonic: Inhibit CANCER

Immunosuppressant, Crohn’s disease, Abortion, Non-Hodgkin lymphoma, Choriocarcinoma, Ectopic pregnancy, Rheumatoid arthritis.

41. Rituximab Uses

🧠 The Mnemonic: ReLIANCe

Rheumatoid arthritis, Lupus, ITP, Autoimmune hemolytic anemia, Non-Hodgkin lymphoma, CLL.
