Subject: General Medicine

Your ultimate survival guide to Internal Medicine. Master clinical triads, diagnostic workups, and emergency protocols across every major system.

Core Emergencies & Assessment

1. Emergency Primary Survey

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDE

Airway, Breathing, Circulation, Disability, Exposure.

2. Pain History

🧠 The Mnemonic: SOCRATES

Site, Onset, Character, Radiation, Associated symptoms, Timing, Exacerbating/relieving factors, Severity.

3. Alternative Pain Check

🧠 The Mnemonic: PQRST

Provoking factors, Quality, Radiation, Severity, Time.

4. Trauma History

🧠 The Mnemonic: AMPLE

Allergies, Medications, Past history, Last meal, Events leading to injury.

5. Secondary Medical Assessment

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAMPLE

Signs/symptoms, Allergies, Medications, Pertinent history, Last oral intake, Events.

6. Urgent Dialysis Indications

🧠 The Mnemonic: AEIOU

Acidosis, Electrolyte imbalance, Intoxications, Overload (fluid), Uremia.

7. High Anion Gap Metabolic Acidosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: MUDPILES

Methanol, Uremia, Diabetic ketoacidosis, Paraldehyde, Iron/Isoniazid, Lactic acidosis, Ethylene glycol, Salicylates.

8. Normal Anion Gap Metabolic Acidosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: HARDASS

Hyperalimentation, Acetazolamide, Renal tubular acidosis, Diarrhea, Ammonium chloride, Spironolactone, Saline infusion.

9. Altered Mental Status

🧠 The Mnemonic: AEIOU TIPS

Alcohol, Endocrine/electrolytes, Infection, Opiates/overdose, Uremia, Trauma/tumor, Insulin (hypoglycemia), Poisoning/psychosis, Stroke/seizure.

10. ICU Daily Checklist

🧠 The Mnemonic: FAST-HUGS

Feeding, Analgesia, Sedation, Thromboprophylaxis, Head-of-bed elevation, Ulcer prophylaxis, Glucose control, Spontaneous breathing trial.

11. Differential Diagnosis Engine

🧠 The Mnemonic: VINDICATE

Vascular, Infectious, Neoplastic, Degenerative, Iatrogenic/intoxication, Congenital, Autoimmune, Traumatic, Endocrine/metabolic.

12. Alternative Differential Engine

🧠 The Mnemonic: I'M VICTOR

Infectious, Metabolic, Vascular, Inflammatory, Congenital, Trauma/Toxin, Oncologic, Recreational drugs.

13. Intubated Patient Deterioration

🧠 The Mnemonic: DOPE

Displacement, Obstruction, Pneumothorax, Equipment failure.

14. Reversible Cardiac Arrest Causes - Part 1

🧠 The Mnemonic: H's

Hypovolemia, Hypoxia, Hydrogen ion (acidosis), Hypo-/Hyperkalemia, Hypothermia.

15. Reversible Cardiac Arrest Causes - Part 2

🧠 The Mnemonic: T's

Tension pneumothorax, Tamponade (cardiac), Toxins, Thrombosis (pulmonary), Thrombosis (coronary).

16. Signs of Inflammation

🧠 The Mnemonic: PRISH

Pain, Redness, Immobility, Swelling, Heat.

17. Alcoholism Screening

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAGE

Cut down, Annoyed by criticism, Guilty feeling, Eye-opener drink.

18. Severe Headache Red Flags

🧠 The Mnemonic: MIDAS

Meningeal signs, Immunocompromised, Deficits (neurological), Age >50, Sudden onset (thunderclap).

19. Order of Clinical Workflow

🧠 The Mnemonic: BEDSIDE

Bedside tests, Examinations, Diagnostic imaging, Specialist referral, Interventions, Discharge planning, Education.

20. Fluid Resuscitation Assessment

🧠 The Mnemonic: GIVE ME

Glucose, Intake/output, Vitals, Electrolytes, Mental status, Exam of lungs.

🧠 The Mnemonic: BASE

🧠 The Mnemonic: CHAMPION

Clot stabilization, Halt plaque growth, Anti-inflammatory, Mortality reduction, Protect endothelium, Inhibit HMG-CoA, Optimize LDL, Numerous stroke reductions.

51. Causes of Atrial Fibrillation

🧠 The Mnemonic: BLAST

Blood pressure high, Lung disease (PE/COPD), Atherosclerosis, Stimulants, Thyrotoxicosis.

🧠 The Mnemonic: LEO

53. Right Bundle Branch Block Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: ROOT

Rabbit ears in V1-V2 (M pattern), Overly wide S wave in lateral leads, Opposite ST depression, Turning right axis.

54. High-Output Heart Failure Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: FAIL

55. Arterial Occlusion 4 Classic Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLOT

Claudication, Limb coolness, Obvious pallor, Tissue necrosis.

56. Pericarditis Etiology

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAPE

57. Pacemaker Indications

🧠 The Mnemonic: PACE

Persistent bradycardia, AV block (Mobitz II/3rd degree), Carotid sinus hypersensitivity, Empty output (Sick sinus syndrome).

58. Cardiogenic Shock Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: SHOCK

Systolic BP <90, Heart rate elevated, Oliguria, Cool skin, Kognitive confusion.

59. Syncope Red Flags

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAFE

60. Aspirin Avoidance Contraindications

🧠 The Mnemonic: STEMI

Stomach active ulcer, Terrible allergy, Evident aortic dissection, Massive active bleeding, Intracranial hemorrhage.

Pulmonology & Respiratory Care

🧠 The Mnemonic: CURB-65

Confusion, Urea >7 mmol/L, Respiratory rate ≥30, Blood pressure SBP <90/DBP ≤60, Age ≥65.

102. Lung Cancer Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPHERE

103. PE Clinical Criteria Score

🧠 The Mnemonic: WELLS

104. Small Cell Lung Cancer Biomarkers

105. Transudative Pleural Effusion Causes

107. Sarcoidosis Presentation Features

108. Aspirin Exacerbated Respiratory Disease Triad

109. Klebsiella Pneumonia Risk Groups

154. Acute Tubular Necrosis Urinalysis

🧠 The Mnemonic: MUDDY

Muddy brown granular casts, Urine sodium >40 mEq/L, Drop in urine osmolality <350, Due to ischemia/toxins, Yielding FENa >2%.

155. Prerenal Azotemia Lab Profile

🧠 The Mnemonic: PRERENAL

Perfusion drop, Ratio of BUN/Creatinine >20:1, Efflux FENa <1%, Retention of water makes urine osmolality >500, Empty urine sodium <20, Normal urinary sediment, Aldosterone highly active, Levels normalize with fluids.

156. Hyperkalemia ECG Progression

🧠 The Mnemonic: PEAKED

Peaked T waves, Elongated PR interval, Amplitude of P wave drops, KRS (QRS) widening, Evident sine wave, Death by asystole.

157. Hypokalemia ECG Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: LOW-K

Low flattened T waves, Obvious ST segment depression, Wide prominent U waves, Kardiac arrhythmias.

158. Renal Tubular Acidosis Type 1

🧠 The Mnemonic: DISTAL

Distal tubule H+ pump failure, Inability to acidify urine, Stone formation (calcium phosphate), Terrible hypokalemia, Alkaline urine pH >5.5, Lost bone calcium.

159. Renal Tubular Acidosis Type 2

🧠 The Mnemonic: PROXIMAL

Proximal tubule defect, Reabsorption of HCO3- lost, Overloaded urine with bicarb initially, X-out systemic bicarb storage, Induces hypokalemia, Marked osteomalacia rickets, Acidic urine pH <5.5 late, Loss of fanconi-like solutes.

160. Renal Tubular Acidosis Type 4

🧠 The Mnemonic: ALDO

Aldosterone deficiency or resistance, Lethargic hyperkalemic state, Distal hydrogen pump secondary deceleration, Originates from diabetic nephropathy hyporeninism.
