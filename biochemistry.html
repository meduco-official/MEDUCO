Chapter 16: Biochemistry

Master the molecular foundations of the human body. From the Krebs cycle to enzyme kinetics and clinical pathology, these high-yield memory hooks make complex metabolic pathways unforgettable.

🧬 Amino Acids & Proteins

1. Essential Amino Acids ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: PVT TIM HALL

Phenylalanine, Valine, Threonine, Tryptophan, Isoleucine, Methionine, Histidine, Arginine (essential in growing children), Leucine, Lysine.
Alternative: "MATT VIL PHLy". Note: Pvt. is short for Private in the military. Arg and His are semi-essential.

2. Non-Essential Amino Acids

🧠 The Mnemonic: GASP CANT

Glycine, Alanine, Serine, Proline, Cysteine, Asparagine, Aspartate, Glutamate, Glutamine, Tyrosine.

3. Ketogenic vs. Glucogenic Amino Acids ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: Ketogenic = LL | Both = PITTT

Purely Ketogenic: Leucine, Lysine.
Both Ketogenic & Glucogenic: Phenylalanine, Isoleucine, Tyrosine, Tryptophan, Threonine.

4. Catabolism of Branched-Chain Amino Acids

🧠 The Mnemonic: Truck hit the Ox to Death

Transamination, Oxidative decarboxylation, Dehydrogenation.
Note: In the fasting state, "Muscles LIVe fast" (Skeletal muscles use Leucine, Isoleucine, Valine).

5. Amino Acids Properties (The Beverly Hillbillies Song)

🧠 The Mnemonic: "Come an' listen to my story..."
Five Al-i-phats kick off our show: Glycine, Alanine, Valine and then Leucine and Iso make up half of ten.
Three aromats: Phenylalanine(F) is right off the bat, tYrosine has alcohol next to its ring, and Tryptophan(W) has indole double ring thing.
Sulfur in Cysteine; it loves to bond. Sulfur Methionine is much more a snob.
Alcoholic Serine, well wouldn't you know, and Threonine's OH gives a warm glow.
Acid-aspartic(D) and glutamic(E) are ionized. With pK of 4, their protons are lysed.
Asparagine(N) and glutamine(Q) play a different role. With amides they're neutral but they both have poles.
Lysine(K) and aRginine are the basic kind, but Histidine's imidazole can't make up its mind.
Proline, the last one, coming at the end. It's imine, an oddball, proteins use to bend.

6. Amino Acid Precursors to Important Molecules

🧠 The Mnemonic: GAMs, AAArg & Dracula

Creatine Phosphate: "Nice GAMs!" = Glycine, Arginine, Methionine.
Nitric Oxide (NO): "AAArg!" before the dentist gives NO = Arginine.
Heme Synthesis: "Dracula wants to Suck a Co-ed's blood [heme] with his Glystening teeth" = Succinyl CoA + Glycine.
AcetylCoA: "A Lighter Lease" = A(AcetylCoA), Ly(Lysine), Tr(Tryptophan), Le(Leucine), Is(Isoleucine).

7. Collagen Biosynthesis & Properties

🧠 The Mnemonic: COLLAGEN

C-terminal propeptide / Covalent Cross links / C vitamin / Connective tissue / Cartilage / Chondroblasts / Copper Cofactor.
Outside cell / Osteoblasts / Osteogenesis imperfecta.
Lysyl hydroxylase & Lysyl oxidase / Long triple helical fibers / Ligaments.
Alpha chains / Attached by H bonds / Ascorbate (Vit C) for hydroxylation.
Gly in every third position / Glycosylation with Glucose and Galactose / GOlgi allows procollagen to GO outside cell.
Extracellular matrix / Eye (cornea, sclera) / Ehlers-Danlos Syndrome.
N-terminal propeptide / Nonhelical terminal extensions.

⚡ Enzymes & Pathways

8. Enzyme Classes ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: Over The HILL (OTHLIL)

Oxidoreductase, Transferase, Hydrolase, Lyase, Isomerase, Ligase.

9. Enzyme Kinetics (Competitive vs. Non-Competitive)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Kompetitive = Km increases | Non-kompetitive = Vmax decreases

"Competitive Competes (at the active site), Non-Competitive Never (binds allosteric site)."
With Kompetitive inhibition: Km increases (velocity/Vmax is unchanged). With Non-kompetitive inhibition: No change in Km, but Vmax decreases.

10. Glycolysis Enzymes ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: Hungry Peter Pan And The Growling Pink Panther Buy Pickled Pumpkins To Prepare Pies

Hexokinase, Phosphoglucose isomerase, Phosphofructokinase-1 (PFK), Aldolase, Triose phosphate isomerase, Glyceraldehyde-3-phosphate dehydrogenase, Phosphoglycerate kinase, Phosphoglycerate mutase, Enolase (Buy/Pickled/Pumpkins to Prepare), Pyruvate kinase.
Alternative: "High Profile People Act Too Glamorous, Picture Posing Every Place."

11. Glycolysis Intermediates (Steps)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Goodness Gracious, Father Franklin Did Go By Picking Pumpkins (to) Prepare Pies

Glucose, Glucose-6-P, Fructose-6-P, Fructose-1,6-diP, Dihydroxyacetone-P, Glyceraldehyde-P, 1,3-Biphosphoglycerate, 3-Phosphoglycerate, 2-Phosphoglycerate, Phosphoenolpyruvate [PEP], Pyruvate.

12. TCA (Krebs) Cycle Intermediates ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: Can I Keep Selling Sex For Money, Officer?

Citrate, Isocitrate, α-Ketoglutarate, Succinyl-CoA, Succinate, Fumarate, Malate, Oxaloacetate.
Alternatives: "Our City Is Kept Safe And Sound From Malice" or "Oh Citric Acid Is Of course A SiLly STupid Funny Molecule" (SilLy = SuccinyL, sTupid = SuccinaTe).

13. Urea Cycle

🧠 The Mnemonic: Ordinarily Careless Crappers Are Also Frivolous About Urination

Ornithine, Carbamoyl phosphate, Citrulline, Aspartate, Argininosuccinate, Fumarate, Arginine, Urea.

14. Electron Transport Chain & Inhibitors

🧠 The Mnemonic: I Can Quit Smoking Constantly

Complex I, Complex II, Complex III, Complex IV, Complex V (ATP synthase).
Rotenone: Site-specific inhibitor of Complex ONE.
Carbon Monoxide: "CO blocks CO" (Carbon Monoxide blocks Cytochrome Oxidase).

15. Rate-Limiting Enzymes Master List ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: Key Pace Makers

Glycolysis: PFK is the Pace Maker (Phosphofructokinase-1)
Gluconeogenesis: Pyruvate Carboxylase + PEPCK
TCA Cycle: Isocitrate Dehydrogenase
Cholesterol Synthesis: HMG-CoA Reductase
Urea Cycle: CPS-I Starts (Carbamoyl phosphate synthetase I)
Fatty Acid Synthesis: Acetyl-CoA Carboxylase

🧬 Genetics & Cell Biology

16. DNA and RNA Bases

🧠 The Mnemonic: GAP (Purines) & CUT (Pyrimidines)

Purines: Guanine, Adenine, Purine.
Pyrimidines: Cytosine, Uracil, Thymine.
DNA Bases: ATGC. RNA Bases: AUGC.

17. DNA Bond Strength

🧠 The Mnemonic: Crazy Glue (C-G)

The strongest bonds are between Cytosine and Guanine (3 H-bonds) — strong like Crazy Glue. A=T only have 2 H-bonds, making them the site where RNA primers make the initial break during replication.

18. DNA Replication Enzymes

🧠 The Mnemonic: PHL

Primase, Helicase, Ligase (Also remember DNA polymerase).

19. Mitochondrial DNA (mtDNA)

🧠 The Mnemonic: M T

Maternal Transfer, Mutates Tremendously (very high mutation rate).

20. tRNA Molecular Shape

🧠 The Mnemonic: tRNA = T

Fortunately, tRNA (Transfer RNA) is shaped exactly like a capital T.

21. Golgi Complex Functions

🧠 The Mnemonic: Golgi Distributes A SPAM

Distributes proteins and lipids from ER, Add mannose onto specific lysosome proteins, Sulfation of sugars and tyrosine, Proteoglycan assembly, Add O-oligosugars to serine/threonine, Modify N-oligosugars on asparagine.

22. The Na+/K+ Pump ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: HIKIN' (3 Na out, 2 K in)

There is a HIgh K concentration INside the cell.
Mechanism: "K+" consists of 2 characters (pump 2 K+ in). "Na+" consists of 3 characters (pump 3 Na+ out).
Song: "Na, Na, Na -- out! K, K goes... inside!"

💊 Vitamins, Hormones & Nutrients

23. Water-Soluble vs. Fat-Soluble Vitamins ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: The FAT cat is in the ADEK (attic) | The Bs And C Are Water

Fat Soluble: Vitamins A, D, E, K.
Water Soluble: Vitamin C and all B Vitamins (B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B7, B9, B12).

24. B Vitamin Names in Order

🧠 The Mnemonic: The Rhythm Nearly Proved Contagious

Thiamine (B1), Riboflavin (B2), Niacin (B3), Pyridoxine (B6), Cobalamin (B12).

25. Vitamin Deficiencies (B3 & Folate)

🧠 The Mnemonic: 3 D's of Pellagra | A FOLIC DROP

Niacin (B3) Pellagra: Diarrhea, Dermatitis, Dementia (and Death if untreated). Note that B3 matches the 3 D's.
Folate (B9) Deficiency causes: Alcoholism, Folic acid antagonists, Oral contraceptives, Low dietary intake, Infection (Giardia), Celiac sprue, Dilantin, Relative folate deficiency, Old, Pregnant.

26. Hypervitaminosis A

🧠 The Mnemonic: Increased Vitamin A makes you HARD

Headache/Hepatomegaly, Anorexia/Alopecia, Really painful bones, Dry skin/Drowsiness.

27. Antioxidants

🧠 The Mnemonic: ACE GS

Vitamin A, Vitamin C, Vitamin E, Glutathione, Selenium.

28. Anterior & Posterior Pituitary Hormones

🧠 The Mnemonic: FLAT PEG (Anterior) | AO (Posterior)

Anterior: FSH, LH, ACTH, TSH, Prolactin, Endorphins, Growth hormone.
Posterior: ADH, Oxytocin.

29. Insulin & Glucagon Function

🧠 The Mnemonic: INsulIN | Mr. Gluca has Gone to the cAMP

INsulIN stimulates 2 things to go IN 2cells: Potassium and Glucose.
Glucagon elevates glucose via the cAMP mechanism.

30. G-Protein Receptor Types

🧠 The Mnemonic: KISS and KICK till you're SICK of SEX (QISS & QIQ till you're SIQ of SQS)

Gives the G-protein type (Gq, Gi, Gs). Receptors in alphabetical order:
alpha1=Q, alpha2=I, beta1=S, beta3=S
M1=Q, M2=I, M3=Q
D1=S, D2=I, H1=Q, H2=S, V1=Q, V2=S

🩸 Clinical Pathology & Lab Tests

31. Diabetic Ketoacidosis (DKA)

🧠 The Mnemonic: DKA

Dehydrated, Ketones / Kussmaul breathing / Ketoacidosis / K drops, Acidosis / Acetone breath.

32. Glycogen Storage Diseases ⭐⭐⭐⭐

🧠 The Mnemonic: Very Poor Carbohydrate Metabolism Happens

Von Gierke (Type 1 - "Von = One"), Pompe, Cori, McArdle, Hers.
Enzyme defects for III & IV: ABCD (Anderson's = Branching enzyme. Cori's = Debranching enzyme).
Pompe's Type: "Police = Po + lys" (Pompe's is a lysosomal storage disease).

33. Porphyrias & Fabry's Disease

🧠 The Mnemonic: 5 P's of Porphyria | FABRY'S

Acute Intermittent Porphyria (5 P's): Pain in abdomen, Polyneuropathy, Psychological abnormalities, Pink urine, Precipitated by drugs.
Fabry's Disease: Foam cells / Febrile episodes, Alpha galactosidase A deficiency / Angiokeratomas, Burning pain, Renal failure, Youth death / YX genotype (X-linked), Sphingolipidoses.

34. Hemoglobin Shift to the Right

🧠 The Mnemonic: CADET, face right!

Causes of Hb binding curve shifting right (decreased affinity, dropping off oxygen): CO2, Acid, 2,3-DPG, Exercise, Temperature.

35. Sickle Cell Anemia Mutation

🧠 The Mnemonic: HbS isn't Very Good

At the Sixth position of the Beta chain, Valine is present instead of Glutamic acid. (SICKle cell = Substitution of the SICKsth amino acid).

36. Coagulation Pathway & Vitamin K

🧠 The Mnemonic: 10 + 5 - 2 = 13 | Several Tend To Nicely Stop Clots

Common Pathway: Factor X to Factor V to Factor II to Factor XIII.
Vitamin K Dependent Factors: Factor Seven, Ten, Two, Nine, Protein S, Protein C.

37. Causes of Elevated BUN/Creatinine Ratio

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCD

Azotemia (pre-renal), Bleeding (GI), Catabolic status, Diet (high protein parenteral nutrition).

38. Normal Anion-Gap Metabolic Acidosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: RAISE K+ (Hyper) | ReDUCE K+ (Hypo)

Hyperkalemia (RAISE K+): RTA type 4, Aldosterone deficiency, Iatrogenic (HCl), Stenosis (obstructive uropathy), Early uremia.
Hypokalemia (ReDUCE K+): Renal TA type 1 & 2, Diarrhea, Urine diversion, Carbonate anhydrase inhibitor, Ex-hyperventilation.

⚛️ Chemistry Basics & Nomenclature

39. Oxidation vs. Reduction

🧠 The Mnemonic: OIL RIG | LEO says GER

Oxidation Is Loss (of electrons). Reduction Is Gain.
Lose Electrons = Oxidation. Gain Electrons = Reduction.
Electrochemical Cell: AN OIL RIG CAT (At the ANode, Oxidation Involves Loss. Reduction Involves Gain at the CAThode. Current Carries Cations to the Cathode).

40. Benzene Ring Substituents

🧠 The Mnemonic: Benzene likes to ROMP

R group, Ortho, Meta, Para.
Addition Effects: "We HOPED she's be safe, but now she's knocked up. ADOPt or WEDD'M?"
HOPED: Halogens are Ortho, Para, Electron withdrawing, Deactivating.
ADOPt: Addititive substituents Direct Ortho & Para.
WEDD'M: Withdrawing Electrons Deactivates, Directs Meta.

41. Geometric Isomers & Thermodynamics

🧠 The Mnemonic: Zame Zide | Good Honey Tastes Sweet

Cis/Trans (Z/E): Zame Zide (same side of double bond, written as a Zigzag). Epposite (opposite sides, cut down the middle). Cis starts with a C and forms a C-shape.
Gibb's Free Energy: Good Honey Tastes Sweet -> (delta)G = H - T(delta)S
