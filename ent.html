Subject: Otolaryngology (ENT)

Master otology, rhinology, laryngology, and head & neck anatomy effortlessly with high-yield clinical mnemonics.

1. Branches of the External Carotid Artery

🧠 The Mnemonic: Some Aunties Like Feeding Others Plenty Much Sweets

Superior thyroid, Ascending pharyngeal, Lingual, Facial, Occipital, Posterior auricular, Maxillary, Superficial temporal.

2. Paranasal Sinuses Present at Birth

🧠 The Mnemonic: ME

Maxillary, Ethmoid. (Note: Frontal and sphenoid sinuses develop later in childhood.)

3. Blood Supply of the Palatine Tonsil

🧠 The Mnemonic: HOTeL SPAP

Facial (ascending palatine & tonsillar branches), Lingual (dorsal lingual), Ascending pharyngeal, Maxillary (greater palatine), and Internal maxillary.

4. Causes of Sensorineural Hearing Loss (SNHL)

🧠 The Mnemonic: SENSORS

Presbycusis / Syndrome, Elevated noise, Neuroma, Ototoxins, Origin infectious, Radiation, Surgery.

5. Gradenigo’s Triad (Petrositis)

🧠 The Mnemonic: PAV

Periorbital pain, Abducens nerve palsy, Persistent otorrhea.

6. Complications of Chronic Suppurative Otitis Media (CSOM)

🧠 The Mnemonic: SCALP COST

Sinus thrombosis, Cerebellar abscess, Abscess, Labyrinthitis, Petrositis/cranial nerve palsies, Ostitis, Serous otitis, Tympanic membrane destruction.

7. Differential Diagnosis of a Neck Mass in Adults

🧠 The Mnemonic: CONGRATS

Congenital, Oncologic/Malignant, Inflammatory/Infectious. (80% rule: neoplastic/malignant/metastatic).

8. Causes of Epistaxis (Local vs. Systemic)

🧠 The Mnemonic: TRAUMA

Trauma, Rhinologic, Anterior/Posterior vessels, Uremia, Medications, Atherosclerosis/Hypertension.

9. Stages of Rhinoscleroma

🧠 The Mnemonic: CFR

Catarrhal, Formative/Granulomatous, Cicatricial.

10. Laryngeal Muscle Innervation

🧠 The Mnemonic: Cricothyroid saves the external laryngeal

Cricothyroid is supplied by the external laryngeal nerve; everything else goes to the recurrent laryngeal nerve (RLN).

11. Contents of the Jugular Foramen

🧠 The Mnemonic: 9, 10, 11, and the IJV

Cranial nerves IX (Glossopharyngeal), X (Vagus), XI (Accessory), and the Internal Jugular Vein.

12. Sensory Innervation of the External Auditory Canal (EAC)

🧠 The Mnemonic: V7, 9, 10

CN V3 (Auriculotemporal), CN VII (Facial), CN IX (Jacobson's nerve), CN X (Arnold's nerve).

13. Ear Ossicles from Lateral to Medial

🧠 The Mnemonic: MIS

Malleus, Incus, Stapes.

14. Wall Relations of the Middle Ear

🧠 The Mnemonic: TIM PAt

Tegmen (Roof), Jugular bulb (Floor), Mastoid (Posterior), Promontory (Medial), Artery/Carotid (Anterior), Tympanic membrane (Lateral).

15. Risk Factors for Ototoxicity

🧠 The Mnemonic: KIDNEY

Kidney failure, Intravenous high doses, Diuretics co-administration, Neonate/Elderly, Elevated serum levels, Years of advanced age.

16. Tuning Fork Test Interpretation (Weber Test)

🧠 The Mnemonic: LOUDer is Conductive, Soft is Sensorineural

Lateralizes to affected ear in conductive loss, lateralizes to better ear in sensorineural loss.

17. Features of Meniere's Disease

🧠 The Mnemonic: TV Ear

Tinnitus, Vertigo, Ear fullness, Aural fluctuating hearing loss, Recruitment positive.

18. Facial Nerve Segments

🧠 The Mnemonic: Labyrinthine, Tympanic, Mastoid

Intracranial (Meatal), Labyrinthine, Tympanic, Mastoid.

19. Structures Passing Through the Internal Acoustic Meatus

🧠 The Mnemonic: 7 up, 8 down

CN 7 (Facial) is superior/anterior, CN 8 (Vestibulocochlear) is inferior.

20. Causes of Conductive Hearing Loss (CHL)

🧠 The Mnemonic: WAX OUT

Wax, Attic cholesteatoma, Xanthoma/Tumour, Otosclerosis, Undone/Disrupted ossicles, Tympanic perforation.

21. Medial Wall of the Orbit (Bones)

🧠 The Mnemonic: My Very Little Eyes

Maxilla, Vomer (Ethmoid), Lacrimal, Ethmoid.

22. Kiesselbach's Plexus (Little's Area) Arteries

🧠 The Mnemonic: LEGS

Labial artery (Superior), Ethmoidal artery (Anterior), Greater palatine artery, Sphenopalatine artery.

23. Woodruff’s Plexus (Posterior Epistaxis) Source

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPA

SphenoPalatine Artery.

24. Complications of Sinusitis

🧠 The Mnemonic: OIO

Orbit (Cellulitis/Abscess), Intracranial (Meningitis/Abscess), Osseous (Osteomyelitis/Pott's puffy tumor).

25. Chandler Classification of Orbital Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-P-O-O-C

Preseptal, Postseptal/Orbital cellulitis, Orbital abscess, Ocular immobility/Subperiosteal abscess, Cavernous sinus thrombosis.

26. Lateral Nasal Wall Meatus Drainage

🧠 The Mnemonic: Frontal, Maxillary, Anterior Ethmoid meet in the Middle

These three drain into the Middle meatus. Posterior ethmoid drains to superior; Sphenoid drains to sphenoethmoidal recess.

27. Presentation of Samter's Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: AAP

Asthma, Aspirin sensitivity, Nasal Polyps.

28. Vocal Cord Abductor Muscle

🧠 The Mnemonic: PCA = Pulls Cords Apart

Posterior CricoArytenoid is the only abductor muscle.

29. Vocal Cord Adductor Muscles

🧠 The Mnemonic: LCA = Leans Cords Assembled

Lateral CricoArytenoid (with Interarytenoid) closes the cords.

30. Sensory Innervation Above vs. Below Vocal Cords

🧠 The Mnemonic: Internal is Superior, Recurrent is Inferior

Internal laryngeal nerve supplies sensory above cords; Recurrent laryngeal nerve supplies sensory below.

31. Boundaries of the Carotid Triangle

🧠 The Mnemonic: SDM

Sternocleidomastoid, Digastric (posterior belly), Omohyoid (superior belly).

32. Content of the Submandibular Triangle

🧠 The Mnemonic: FFGH

Facial artery, Facial vein, Glossopharyngeal nerve, Hypoglossal nerve.

33. Layers of the Deep Cervical Fascia

🧠 The Mnemonic: IPTC

Investing, Prevertebral, PreTracheal, Carotid sheath.

34. Deep Neck Space Infections (Ludwig's Angina Spaces)

🧠 The Mnemonic: SMS

Sublingual, Submandibular, Submental spaces.

35. Hard Signs of Penetrating Neck Trauma

🧠 The Mnemonic: BAAD

Blowout hemorrhage, Airway compromise, Air bubbling from wound, Deficit (neurological/pulse).

36. Criteria for Tonsillectomy (Paradise Criteria)

🧠 The Mnemonic: 7-5-3 Rule

7 episodes in past year, 5 per year for 2 years, or 3 per year for 3 years.

37. Risk Factors for Head and Neck SCC

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAVE

Smoking, Alcohol, Virus (HPV/EBV), Environmental toxins.

38. Taste Innervation of the Tongue

🧠 The Mnemonic: 7, 9, 10

Anterior 2/3 = CN VII (Chorda tympani), Posterior 1/3 = CN IX, Root/Epiglottis = CN X.

39. Structures Passing Through the Parotid Gland

🧠 The Mnemonic: FRAG

Facial nerve, Retromandibular vein, Auriculotemporal nerve, Great auricular nerve / External Carotid Artery.

40. True Vocal Cord Histological Layers (Out to In)

🧠 The Mnemonic: ESPDM

Epithelium, Superficial lamina propria (Reinke's), Intermediate, Deep lamina propria, Muscle (Thyroarytenoid).

41. Layers of the Tympanic Membrane

🧠 The Mnemonic: EFM

Epithelial layer (outer), Fibrous layer (middle), Mucosal layer (inner).

42. Structures in the Internal Auditory Canal (Quadrant Anatomy)

🧠 The Mnemonic: 7 up, Coke down; More Mike, Less Peter

7 up: CN VII anterosuperior | Coke down: Cochlear anteroinferior | More Mike: Superior vestibular posterosuperior | Less Peter: Inferior vestibular posteroinferior.

43. Risk Factors for Otitis Media with Effusion (OME)

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLEFT

Cleft palate, Low socioeconomic status, Eustachian tube dysfunction, Family history, Tobacco smoke exposure.

44. Causes of Peripheral Vertigo

🧠 The Mnemonic: BMVL

BPPV, Meniere’s disease, Vestibular neuritis, Labyrinthitis.

45. Absolute Indications for Mastoidectomy

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAPS

Cholesteatoma, Abscess (Mastoid), Petrositis, Significant intracranial complication.

46. Boundaries of the Frontal Recess

🧠 The Mnemonic: APML

Anterior: Agger nasi | Posterior: Bulla ethmoidalis | Medial: Middle turbinate | Lateral: Lamina papyracea.

47. Signs of a Basilar Skull Fracture

🧠 The Mnemonic: BROTH

Battle’s sign, Raccoon eyes, Otorrhea (CSF), Tympanic membrane bruising (Hemotympanum), Hearing deficit.

48. Innervation of the Septum

🧠 The Mnemonic: OPNA

Olfactory nerve, Palatine nerve (Greater), Nasopalatine nerve, Anterior ethmoidal nerve.

49. Functions of the Paranasal Sinuses

🧠 The Mnemonic: WIRE

Weight reduction of skull, Insulation, Resonance of voice, Emmanation (humidification) of air.

50. CSF Rhinorrhoea Lab Tests

🧠 The Mnemonic: BTG

Beta-2 Transferrin (most specific), Glucose level (higher than mucus).

51. Pediatric vs. Adult Airway Differences

🧠 The Mnemonic: SCALP

Shorter trachea, Conic/Funnel shape, Anterior larynx, Large tongue, Pliable epiglottis.

52. Nerve Supply of the Tongue Muscles

🧠 The Mnemonic: All 12 except Palato-10

All intrinsic/extrinsic muscles supplied by CN XII, except Palatoglossus supplied by CN X.

53. Unpaired Laryngeal Cartilages

🧠 The Mnemonic: ECT

Epiglottis, Cricoid, Thyroid.

54. Paired Laryngeal Cartilages

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAC

Corniculate, Arytenoid, Cuneiform.

55. Benign Vocal Cord Lesions

🧠 The Mnemonic: NPC

Nodules (bilateral), Polyps (unilateral), Cysts (subepithelial).

56. Surgical Levels of Neck Dissection

🧠 The Mnemonic: 1 to 6, Top to Bottom, Front to Back

I: Submental/Submandibular, II-IV: Upper/Middle/Lower Jugular, V: Posterior Triangle, VI: Anterior/Central.

57. Major Salivary Glands

🧠 The Mnemonic: PSM

Parotid (serous), Submandibular (mixed), Sublingual (mucous).

58. Absolute Indications for Tracheostomy

🧠 The Mnemonic: BOSS

Bilateral vocal cord paralysis, Obstructing upper airway tumor, Severe maxillofacial trauma, Secretion retention/prolonged ventilation.

59. Management of Anaphylaxis / Acute Stridor

🧠 The Mnemonic: OEAI

Oxygen, Epinephrine, Airway maintenance, IV fluids/corticosteroids.

60. Complications of Thyroidectomy

🧠 The Mnemonic: HIPS

Hemorrhage (hematoma), Infection, Parathyroid injury (hypocalcemia), Superior/Recurrent laryngeal nerve damage.

61. Components of Alport Syndrome

🧠 The Mnemonic: EKH

Eye abnormalities, Kidney disease (nephritis), Hearing loss (SNHL).

62. Features of Waardenburg Syndrome

🧠 The Mnemonic: WHITE

Wide-set eyes, Hearing loss, Iris heterochromia, Tuft of white hair (forelock), Early greying.

63. Symptoms of Acute Epiglottitis

🧠 The Mnemonic: 4 Ds

Drooling, Dysphagia, Dysphonia, Distressed inspiratory stridor.

64. Diagnostic Signs of Choanal Atresia

🧠 The Mnemonic: CANT

Cyclic cyanosis, An inability to pass a 6F catheter, Nasal discharge, Turning pink when crying.

65. Features of Treacher Collins Syndrome

🧠 The Mnemonic: MAXILLA

Mandibulofacial dysostosis, Antimongoloid slant, X-ray showing absent zygoma, Incomplete lower eyelid, Low-set ears, Loss of hearing, Airway restriction.
