Chapter 6: Lymphatic System

The lymphatic system is a network of lymph vessels, lymph nodes, lymphoid organs, and lymph that helps maintain fluid balance, absorb fats, and provide immunity.

1. Functions of the Lymphatic System

The Mnemonic: FID

Fat absorption, Immunity, Drainage of tissue fluid

2. Components of the Lymphatic System

The Mnemonic: VNOL

Lymph Vessels, Lymph Nodes, Lymphoid Organs, Lymph

3. Primary Lymphoid Organs

The Mnemonic: BT

Bone Marrow, Thymus

4. Secondary Lymphoid Organs

The Mnemonic: SALT

Spleen, Adenoids, Lymph Nodes, Tonsils (Also includes MALT such as Peyer's patches and appendix.)

5. Lymphoid Tissue (MALT)

The Mnemonic: PATA

Peyer's patches, Appendix, Tonsils, Adenoids

6. Functions of Lymph

The Mnemonic: FIT

Fat transport, Immunity, Tissue fluid return

7. Functions of Lymph Nodes

The Mnemonic: FIP

Filter lymph, Immune response, Produce lymphocytes

8. Structure of a Lymph Node

The Mnemonic: CCCM

Capsule, Cortex, Central Medulla, Medullary cords/sinuses

9. Afferent vs Efferent Lymphatics

The Mnemonic: A Comes, E Exits

Afferent → enters lymph node | Efferent → leaves lymph node

10. Spleen Functions

The Mnemonic: FILTER

Filters blood, Immunity, Lymphocyte production, Traps old RBCs, Emergency blood reservoir, Recycles iron

11. White Pulp of Spleen

The Mnemonic: White = White Blood Cells

Contains lymphocytes.

12. Red Pulp of Spleen

The Mnemonic: Red Removes Red Cells

Destroys old RBCs.

13. Thymus Functions

The Mnemonic: T Train T Cells

Maturation of T lymphocytes and secretes thymosin.

14. Tonsils

The Mnemonic: PLT

Palatine, Lingual, Pharyngeal (Tonsil)

15. Waldeyer's Ring

The Mnemonic: PALT

Pharyngeal tonsil, Adenoid, Lingual tonsil, Tubal & Palatine tonsils

16. Types of Immunity

The Mnemonic: IA

Innate, Adaptive

17. Adaptive Immunity

The Mnemonic: HC

Humoral, Cell-mediated

18. Types of Lymphocytes

The Mnemonic: BTNK

B cells, T cells, Natural Killer cells, Killer function

19. B Cell Function

The Mnemonic: B Builds Antibodies

Primary cells responsible for humoral immunity.

20. T Cell Function

The Mnemonic: T Targets Infected Cells

Primary cells responsible for cell-mediated immunity.

21. Natural Killer Cells

The Mnemonic: NK = Natural Killers

Destroy virus-infected and tumor cells without prior sensitization.

22. Lymphatic Ducts

The Mnemonic: RT

Right lymphatic duct, Thoracic duct

23. Thoracic Duct Drains

The Mnemonic: Thoracic Takes The Rest

Drains almost the entire body except the right upper quadrant.

24. Right Lymphatic Duct Drains

The Mnemonic: Right = Right Upper Quarter

Right side of head & neck, Right upper limb, Right side of thorax.

25. Cisterna Chyli

The Mnemonic: Chyle Collector

The dilated sac at the beginning of the thoracic duct.

26. Lacteals

The Mnemonic: Lacteals Love Lipids

Absorb dietary fats in the small intestine.

27. Lymph Flow

The Mnemonic: TVNVB

Tissue fluid → Lymph Vessels → Lymph Nodes → Lymphatic Ducts (Vessels) → Blood (venous circulation)

28. Causes of Lymph Flow

The Mnemonic: MSP

Muscle contraction, Skeletal muscle pump, Pressure changes during breathing

29. Lymph Node Regions

The Mnemonic: CANI

Cervical, Axillary, Nodes (Inguinal), Intestinal (mesenteric)

30. Clinical Importance

The Mnemonic: LIMP

Lymphedema, Infection, Metastasis, Primary lymphomas
