Subject: Orthopedics

Master bone trauma, joint dislocations, nerve injuries, pediatric orthopedics, and complex surgical anatomy with these high-yield mnemonics.

Forearm, Humerus & Hand Trauma

1. Forearm Fractures & Dislocations

🧠 The Mnemonic: MUGR

Monteggia: Ulna fracture with proximal radial head dislocation.
Galeazzi: Radius fracture with distal radioulnar joint dislocation.

2. Nerve Injuries from Humerus Fractures

🧠 The Mnemonic: ARMU

Axillary nerve: Proximal humerus / Surgical neck fracture.
Radial nerve: Mid-shaft humerus / Spiral groove fracture.
Median nerve: Supracondylar humerus fracture (anteromedial displacement).
Ulnar nerve: Medial epicondyle fracture.

3. Hand Deformities from Nerve Damage

🧠 The Mnemonic: Dr. CUMA

Drop wrist: Radial nerve injury.
Claw hand: Ulnar nerve injury.
Median nerve: Ape thumb deformity.

4. Pediatric Physeal Injuries

🧠 The Mnemonic: SALTER

Straight across: Type 1 (Slipped physis only).
Above: Type 2 (Physis and metaphysis).
Lower: Type 3 (Physis and epiphysis).
Through: Type 4 (Metaphysis, physis, and epiphysis).
ERased / Rammed: Type 5 (Crushed/compressed physis).

5. Compartment Syndrome Emergency

🧠 The Mnemonic: The 6 P's

Pain (out of proportion to injury - earliest sign), Pallor, Pulselessness (late sign), Paresthesia, Paralysis, Poikilothermia (cold limb).

6. Shoulder Rotator Cuff Muscles

🧠 The Mnemonic: SITS

Supraspinatus (Initiates arm abduction), Infraspinatus (Controls external rotation), Teres minor (Aids external rotation), Subscapularis (Controls internal rotation).

7. Pediatric Hip Dysplasia Tests

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-O-B & O-A-B

Barlow Outs the Bone: Adduction and downward pressure pushes the loose hip out.
Ortolani And Back: Abduction pulls the hip back into place with a "clunk".

8. Carpal Tunnel Syndrome Etiology

🧠 The Mnemonic: MEDIAN TRAP

Myxedema, Edema, Diabetes mellitus, Idiopathic, Acromegaly, Neoplasia, Trauma, Rheumatoid arthritis, Amyloidosis, Progression of age.

9. Carpal Tunnel Management

🧠 The Mnemonic: WRIST

Wear splint (Volar wrist splint at night), Rest, Inject (Corticosteroid), Surgical decompression, Take NSAIDs.

Fracture Rules & Classifications

10. Open Fracture Sub-classification

🧠 The Mnemonic: Gustilo-Anderson Type 3

3A: Adequate soft tissue coverage.
3B: Bone exposed; requires soft tissue flap.
3C: Circulation compromised; requires arterial repair.

11. Fracture Descriptors

🧠 The Mnemonic: BLT LARD

Bone, Location, Type (Transverse/oblique/spiral), Lengthening (or shortening), Angulation, Rotation, Displacement.

12. Reasons for Bone Nonunion

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPLINT

Soft tissue interposition, Position (Poor reduction), Location (Poor vascularity), Infection, Nutrition, Tumor.

13. Hip Dislocation Leg Attitude

🧠 The Mnemonic: FADIR vs FABER

Posterior Dislocation: FADIR (Flexion, Adduction, Internal Rotation).
Anterior Dislocation: FABER (Flexion, Abduction, External Rotation).

14. Brachial Plexus Birth Palsies

🧠 The Mnemonic: Erb's vs Klumpke's

Erb's = Engages Upper roots (C5-C6) → Waiter's tip hand.
Klumpke's = Klaw hand → Lower roots (C8-T1).

15. Femoral Triangle Architecture

🧠 The Mnemonic: NAVEL

Nerve, Artery, Vein, Empty space, Lymphatics (Lateral to medial).

16. Thoracic Outlet Syndrome Provocative Tests

🧠 The Mnemonic: WAR

Wright's test, Adson's test, Roos test (EAST for 3 minutes).

17. Foot Drop Tendon Pathology

🧠 The Mnemonic: TA drops the Toe

Tibialis Anterior (TA): Responsible for ankle dorsiflexion. Injury or peroneal nerve palsy causes foot drop.

18. Orthopedic Emergency Differential Diagnoses

🧠 The Mnemonic: SCARED OF

Septic arthritis, Compartment syndrome, Abuse (Non-accidental trauma), Referred pain, Examiner / Report error, Disease / Occult Fracture.

19. Sacroiliitis Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: PUB CAR

Psoriatic arthritis, Ulcerative colitis, Behcet's disease, Crohn's disease, Ankylosing spondylitis, Reiter’s syndrome.

20. Ulnar vs Median Nerve Clinical Testing

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDEF vs MNOP

Ulnar (ABCDEF): Alnar nerve → Book test (Froment's), Card test, Dorsal interossei test.
Median (MNOP): Median nerve → Nevin sign, O-clasp test, Pen test.

Forearm, Wrist & Hand Anatomy

21. Muscle layers of the anterior forearm (Superficial to deep)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Please Try Not To Lose Control, Max

Pronator teres, Flexor carpi radialis, Palmaris longus, Flexor carpi ulnaris, Flexor digitorum superficialis, Flexor digitorum profundus, Pronator quadratus.

22. Proximal to distal carpal bones (Lateral to medial)

🧠 The Mnemonic: She Looks Too Pretty, Try To Catch Her

Scaphoid, Lunate, Triquetrum, Pisiform, Trapezium, Trapezoid, Capitate, Hamate.

23. Proximal row carpal ossification order

🧠 The Mnemonic: Only Bones Lack Capacities

Ossification begins: Capitate (1-3 months).
Bones follow: Hamate (2-4 months).
Lunate (2-4 years).
Carpals continue: Triquetrum (2-4 years).

24. Radial carpal alignment

🧠 The Mnemonic: Trapezium Thumb

Trapezium: Articulates directly with the first metacarpal (thumb).

25. Hand interossei muscle actions

🧠 The Mnemonic: Palmar PAD, Dorsal DAB

Palmar interossei: Adduct the fingers.
Dorsal interossei: Abduct the fingers.

26. Muscles innervated by the Median Nerve in the hand

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLOAF

Co-innervated: First two Lumbricals, Opponens pollicis, Abductor pollicis brevis, Flexor pollicis brevis.

27. Guyon’s canal contents

🧠 The Mnemonic: Medial Mirror

Ulnar nerve: Sits medial to the ulnar artery inside the canal.

Upper Limb Fractures & Deformities

28. Colles’ fracture displacement

🧠 The Mnemonic: Posterior Push

Posterior: Distal radius fragment displaces dorsally ("dinner fork").

29. Smith’s fracture presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: Smith = Spade

Smith's: Distal radius fragment displaces volarly ("garden spade").

30. Barton's fracture anatomy

🧠 The Mnemonic: Cut By Blade

Carpal subluxation: Accompanies this intra-articular distal radius fracture.

31. Chauffeur’s fracture mechanism

🧠 The Mnemonic: Chauffeur Crank

Crank start: Backfire hits radial styloid (isolated intra-articular fracture).

32. Boxer's fracture definition

🧠 The Mnemonic: Boxer Bang

Break: Fracture of the 5th metacarpal neck from punching.

33. Bennett’s fracture location

🧠 The Mnemonic: Base Bennett

Base: Intra-articular fracture-subluxation at the base of the 1st metacarpal.

34. Rolando fracture pattern

🧠 The Mnemonic: Rolando Y-split

Comminuted: T- or Y-shaped comminuted base of the 1st metacarpal.

35. Biceps tendon insertion

🧠 The Mnemonic: Biceps Button

Radial tuberosity: The berthed anchor point for the distal biceps.

36. Holstein-Lewis fracture risk

🧠 The Mnemonic: Holiday Humerus

Radial nerve: Trapped in distal 1/3 humerus shaft fractures.

37. Shoulder dislocation prevalence

🧠 The Mnemonic: Posterior Push Shoulder

Anterior: Most common (95%); Posterior is rare (seizures/shocks).

Spine & Pelvis

38. Phrenic nerve roots

🧠 The Mnemonic: C3-4-5 Keep the Diaphragm Alive

Cervical roots: Injury above C3 causes immediate respiratory paralysis.

39. Pudendal nerve roots

🧠 The Mnemonic: S2-3-4 Keep the Poop off the Floor

Sacral roots: Controls the anal and urethral sphincters.

40. L4 nerve root clinical test

🧠 The Mnemonic: L4 Foot Lift

L4: Controls Tibialis Anterior (Ankle dorsiflexion and patellar reflex).

41. L5 nerve root clinical test

🧠 The Mnemonic: L5 Long Toe

L5: Controls Extensor Hallucis Longus (Great toe extension).

42. S1 nerve root clinical test

🧠 The Mnemonic: S1 Sole Stand

S1: Controls Gastrocnemius (Ankle plantarflexion and Achilles reflex).

43. Thoracic spinal dermatome

🧠 The Mnemonic: Ten Titty

T10: Dermatome level located right at the umbilicus.

44. Lumbar spinal dermatome

🧠 The Mnemonic: Four Feed

L4: Dermatome crosses directly over the knee.

45. Anterior Cord Syndrome cause

🧠 The Mnemonic: Anterior Accident

Artery: Flexion injuries compressing the anterior spinal artery.

46. Brown-Séquard Syndrome deficits

🧠 The Mnemonic: Motor Meets Opposite

Ipsilateral: Motor loss.
Contralateral: Pain and temperature loss.

47. Cauda Equina Syndrome urgency

🧠 The Mnemonic: Saddle Surgical

Saddle anesthesia: Absolute surgical emergency to prevent permanent bladder loss.

Lower Limb & Sports Injuries

48. Deep calf muscles (Medial malleolus order)

🧠 The Mnemonic: Pass Around The Liquor

Tibialis posterior, Flexor digitorum longus, Posterior tibial artery, Tibial nerve, Flexor hallucis longus.

49. Anterior Cruciate Ligament function

🧠 The Mnemonic: ACL Apt

Anterior: Prevents anterior displacement of tibia on femur.

50. Posterior Cruciate Ligament function

🧠 The Mnemonic: PCL Push

Posterior: Prevents posterior displacement of tibia on femur.

51. Meniscal mobility comparison

🧠 The Mnemonic: Medial Meniscus Moored

Medial: Fixed tightly to MCL; more commonly torn than lateral.

52. Severe knee blow components

🧠 The Mnemonic: O'Donoghue's Terrible Triad

ACL tear, MCL tear, Medial meniscus tear (or lateral meniscus in acute sports).

53. Hamstring insertion trick

🧠 The Mnemonic: Bicep Bouter

Fibular head: Biceps femoris inserts laterally on the fibular head.

54. Tarsal tunnel syndrome contents

🧠 The Mnemonic: Tom, Dick, And Harry

Tibialis posterior, Flexor digitorum longus, Artery (Posterior tibial), Nerve (Tibial), Hallucis longus (Flexor).

55. Peroneal nerve division

🧠 The Mnemonic: Peroneal Points Plantor

Superficial peroneal: Controls foot eversion.
Deep peroneal: Controls foot dorsiflexion.

56. Morton's Neuroma location

🧠 The Mnemonic: Morton’s Middle

Metatarsals: Most commonly found between the 3rd and 4th toes.

Bone Tumors, Infections & Trauma

57. Ewing Sarcoma presentation & X-ray look

🧠 The Mnemonic: Malignant Midshaft / Ewing Eggshell

Midshaft: Diaphyseal bone tumor seen in children.
Onion-skin: Concentric periosteal reaction mimicking onion layers.

58. Osteosarcoma location & X-ray sign

🧠 The Mnemonic: Osteo Outside / Codman Cancer

Metaphysis: Occurs predominantly around the knee.
Codman's triangle: Periosteal elevation forming a triangle on X-ray.

59. Osteoid Osteoma behavior

🧠 The Mnemonic: Night NSAID

Night pain: Severe bone pain dramatically relieved by aspirin/NSAIDs.

60. Acute soft-tissue injury management protocol

🧠 The Mnemonic: PRICE

Protect, Rest, Ice, Compress, Elevate.
