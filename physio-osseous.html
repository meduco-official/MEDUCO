Chapter 4: Osseous System

The Osseous System includes all the bones, cartilages, joints, and ligaments that form the skeleton. Use these memory techniques to master skeletal divisions, bone types, and ossification processes.

1. Functions of Bone

The Mnemonic: SPERM B

Support, Protection, Electrolyte storage (Ca²⁺, PO₄³⁻), Red blood cell formation (hematopoiesis), Movement, Body fat storage (yellow marrow)

2. Divisions of Skeleton

The Mnemonic: AA

Axial Skeleton, Appendicular Skeleton

3. Bones of Axial Skeleton

The Mnemonic: SHRV

Skull, Hyoid bone, Ribs & Sternum, Vertebral column

4. Bones of Appendicular Skeleton

The Mnemonic: PLL

Pectoral girdle, Upper Limb, Lower Limb (including pelvic girdle)

5. Types of Bones (by Shape)

The Mnemonic: Long Students Study Flat Irregular Pages

Long, Short, Sesamoid, Flat, Irregular, Pneumatic

6. Examples of Long Bones

The Mnemonic: HURTFT

Humerus, Ulna, Radius, Tibia, Fibula, Temur (Femur)

7. Examples of Short Bones

The Mnemonic: Carpal & Tarsal = Short

The bones of the wrist (carpals) and ankle (tarsals) are the primary short bones.

8. Flat Bones

The Mnemonic: SSP

Skull bones, Sternum, Protective flat ribs

9. Irregular Bones

The Mnemonic: VIP

Vertebrae, Ihip (Hip bone), Psphenoid (example of irregular skull bone)

10. Sesamoid Bones

The Mnemonic: Patella Protects

The most important sesamoid bone is the Patella.

11. Pneumatic Bones

The Mnemonic: FEMS

Frontal, Ethmoid, Maxilla, Sphenoid

12. Parts of Long Bone

The Mnemonic: DEM

Diaphysis, Epiphysis, Metaphysis

13. Structure of Long Bone

The Mnemonic: CPME

Compact bone, Periosteum, Medullary cavity, Endosteum

14. Layers Covering Bone

The Mnemonic: PE

Periosteum (outer), Endosteum (inner)

15. Bone Cells

The Mnemonic: Blast Builds, Clast Crushes, Cyte Cares

Osteoblast builds bone, Osteoclast resorbs bone, Osteocyte maintains bone

16. Ossification Types

The Mnemonic: IM

Intramembranous, M (Endochondral) - Memorize the pair

17. Bones Formed by Intramembranous Ossification

The Mnemonic: Flat Face Forms First

Flat bones of skull, Facial bones, Forming part of clavicle

18. Bones Formed by Endochondral Ossification

The Mnemonic: Long Bones Love Cartilage

Most long bones develop from cartilage.

19. Bone Matrix

The Mnemonic: COH

Collagen, Osteoid, Hydroxyapatite

20. Functions of Periosteum

The Mnemonic: NAG

Nourishment, Appositional growth, Gives attachment

21. Compact Bone

The Mnemonic: HO

Haversian system, Osteons

22. Osteon Parts

The Mnemonic: HLLC

Haversian canal, Lamellae, Lacunae, Canaliculi

23. Bone Blood Supply

The Mnemonic: NMPE

Nutrient artery, Metaphyseal artery, Periosteal artery, Epiphyseal artery

24. Bone Remodeling Hormones

The Mnemonic: PCTD

PTH, Calcitonin, Thyroid hormones, Vitamin D

25. Bone Marrow

The Mnemonic: RY

Red marrow, Yellow marrow

26. Red Marrow Function

The Mnemonic: RBC Factory

Produces blood cells.

27. Yellow Marrow Function

The Mnemonic: Yellow = Fat

Stores fat.

28. Bone Healing Stages

The Mnemonic: HFCR

Hematoma, Fibrocartilaginous callus, Cony (hard) callus, Remodeling

29. Bone Classification by Development

The Mnemonic: Primary Before Secondary

Primary ossification center, Secondary ossification center

30. Skeleton Numbers

The Mnemonic: 206 Adult, 270 Baby

Adult = 206 bones, Newborn ≈ 270 bones
