Chapter 10: Skeletal Muscles

The Skeletal Muscle System is responsible for body movement, posture, heat production, and joint stability.

1. Functions of Skeletal Muscles

The Mnemonic: MPHS

Movement, Posture, Heat production, Stabilization of joints

2. Types of Muscle Tissue

The Mnemonic: SSC

Skeletal, Smooth, Cardiac

3. Characteristics of Skeletal Muscle

The Mnemonic: SVMS

Striated, Voluntary, Multinucleated, Somatic nerve supply

4. Connective Tissue Coverings of Muscle

The Mnemonic: EPM

Endomysium, Perimysium, Epimysium (M)

5. Structural Organization of Skeletal Muscle

The Mnemonic: MMFFMS

Muscle, Muscle fascicle, Muscle fiber, Myofibril, Myofilament, Sarcomere

6. Types of Myofilaments

The Mnemonic: AT

Actin (Thin filament), Thick filament (Myosin)

7. Components of Thin Filament

The Mnemonic: ATT

Actin, Tropomyosin, Troponin

8. Troponin Components

The Mnemonic: ICT

Troponin I, Troponin C, Troponin T

9. Sarcomere Parts

The Mnemonic: AZHM

A Band, Z Line, H Zone, M Line

10. Sliding Filament Theory

The Mnemonic: Myosin Pulls Actin

Myosin heads pull actin toward the center of the sarcomere via the power stroke, shortening the sarcomere.

11. Requirements for Muscle Contraction

The Mnemonic: CAT

Calcium, ATP, Troponin

12. Sliding Filament Changes

The Mnemonic: I & H Hide, A Always

During contraction: I Band decreases, H Zone decreases/disappears, A Band remains constant.

13. Neuromuscular Junction

The Mnemonic: NAM

Nerve ending, Acetylcholine, Muscle membrane

14. Neurotransmitter at NMJ

The Mnemonic: ACh Acts

Acetylcholine is released at the neuromuscular junction.

15. Sequence of Muscle Contraction

The Mnemonic: NACCMR

Nerve impulse, Acetylcholine release, Calcium release, Cross-bridge formation, Muscle contraction, Relaxation

16. Muscle Relaxation

The Mnemonic: ATP Pumps Calcium Back

Relaxation requires ATP and Calcium reuptake into the sarcoplasmic reticulum.

17. Muscle Fiber Types

The Mnemonic: RSW

Red fibers (Slow oxidative), Slow-twitch, White fibers (Fast glycolytic)

18. Red Muscle Fibers

The Mnemonic: RAM

Rich in mitochondria, Aerobic metabolism, Myoglobin rich

19. White Muscle Fibers

The Mnemonic: FGF

Fast contraction, Glycolytic, Fatigue quickly

20. Muscle Shapes

The Mnemonic: PCQC

Parallel, Circular, Quadrate, Convergent

21. Muscle Actions

The Mnemonic: FAME

Flexion, Adduction, Medial rotation, Extension

22. Muscle Roles

The Mnemonic: PAFS

Prime mover (Agonist), Antagonist, Fixator, Synergist

23. Types of Muscle Contraction

The Mnemonic: ICI

Isometric, Concentric, Eccentric (I - remember as "Inverse" lengthening)

24. Muscle Blood Supply

The Mnemonic: AVC

Artery, Vein, Capillary

25. Muscle Energy Sources

The Mnemonic: AGF

ATP, Glycogen (Glucose), Fatty acids

26. Major Muscle Disorders

The Mnemonic: CMDM

Cramps, Myasthenia gravis, Duscular (Muscular) dystrophy, Myositis

27. Rotator Cuff Muscles

The Mnemonic: SITS

Supraspinatus, Infraspinatus, Teres minor, Subscapularis

28. Hamstring Muscles

The Mnemonic: Semi Semi Bi

Semitendinosus, Semimembranosus, Biceps femoris

29. Quadriceps Muscles

The Mnemonic: RVIM

Rectus femoris, Vastus lateralis, I (Vastus intermedius), M (Vastus medialis)

30. Muscles of Mastication

The Mnemonic: MMTL

Masseter, Medial pterygoid, Temporalis, Lateral pterygoid
