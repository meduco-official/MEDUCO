Chapter 5: Haemopoietic System

The Haemopoietic System is responsible for the formation, development, and maturation of blood cells. It includes bone marrow, blood, lymphoid organs, and blood-forming stem cells.

1. Components of the Haemopoietic System

The Mnemonic: BBLS

Bone marrow, Blood, Lymphoid organs, Stem cells

2. Functions of Blood

The Mnemonic: TRIP

Transport, Regulation, Immunity, Protection (clotting)

3. Components of Blood

The Mnemonic: PRWP

Plasma, Red Blood Cells (RBCs), White Blood Cells (WBCs), Platelets

4. Plasma Components

The Mnemonic: WAPS

Water, Albumin, Plasma proteins, Salts & solutes

5. Plasma Proteins

The Mnemonic: AFG

Albumin, Fibrinogen, Globulins

6. Functions of Albumin

The Mnemonic: TOP

Transport, Osmotic pressure, Protein reserve

7. Formed Elements of Blood

The Mnemonic: RWP

RBCs, WBCs, Platelets

8. Hematopoiesis Sites by Age

The Mnemonic: YSLBM

Yolk sac (embryo), Spleen, Liver, Bone marrow, Mature bone marrow (after birth)

9. Adult Active Bone Marrow

The Mnemonic: RIPS

Ribs, Iliac crest, Pelvis, Sternum (Also vertebrae and skull contribute.)

10. Blood Cell Lineages

The Mnemonic: MEM

Myeloid stem cell, Erythroid lineage, Megakaryocyte lineage

11. Blood Cell Types

The Mnemonic: RWP

Red cells, White cells, Platelets

12. RBC Functions

The Mnemonic: OCT

Oxygen transport, Carbon dioxide transport, Tbuffering (acid–base balance)

13. RBC Production Needs

The Mnemonic: BIFE

Vitamin B₁₂, Iron, Folic acid, Erythropoietin

14. RBC Life Span

The Mnemonic: 120 Days = 4 Months

The average lifespan of a circulating red blood cell.

15. RBC Destruction

The Mnemonic: SLL

Spleen, Liver, Limited Bone marrow (lesser extent)

16. White Blood Cells

The Mnemonic: Never Let Monkeys Eat Bananas

Neutrophils, Lymphocytes, Monocytes, Eosinophils, Basophils

17. Differential WBC Count (Normal Order)

The Mnemonic: Never Let Monkeys Eat Bananas

Lists WBCs in order of frequency: Neutrophils, Lymphocytes, Monocytes, Eosinophils, Basophils

18. Granulocytes

The Mnemonic: NEB

Neutrophils, Eosinophils, Basophils

19. Agranulocytes

The Mnemonic: LM

Lymphocytes, Monocytes

20. Functions of Neutrophils

The Mnemonic: NAP

Neutralize bacteria, Acute inflammation, Phagocytosis

21. Functions of Eosinophils

The Mnemonic: PAA

Parasites, Allergy, Asthma

22. Functions of Basophils

The Mnemonic: HH

Histamine, Heparin

23. Functions of Monocytes

The Mnemonic: MAP

Macrophages, Antigen presentation, Phagocytosis

24. Functions of Lymphocytes

The Mnemonic: BTNK

B cells, T cells, Natural killer cells, Killer function

25. Platelet Functions

The Mnemonic: CAP

Clot formation, Aggregation, Prevent bleeding

26. Platelet Life Span

The Mnemonic: 10 Days

Usually 7–10 days.

27. Clotting Factors

The Mnemonic: Fabulous People Try Climbing Long Slopes After Christmas Some People Have Fallen

This helps remember Factors I–XIII in order.

28. Hemostasis Steps

The Mnemonic: VPC

Vasoconstriction, Platelet plug, Coagulation

29. Blood Groups

The Mnemonic: ABO + Rh

Blood typing includes: A, B, AB, O, and Rh positive/negative

30. Universal Donor & Recipient

The Mnemonic: O Gives / AB Accepts

O Gives → O negative = Universal donor | AB Accepts → AB positive = Universal recipient

31. Hemoglobin Structure

The Mnemonic: 4 Globins + 4 Heme

Hemoglobin = 4 globin chains + 4 heme groups.

32. Iron Metabolism

The Mnemonic: FIT

Ferritin (storage), Iron, Transferrin (transport)

33. Major Hematopoietic Growth Factors

The Mnemonic: EGT

Erythropoietin, GM-CSF, Thrombopoietin

34. Bone Marrow Cell Lineages

The Mnemonic: MEN

Myeloid, Erythroid, Nymphoid (Lymphoid - think "New immune cells")
