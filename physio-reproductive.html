Chapter 15: Reproductive System

The Reproductive System is responsible for reproduction, production of gametes, secretion of sex hormones, and development of secondary sexual characteristics.

1. Components of the Male Reproductive System

The Mnemonic: TEVSPP

Testes, Epididymis, Vas deferens, Seminal vesicles, Prostate gland, Penis

2. Components of the Female Reproductive System

The Mnemonic: OVUTV

Ovaries, Vterine (Uterine/Fallopian) tubes, Uterus, Tunnel (Cervix), Vagina

3. Functions of the Testis

The Mnemonic: ST

Sperm production, Testosterone secretion

4. Parts of the Testis

The Mnemonic: TLR

Tunica albuginea, Lobules, Rete testis

5. Pathway of Sperm

The Mnemonic: SEVEPU

Seminiferous tubules, Epididymis, Vas deferens, Ejaculatory duct, Prostatic urethra, Urethra

6. Cells of Seminiferous Tubules

The Mnemonic: SSL

Spermatogenic cells, Sertoli cells, Leydig cells (between tubules)

7. Sertoli Cell Functions

The Mnemonic: SNAB

Support developing sperm, Nutrition, Androgen-binding protein, Blood-testis barrier

8. Leydig Cell Function

The Mnemonic: Leydig Loves Testosterone

Produces testosterone.

9. Spermatogenesis Stages

The Mnemonic: SSST

Spermatogonia, Primary Spermatocyte, Spermatid, Spermatozoa (T for Tail development/spermaTozoa)

10. Parts of a Sperm

The Mnemonic: HMT

Head, Middle piece, Tail

11. Structures of the Sperm Head

The Mnemonic: AN

Acrosome, Nucleus

12. Accessory Glands

The Mnemonic: SPB

Seminal vesicle, Prostate, Bulbourethral gland

13. Functions of Seminal Vesicles

The Mnemonic: FPS

Fructose secretion, Prostaglandins, Seminal fluid

14. Functions of the Prostate

The Mnemonic: PAF

Prostatic fluid, Activates sperm, Fertility support

15. Functions of Bulbourethral Glands

The Mnemonic: LUBE

Lubricates urethra, Neutralizes acidity before ejaculation

16. Parts of the Penis

The Mnemonic: 2C1S

2C – Two Corpora cavernosa, 1S – One Corpus spongiosum

17. Scrotum Function

The Mnemonic: 2°C Cooler

Keeps testes about 2–3°C below body temperature.

18. Ovarian Functions

The Mnemonic: OE

Ovum production, Estrogen & progesterone secretion

19. Parts of the Ovary

The Mnemonic: CMM

Cortex, Medulla, Mature follicles

20. Oogenesis

The Mnemonic: OOOP

Oogonia, Primary Oocyte, Secondary Oocyte, P (Ovum)

21. Follicle Development

The Mnemonic: PSGG

Primordial, Sprimary (Primary), Growing (Secondary), Graafian follicle

22. Uterine Tube Parts

The Mnemonic: FIAI

Fimbriae, Infundibulum, Ampulla, Isthmus

23. Site of Fertilization

The Mnemonic: Ampulla = Amazing Meeting Place

Fertilization usually occurs in the ampulla.

24. Layers of the Uterus

The Mnemonic: PEM

Perimetrium, Endometrium, Myometrium

25. Parts of the Uterus

The Mnemonic: FBI

Fundus, Body, Isthmus (leading to cervix)

26. Parts of the Vagina

The Mnemonic: CFM

Cervix end, Fornices, Muscular canal

27. Menstrual Cycle Phases

The Mnemonic: MPOS

Menstrual, Proliferative, Ovulation, Secretory

28. Ovulation

The Mnemonic: Day 14 = Egg Out

Occurs around day 14 of a 28-day cycle.

29. Hormones of the Menstrual Cycle

The Mnemonic: FLEP

FSH, LH, Estrogen, Progesterone

30. Hormone Functions

The Mnemonic: FELP

FSH → Follicle growth, Estrogen → Endometrial proliferation, LH → Ovulation, Progesterone → Pregnancy support

31. Fertilization Steps

The Mnemonic: CAMB

Capacitation, Acrosome reaction, Membrane fusion, Blastocyst formation

32. Placenta Functions

The Mnemonic: NGEH

Nutrition, Gas exchange, Excretion, Hormone production

33. Placental Hormones

The Mnemonic: HEPP

HCG (hCG), Estrogen, Progesterone, Placental lactogen (hPL)

34. Mammary Gland Hormones

The Mnemonic: POM

Prolactin (milk production), Oxytocin (milk ejection), Mammary gland

35. Common Clinical Conditions

The Mnemonic: PCEF

Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS), Cryptorchidism, Ectopic pregnancy, Fibroids
