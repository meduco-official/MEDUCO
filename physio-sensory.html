Chapter 12: Sensory Organs

The Sensory Organs include the eye, ear, nose, tongue, and skin, which receive and transmit sensory information to the brain.

1. Five Sense Organs

The Mnemonic: EENTS

Eye, Ear, Nose, Tongue, Skin

2. Five Special Senses

The Mnemonic: SVGST

Sight (Vision), Vestibular (Balance), Gustation (Taste), Smell (Olfaction), T (Hearing - Think "Tone")

3. Fibrous Layer of the Eye

The Mnemonic: SC

Sclera, Cornea

4. Vascular Layer (Uvea)

The Mnemonic: CCI

Choroid, Ciliary body, Iris

5. Nervous Layer of the Eye

The Mnemonic: Retina Receives

Retina contains photoreceptors that receive light.

6. Layers of the Eyeball

The Mnemonic: FVN

Fibrous layer, Vascular layer, Nervous layer

7. Transparent Structures of the Eye

The Mnemonic: CALL

Cornea, Aqueous humor, Lens, L (Lens to Vitreous humor - Light passes through vitreous)

8. Light Path Through the Eye

The Mnemonic: CALVRON

Cornea, Aqueous humor, Lens, Vitreous humor, Retina, Optic nerve, N (Nervous system/Brain)

9. Photoreceptors

The Mnemonic: RC

Rods, Cones

10. Rods Function

The Mnemonic: R = Night

Dim light, Black & white vision.

11. Cones Function

The Mnemonic: C = Color

Color vision, Daylight vision.

12. Primary Colors Detected by Cones

The Mnemonic: RGB

Red, Green, Blue

13. Cranial Nerves for Vision

The Mnemonic: II, III, IV, VI

II (Optic), III (Oculomotor), IV (Trochlear), VI (Abducens)

14. Extraocular Muscles

The Mnemonic: LR6 SO4, All Others 3

Lateral Rectus → CN VI | Superior Oblique → CN IV | All other extraocular muscles → CN III

15. Parts of the Ear

The Mnemonic: MOI

Middle ear, Outer ear, Inner ear

16. Ossicles of the Middle Ear

The Mnemonic: MIS

Malleus, Incus, Stapes

17. Functions of Ear

The Mnemonic: HB

Hearing, Balance

18. Inner Ear

The Mnemonic: CSV

Cochlea, Semicircular canals, Vestibule

19. Semicircular Canals

The Mnemonic: SAL

Superior (Anterior), A (Posterior - think "Another"), Lateral

20. Cranial Nerve for Hearing

The Mnemonic: 8 Hears

CN VIII = Vestibulocochlear nerve.

21. Parts of the Nose

The Mnemonic: NSP

Nostrils, Stratum (Nasal cavity), Paranasal sinuses

22. Paranasal Sinuses

The Mnemonic: FEMS

Frontal, Ethmoid, Maxillary, Sphenoid

23. Cranial Nerve for Smell

The Mnemonic: I Smell

CN I = Olfactory nerve.

24. Types of Lingual Papillae

The Mnemonic: FFCV

Filiform, Fungiform, Circumvallate, V (Foliate - think "V" for variation)

25. Taste Modalities

The Mnemonic: SSSBU

Sweet, Salty, Sour, Bitter, Umami

26. Cranial Nerves for Taste

The Mnemonic: 7, 9, 10 Taste

VII (Facial), IX (Glossopharyngeal), X (Vagus)

27. Layers of the Skin

The Mnemonic: EDH

Epidermis, Dermis, Hypodermis (Subcutaneous tissue)

28. Epidermal Layers

The Mnemonic: Come Let's Get Sun Burned

Stratum Corneum, Stratum Lucidum, Stratum Granulosum, Stratum Spinosum, Stratum Basale

29. Skin Receptors

The Mnemonic: MPRK

Meissner corpuscles (Light touch), Pacinian corpuscles (Pressure), Ruffini endings (Stretch), Krause end bulbs (Cold)

30. Common Disorders

The Mnemonic: CGOD

Cataract, Glaucoma, Otitis, Deafness
