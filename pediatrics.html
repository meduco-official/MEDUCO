Subject: Pediatrics

Master neonatal assessments, congenital syndromes, developmental milestones, and pediatric emergencies with these high-yield mnemonics.

Neonatology & APGAR

1. Newborn Assessment

🧠 The Mnemonic: APGAR

Appearance (color), Pulse (heart rate), Grimace (reflex irritability), Activity (muscle tone), Respiration (breathing).

2. Neonatal Jaundice Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: ARGENT

Anesth/drugs, Rh incompatibility, G6PD deficiency, Enclosed hemorrhage, Newborn hemolytic disease, TORCH infections.

3. Congenital Infections

🧠 The Mnemonic: TORCH

Toxoplasmosis, Other (Syphilis, Varicella, Parvovirus), Rubella, Cytomegalovirus (CMV), Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV).

4. Neonatal Sepsis Risks

🧠 The Mnemonic: NEONATE

Needle use (maternal IV), Early rupture of membranes, Obstetric complications, Non-sterile delivery, Amnotic fluid foul, Temperature spikes in mother, Empty prenatal care.

5. Neonatal Post-Resuscitation

🧠 The Mnemonic: STABLE

Sugar, Temperature, Airway, Blood pressure, Lab work, Emotional support.

6. Neonatal Hypoglycemia Risk

🧠 The Mnemonic: BABY

Berth weight extremes, Asphyxia, Baby of diabetic mother, Young gestational age.

7. Normal Transitional Newborn Breathing

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLEAR

Cry clears fluid, Lungs expand, Effort is regular, Air entry bilateral, Rate 40–60.

8. Umbilical Cord Structure

🧠 The Mnemonic: CORD

Connective tissue (Wharton's), One large vein, Right artery, Left artery.

9. VACTERL Association

🧠 The Mnemonic: VATER

Vertebral defects, Anal atresia, Cardiac defects, Tracheo-Esophageal fistula, Renal anomalies, Limb abnormalities.

10. Neonatal Resuscitation Steps

🧠 The Mnemonic: RESUS

Radiant warmer, Evaluate airway, Stimulate to cry, Use positive pressure ventilation, Start chest compressions.

11. Gastroschisis vs Omphalocele

🧠 The Mnemonic: GASTROS

Gastroschisis: Abdominal wall defect, Side of midline (Right), Tolerates no sac covering.
Omphalocele: Round, Sac covers herniation.

12. Meconium Aspiration Risks

🧠 The Mnemonic: MECONIUM

Maturity (post-term), Environmental hypoxia, Cord compression, Obstruction of small airways, Newborn respiratory distress, Inflammatory pneumonitis, Underviewed patchy infiltrates, Milky/green fluid.

13. Breastmilk vs Formula Jaundice

🧠 The Mnemonic: MILK

Milk insufficiency (Breastfeeding jaundice, week 1), Increased enterohepatic circulation, Late-onset glucuronosyltransferase inhibition (Breast milk jaundice, week 2), Keep monitoring bilirubin.

Growth & Development

14. Stranger Anxiety

🧠 The Mnemonic: STRANGER

Starts at Twelve weeks (approx. 6-9 months clinically), Reacts to Aliens (unfamiliar faces), Normal developmental Growth, Ends Rapidly around age two.

15. Developmental Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: PALS

Person-Social, Adaptive/Fine Motor, Language, Social/Gross Motor.

16. Signs of Autism

🧠 The Mnemonic: WATCH

Words are delayed, Attention to joint objects missing, Turning when name called is absent, Cures/clues from faces ignored, Hand flapping.

17. 2-Month Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: SMILE

Social smile, Moves head side-to-side, Imitates some sounds, Lifts head on tummy, Eyes follow objects.

18. 6-Month Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: SIT

Sits with support, Interacts with mirror, Transfers objects hand-to-hand.

19. 18-Month Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLIMB

Climbs stairs, Loves stacking blocks, Imitates housework, Makes 10+ words, Bowl/spoon usage starts.

20. 3-Year Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: PLAY

Pedal tricycle, Language (3-word sentences), Associative play, Years of age equals circle drawing.

21. Developmental Delay Red Flags

🧠 The Mnemonic: STEP

Speech missing by 18 months, Tonic neck reflex past 6 months, Eye contact absent, Poor head control at 4 months.

22. 12-Month Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: TALK

Twostep command understanding, Attempts single words, Leans forward to wave goodbye, Knows its own name.

23. 4-Year Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: DRAW

Draws a cross/square, Rehearses stories, Aops over obstacles, Working with other children (cooperative play).

24. 5-Year Milestones

🧠 The Mnemonic: WRITE

Writes some letters, Recogizes colors, Independent dressing, Triangle drawing, Excellent balance on one foot.

25. Primary Tooth Eruption Order

🧠 The Mnemonic: TEETH

Two central incisors, Edge lateral incisors, Erupid first molars, Toppling canines, Hind second molars.

26. Male Sexual Maturity Order

🧠 The Mnemonic: PUBERTY

Pelvic testicular enlargement, Under-pubic hair development, Batting penile growth, Extension of height spurt, Ready axillary hair, Thickening voice.

Cardiology & Respiratory

27. Tetralogy of Fallot

🧠 The Mnemonic: PROVe

Pulmonary stenosis, Right ventricular hypertrophy, Overriding aorta, Ventricular septal defect.

28. Cyanotic Heart Defects (5 Ts)

🧠 The Mnemonic: HEAL

1 Transposition, 2 Tetralogy, 3 Truncus, 4 Total anomalous pulmonary venous return, 5 Tricuspid atresia.

29. Epiglottitis Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: ALARM

Airway inflammation, Look for drooling, Anxiety/stridor, Respiratory distress, Mouth open (tripod position).

30. Rheumatic Heart Disease Valve Order

🧠 The Mnemonic: HEART

Highest risk Mitral, Every second Aortic, Almost never Tricuspid, Rarest Pulmonary.

31. Acyanotic Heart Defects

🧠 The Mnemonic: SHUNT

Septal defect (ASD), Heart volume overload, Unoxygenated blood stays right, Normal saturation, Total shunt Left-to-Right.

32. PDA Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: DUCT

Down syndrome, Utero rubella infection, Congental prematurity, Toppling birth weight.

33. Tet Spell Compensation

🧠 The Mnemonic: SQUAT

Systemic vascular resistance increases, Quenches right-to-left shunt, Upgrades pulmonary blood flow, Alleviates hypoxia, Turns pink.

34. Coarctation of Aorta

🧠 The Mnemonic: PULSE

Pressure high in arms, Under-perfused lower extremities, Leg pulses weak, Systolic murmur, Ejection click.

35. 5 Cyanotic Heart Congenital Lesions

🧠 The Mnemonic: CYANOSIS

Cone truncus arteriosus, Yoked transposition, Atresia of tricuspid, Narrowed tetralogy of Fallot, Obvious total anomalous pulmonary venous connection.

36. Benign/Innocent Murmur Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: MURMUR

Musical quality, Unchanged pulses, Radiation absent, Maximal at left sternal border, Upright position decreases intensity, Regular soft grading (I-II/VI).

Infectious & Rheumatic Diseases

37. Kawasaki Disease

🧠 The Mnemonic: CRASH and Burn

Conjunctivitis, Rash, Adenopathy (cervical), Strawberry tongue, Hand/foot changes, and Burn (high fever for 5+ days).

38. Kawasaki Disease Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: KAPPA

Koronary (Coronary) artery aneurysms, Acute myocardial infarction, Pericardial effusion, Polyarthritis, Arrythmias.

39. Acute Rheumatic Fever - Jones Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: FEVER

Fever, Erythema marginatum, Valvular involvement (Carditis), Elevated ESR/CRP, Reversible polyarthritis.

40. Jones Major Criteria Extensions

🧠 The Mnemonic: RHEUM

Reversible polyarthritis, Heart carditis, Erythema marginatum, Under-skin subcutaneous nodules, Movements of Sydenham chorea.

Neurology & Genetics

41. Infantile Spasms / West Syndrome

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPASM

Sudden jerks, Peak onset at 4-7 months, Activity on EEG is Hypsarrhythmia, Spasm types (flexor/extensor), Mental retardation risk.

42. Down Syndrome Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: DOWN

Decreased muscle tone, Oblique palpebral fissures (slanted eyes), Wide gap between 1st and 2nd toes, Neck skin folding.

43. Neurofibromatosis Type 1

🧠 The Mnemonic: CAFE

Café-au-lait spots, Axillary freckling, Fibromas (neurofibromas), Eye Lisch nodules.

44. Hydrocephalus Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: HYDRO

Head circumference rising, Young fontanelle bulging, Dilated scalp veins, Reflexes hyperactive, Ocular setting-sun sign.

45. Cerebral Palsy Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: CEREBRAL

Corioamnionitis, Encephalopathy, Radiation, Extremely low birth weight, Brain malformation, Rh disease, Asphyxia, Labor prolonged.

46. Febrile Seizure Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: SEIZURE

Spikes with fever, Excludes CNS infection, Interval under 15 minutes (simple), Zero prior unprovoked seizures, Under 5 years old, Rapid recovery, Essentially benign.

47. Microcephaly Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: HEAD

Hereditary, Exposure to toxins, Alcohol (FAS), Domestic TORCH infections.

48. Abusive Head Trauma / SBS

🧠 The Mnemonic: SHAKE

Subdural hematoma, Hemorrhages in retina, Axonal shear injury, Known rib fractures, Encephalopathy.

GI, Renal & Metabolic

49. Pyloric Stenosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: MUSH

Male predominance, Undiscovered (presents at 3-6 weeks), Secondary metabolic alkalosis, Hard "olive" mass in abdomen.

50. Dehydration Assessment

🧠 The Mnemonic: TEARS

Tears absent, Energy low (lethargy), Anterior fontanelle sunken, Radial pulse weak, Skin turgor decreased.

51. Pediatric Fluid Resuscitation

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDE

Assess weight, Bolus (20 mL/kg NS), Calculate maintenance, Deficit calculation, Electrolyte replacement.

52. Intussusception

🧠 The Mnemonic: INTUSS

Intermittent colicky pain, Neonate/infant age, Target sign on ultrasound, Underlying Meckel's lead point, Sausage-shaped mass, Std currant-jelly stool.

53. Celiac Disease Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: CELIAC

Chronic diarrhea, Emaciated extremities, Loss of weight, IgA tissue transglutaminase positive, Attrophied villi, Crusting dermatitis herpetiformis.

54. Phenylketonuria Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: PKU

Phenylalanine hydroxylase deficiency, Ketonuria (phenylpyruvate), Unique musty/mousy odor, blond hair, blue eyes, intellectual disability.

Hematology & Oncology

55. Sickle Cell Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: SICKLE

Splenic sequestration, Infections (encapsulated), Crisis (vaso-occlusive), Kidney infarcts, Lung syndrome (acute chest), Erased bone marrow (aplastic crisis).

56. Acute Lymphoblastic Leukemia

🧠 The Mnemonic: BLAST

Bone pain, Lymphadenopathy, Anemia, Splenomegaly, Thrombocytopenia.

57. Neuroblastoma Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: MASS

Midline crossing tumor, Adrenal medulla origin, Sympathetic chain, Secreting catecholamines (VMA/HVA).

58. Wilms Tumor

🧠 The Mnemonic: WILMS

Without crossing midline, Iris absence (Aniridia association), Lump in flank, Maturity peak 3-4 years, Surgically resectable.

Emergency & Safety

59. Pediatric Assessment Triangle

🧠 The Mnemonic: TICLS

Tone, Irritability, Consolability, Look/Gaze, Speech/Cry.

60. Accidental Poisoning Risks

🧠 The Mnemonic: CHILD

Curious age group, Household chemicals accessible, Improper storage, Lookalike packaging (candy/juice), Dose toxicity threshold is low.
