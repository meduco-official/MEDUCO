Chapter 9: Digestive System

The Digestive System is responsible for ingestion, digestion, absorption of nutrients, and elimination of waste.

1. Functions of the Digestive System

The Mnemonic: IDAE

Ingestion, Digestion, Absorption, Elimination

2. Parts of the Alimentary Canal

The Mnemonic: Never Eat Old People, They Can Make Bad Food

Nose/Mouth (Oral cavity), Esophagus, Old (Stomach), P (Small intestine), They (Large intestine/Colon), Can (Rectum), Make (Anal canal), Bad (Anus), Food (Food exits)

3. Accessory Digestive Organs

The Mnemonic: SLPG

Salivary glands, Liver, Pancreas, Gallbladder

4. Salivary Glands

The Mnemonic: Please Sub My Dinner

Parotid, Submandibular, M (Sublingual)

5. Layers of the Alimentary Canal

The Mnemonic: MSMS

Mucosa, Submucosa, Muscularis externa, Serosa (or Adventitia)

6. Parts of the Pharynx

The Mnemonic: Never Offer Lunch

Nasopharynx, Oropharynx, Laryngopharynx

7. Parts of the Stomach

The Mnemonic: CFBP

Cardia, Fundus, Body, Pylorus

8. Cells of the Stomach

The Mnemonic: MCCG

Mucous cells, Chief cells, C (Parietal - think "Covering" acid cells), G cells

9. Gastric Secretions

The Mnemonic: HAM

Hydrochloric acid (HCl), Antrinsic (Intrinsic factor - Aid for B₁₂ absorption), Mucus

10. Small Intestine Parts

The Mnemonic: DJI

Duodenum, Jejunum, Ileum

11. Large Intestine Parts

The Mnemonic: CCSATRA

Cecum, C (Ascending colon), S (Transverse colon), A (Descending colon), T (Sigmoid colon), Rectum, Anal canal

12. Liver Lobes

The Mnemonic: RLQC

Right lobe, Left lobe, Quadrate lobe, Caudate lobe

13. Functions of the Liver

The Mnemonic: BDMDS

Bile production, Detoxification, Metabolism, D (Storage - glycogen, vitamins), S (Plasma protein synthesis)

14. Functions of Bile

The Mnemonic: FEE

Fat emulsification, Excretion of bilirubin, E (Fat absorption helps)

15. Gallbladder Function

The Mnemonic: SCC

Stores bile, Concentrates bile, Contracts to release bile

16. Pancreas Functions

The Mnemonic: DEP

Digestive enzymes, Endocrine hormones, Pancreatic bicarbonate

17. Pancreatic Enzymes

The Mnemonic: LAP

Lipase, Amylase, Proteases (Trypsin, Chymotrypsin)

18. Brush Border Enzymes

The Mnemonic: LIMS

Lactase, Isomaltase, Maltase, Sucrase

19. Functions of the Small Intestine

The Mnemonic: DAM

Digestion, Absorption, Motility

20. Parts of a Tooth

The Mnemonic: CREP

Crown, Root, Enamel, Pulp

21. Layers of a Tooth

The Mnemonic: EDCP

Enamel, Dentin, Cementum, Pulp

22. Functions of Saliva

The Mnemonic: LAMS

Lubrication, Amylase digestion, Moistening food, Speech & swallowing

23. Portal Vein Formation

The Mnemonic: SM + Splenic = Portal

Superior Mesenteric Vein + Splenic Vein

24. Blood Supply of the Gut

The Mnemonic: CSI

Celiac trunk, Superior mesenteric artery, Inferior mesenteric artery

25. Peritoneum

The Mnemonic: PV

Parietal peritoneum, Visceral peritoneum

26. Retroperitoneal Organs

The Mnemonic: SAD PUCKER

Suprarenal glands, Aorta/IVC, Duodenum (2nd–4th parts), Pancreas (except tail), Ureters, Colon (ascending & descending), Kidneys, Esophagus (abdominal part), Rectum (partly)

27. Intraperitoneal Organs

The Mnemonic: SLIP

Stomach, Liver, Ileum/Jejunum, Pspleen (Also includes transverse & sigmoid colon, appendix, tail of pancreas.)

28. Major Digestive Hormones

The Mnemonic: GCCS

Gastrin, Cholecystokinin (CCK), C (Secretin), Somatostatin

29. Clinical Conditions

The Mnemonic: GAPH

Gastritis, Appendicitis, Peptic ulcer, Hepatitis

30. Digestive Tract Order

The Mnemonic: MESPSCRA

Mouth, Esophagus, Stomach, P (Small intestine), S (Large intestine/Colon), C (Rectum), R (Anal canal), Anus
