🦠 1. Bacterial Morphology, Staining & Classification

1. Spirochetes (Spiral-shaped Bacteria)

🧠 The Mnemonic: BLT

Borrelia (Lyme disease) | Leptospira (Leptospirosis / Weil's disease) | Treponema (Syphilis)

2. Spore-Forming Gram-Positive Rods

🧠 The Mnemonic: BC (Before Christ)

Spores survive for ages.
Bacillus (B. anthracis, B. cereus) | Clostridium (C. tetani, C. botulinum, C. difficile, C. perfringens)

3. Filamentous/Branching Gram-Positive Rods

🧠 The Mnemonic: SNAPS

🦠 5. Gram-Negative Pathogens (Specifics)

21. Pseudomonas aeruginosa Virulence & Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: AERUGINOSA

Aerobic | Exotoxin A | Rod shape | UTI risk | Green-blue pigments (Pyocyanin) | Iron-regulated virulence | Non-lactose fermenter | Oxidase-positive | Slime layer (Biofilm) | Aminoglycosides treat it

22. Haemophilus influenzae Culture Demands

🧠 The Mnemonic: Needs Factors 5 and 10

Factor V (NAD) and Factor X (Hematin). Must be grown on Chocolate Agar (lysed RBCs).

23. Salmonella vs. Shigella Differentiation

🧠 The Mnemonic: Salmonella swims, Shigella sits

Swims – Salmonella is motile and produces H₂S gas. Sits – Shigella is non-motile and does not produce H₂S.
