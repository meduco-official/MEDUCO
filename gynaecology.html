Subject: Gynaecology & Obstetrics

Master maternal-fetal medicine, labor complications, gynecological oncology, and endocrine reproductive disorders with these 100 definitive mnemonics.

Antenatal & Diagnostic Foundations

1. HELLP Syndrome Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: HELLP

Hemolysis, Elevated Liver enzymes, Low Platelets.

2. Shoulder Dystocia Management

🧠 The Mnemonic: ALARMER

Ask for help, Leg flexion (McRoberts manoeuvre), Anterior shoulder disimpaction (Suprapubic pressure), Rubin / Woods Screw manoeuvre, Manual delivery of posterior arm, Episiotomy, Roll onto all fours (Gaskin manoeuvre).

3. CTG Interpretation

🧠 The Mnemonic: DR C BRAVADO

Define Risk, Contractions, Baseline Rate, Variability, Accelerations, Decelerations, Overall assessment / Outcome.

4. CTG Decelerations & Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: VEAL CHOP

Variable → Cord compression
Early → Head compression
Accelerations → Oxygenated / Ok
Late → Placental insufficiency

5. Postpartum Haemorrhage Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: 4 Ts

Tone (Uterine atony - most common), Tissue (Retained placenta), Trauma (Lacerations, uterine rupture), Thrombin (Coagulopathy).

6. Antenatal History Taking

🧠 The Mnemonic: ABCDEF

Antenatal care / Attendance, Bleeding / Movements, Contractions, Discharge / Dribbling, Energy (Fetal movements), Fever or chills.

7. Obstetric History Record

🧠 The Mnemonic: GTPAL

Gravida (Total pregnancies), Term births (≥ 37 weeks), Preterm births (20 to 36 weeks), Abortions / Miscarriages (< 20 weeks), Living children.

8. Pre-eclampsia Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: PRE-ECLAMPSIA

Proteinuria, Reflexes hyperactive, Edema, Elevated blood pressure, Calcium low in urine, Liver enzymes elevated, Arterial spasms, Middle-aged, Patellar clonus, Severe headache, Ill-defined vision, Aggravated epigastric pain.

9. Congenital Infections

🧠 The Mnemonic: TORCH

Toxoplasmosis, Other (Syphilis, Varicella, Parvovirus B19), Rubella, Cytomegalovirus (CMV), Herpes Simplex Virus (HSV).

10. APGAR Score Components

🧠 The Mnemonic: NEWBORN

Number of heartbeats, Effort of breathing, Well-flexed (Muscle tone), Blueness absent, Outcry (Reflex irritability), Response to stimulation, Normal vigor.

Core Gynaecology

11. Abnormal Uterine Bleeding Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: PALM-COEIN

Polyp, Adenomyosis, Leiomyoma (Fibroids), Malignancy / Hyperplasia, Coagulopathy, Ovulatory dysfunction, Endometrial, Iatrogenic, Not yet classified.

12. Polycystic Ovary Syndrome - PCOS Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: OH MY GOD

Ovulatory dysfunction (Oligo/Anovulation), Hyperandrogenism (Clinical or biochemical), Morphology on ultrasound (Polycystic ovaries). (Note: Needs 2 out of 3 for Rotterdam Criteria)

13. Pelvic Inflammatory Disease Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: PID

Promiscuity (Multiple partners), IUD insertion (Recent), Douche usage.

14. FIGO Staging System Core Layers

🧠 The Mnemonic: Stages I - IV

Stage I: Confined to the Inside organ.
Stage II: Into surrounding pelvic tissues.
Stage III: Into the peritoneal cavity / Lymph nodes.
Stage IV: Invasive distant metastasis.

15. Contraindications to Combined Oral Contraceptive Pill

🧠 The Mnemonic: 4 Cs

Coagulation disorders (DVT/PE history), Cardiovascular disease (Stroke, severe HTN), Cancer (Estrogen-dependent), Cirrhosis / Liver disease.

16. Severe Complications of OCPs

🧠 The Mnemonic: ACHES

Abdominal pain (Severe), Chest pain / Shortness of breath, Headaches (Severe, neurologic), Eye problems (Blurred vision), Severe leg pain (DVT).

17. Endometriosis Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: MUD

Menorrhagia, Urinary/Bowel symptoms (Dysuria/Dyschezia), Dysmenorrhea / Dyspareunia.

18. Breaking Bad News Protocol

🧠 The Mnemonic: SPIKES

Setting up the interview, Perception of the patient, Invitation to share info, Knowledge giving, Emotions / Empathy, Strategy & Summary.

19. Secondary Amenorrhea Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: AIME

Anatomical (Asherman syndrome), Iatrogenic (Chemotherapy), Metabolic / Endocrine (PCOS, Thyroid), Early menopause (Premature Ovarian Insufficiency).

20. Vulvar Pruritus Differential

🧠 The Mnemonic: FIT

Fungal (Candidiasis), Inflammatory (Lichen sclerosus, eczema), Tumor (Vulvar Intraepithelial Neoplasia / Carcinoma).

Pregnancy Risks & Complications

21. Vasa Previa Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: VIP

Velamentous cord insertion, IVF pregnancy, Placenta previa or succenturiate lobe.

22. Spontaneous Abortion Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAD

Smoking or substance abuse, Age (Advanced maternal age), Disease (Uncontrolled diabetes, thyroid, or autoimmune).

23. Ectopic Pregnancy Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: ECTOPIC

Endometriosis, Chlamydia / PID history, Tubal surgery, Ovulation induction (IVF), Prior ectopic, Intrauterine device (IUD) failure, Cigarette smoking.

24. Hydatidiform Mole Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: H-MOLE

Hyperemesis gravidarum, Malignancy (Choriocarcinoma risk), Ovarian theca lutein cysts, Large uterus for gestational age, Early-onset pre-eclampsia.

25. Maternal Obesity Pregnancy Risks

🧠 The Mnemonic: FAT

Fetal macrosomia, Anomalies (Neural tube defects), Thrombosis / Thromboembolism.

26. Causes of Polyhydramnios

🧠 The Mnemonic: CUP

Congenital anomalies, Unknown (Idiopathic), Plural (Multiple) gestations / Placental chorioangioma.

27. Causes of Oligohydramnios

🧠 The Mnemonic: DRIP

Demise (Fetal death), Renal agenesis / ROM (Rupture of membranes), IUGR, Post-term pregnancy / Placental insufficiency.

28. Absolute Contraindications to VBAC / TOLAC

🧠 The Mnemonic: BAD

Baseline classical uterine incision (Vertical), Anatomy deformed (Prior uterine rupture), Disproportion (Cephalopelvic disproportion).

29. Indications for Anti-D Prophylaxis in Rh-ve Mothers

🧠 The Mnemonic: RAPID

Routine administration (at 28 weeks), Abortion, Procedures (Amniocentesis/CVS), Injury (Abdominal trauma), Delivery of an Rh-positive baby.

Medical Disorders in Pregnancy

30. Gestational Diabetes Maternal Risks

🧠 The Mnemonic: DIABETES

Damage to kidneys, Infections, Amniotic fluid excess, Blood pressure rise, Emergency C-section, Tear of perineum, Early delivery, Stillbirth risk.

31. Infant of Diabetic Mother - Neonatal Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: SWEET

Sugar low (Neonatal hypoglycemia), Weight high (Macrosomia), Erythroblastosis / Polycythemia, Early delivery respiratory distress, Tiny calcium (Hypocalcemia).

32. Antiphospholipid Syndrome - APS Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLOT

Coagulation (thrombosis), Late pregnancy loss (>10 weeks), Offspring premature (<34 weeks due to pre-eclampsia), Three or more consecutive early pregnancy losses (<10 weeks).

33. Risk Factors for Gestational Hypertension

🧠 The Mnemonic: HIRE

History of pre-eclampsia, Inter-pregnancy interval long (>10 years), Renal disease / Chronic hypertension, Egg donation / ART.

34. Severe Features of Pre-eclampsia

🧠 The Mnemonic: FAST

Fetal growth restriction, AST/ALT elevation with epigastric pain, Severe headache or visual disturbances, Thrombocytopenia.

35. Magnesium Sulfate Toxicity Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: TOXIC

Tiredness, Output of urine low, 'X'ed out reflexes, Inhibited respirations, Cardiac arrest.

36. Magnesium Toxicity Management

🧠 The Mnemonic: CALM

Calcium gluconate (the antidote), Arrest MgSO4 infusion immediately, Low-flow or high-flow oxygen support, Monitor urine output and vitals.

37. Causes of Non-Immune Hydrops Fetalis

🧠 The Mnemonic: SICK

Structural heart defects, Infections (Parvovirus B19, CMV), Chromosomal abnormalities, Karyotype/Genetic metabolic errors.

Labor, Delivery & Postpartum

38. The Components of Labor Progression

🧠 The Mnemonic: 3 Ps

Power (Uterine contractions), Passenger (Fetal size/presentation), Passage (Maternal bony pelvis).

39. 7 Cardinal Movements of Labor

🧠 The Mnemonic: Every Darn Fool In Egypt Eats Raw Eggs

Engagement, Descent, Flexion, Internal rotation, Extension, External rotation (Restitution), Expulsion.

40. Umbilical Cord Prolapse Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: CORD

Customary malpresentation, Outflow/Polyhydramnios, ROM artificial, Deliveries that are multiple.

41. Indications for Operative Vaginal Delivery

🧠 The Mnemonic: BLAST

Baby in distress, Labor prolonged, Ablation of maternal pushing needed, Stationary head, Tired mother.

42. Prerequisites for Forceps Application

🧠 The Mnemonic: FORCEPS

Fully dilated cervix, Occiput/Head engaged, Ruptured membranes, Cephalopelvic disproportion ruled out, Empty bladder, Position of head known, Suitable anesthesia.

43. Types of Breech Presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: FCF

Frank breech (Hips flexed, knees extended), Complete breech (Hips flexed, knees flexed), Footling breech (One or both feet presenting first).

44. Medical Management of Postpartum Haemorrhage (PPH)

🧠 The Mnemonic: PINTS

Pitocin (Oxytocin), Intrauterine balloon, Next-gen prostaglandins (Carboprost), Tranexamic acid (TXA), Syntometrine or Misoprostol.

45. Causes of Puerperal Pyrexia

🧠 The Mnemonic: FEVER

Flesh (Endometritis), Embolism (DVT / PE), Voiding (UTI), Expressed milk (Mastitis), Respiration (Atelectasis/pneumonia).

Gynaecology Oncology

46. Endometrial Cancer Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: ENDOMET

Estrogen unopposed, Nulliparity, Diabetes mellitus, Obesity, Menopause late / Menarche early, Elderly, Tamoxifen use.

47. Causes of Elevated CA-125 (Non-Malignant)

🧠 The Mnemonic: CA-125

Cysts of ovary, Adenomyosis / Endometriosis, 1st trimester pregnancy, 2: Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID), 5: Fibroids.

48. Cervical Cancer Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: CERVIX

Coitus at an early age, Early childbearing, Risk-taking sexual behavior, Viral infection (HPV 16, 18), Immunosuppression (HIV), Xerosis / Smoking.

49. Ovarian Cancer Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: BRCA

Breast cancer history, Reproduction low (Nulliparity), Celibacy / Infertility, Advanced age.

50. Factors that Reduce Ovarian Cancer Risk

🧠 The Mnemonic: PROTECT

Pill use (Combined Oral Contraceptive Pill), Removal of tubes/ovaries, Offspring (Pregnancy), Total breastfeeding duration.

Menstrual Disorders & Endocrine

51. Primary Amenorrhea Evaluation Steps

🧠 The Mnemonic: UKF

Ultrasound for Uterus, Karyotype, FSH levels.

52. Asherman Syndrome Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-S-H

Amenorrhea / Hypomenorrhea, Surgical history (Prior D&C), Hysteroscopy diagnosis (Intrauterine adhesions).

53. Premenstrual Syndrome Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: PMS

Physical symptoms, Mood swings / Irritability, Symptom-free interval (Must clear up during follicular phase).

54. Menopausal Vasomotor Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: HOT

Hot flushes, Occasional night sweats, Tachycardia / Palpitations.

55. Genitourinary Syndrome of Menopause (GSM)

🧠 The Mnemonic: ATROPHY

Acid decrease (pH > 4.5), Thinning of epithelium, Recurrent UTIs, Outer dryness, Painful intercourse.

Contraception & Infections

56. Contraindications for Intrauterine Device Insertion

🧠 The Mnemonic: IUD-S

Infection current (PID/STI), Uterine anomaly, Disturbed bleeding, Suspected pregnancy.

57. Progesterone-Only Pill Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: POP

Period irregularity (Spotting - most common), Ovarian cysts, Pimples.

58. Bacterial Vaginosis - Amsel's Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: CLUE

Clue cells, Liquid discharge (Thin, white-grey), Unpleasant fishy odor, Elevated pH (> 4.5).

59. Bacterial Vaginosis Treatment

🧠 The Mnemonic: FISHY

Flagyl (Metronidazole), Intravaginal clindamycin alternative.

60. Trichomoniasis Key Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: STRAWBERRY

Strawberry cervix, Trichomonads motile, Rank green-yellow frothy discharge.

61. Chlamydia Complications

🧠 The Mnemonic: CHEAP

Chronic pelvic pain, Fitz-Hugh-Curtis syndrome, Ectopic pregnancy risk, Abortions / Preterm labor, Pelvic Inflammatory Disease (PID).

Urogynaecology & Pelvic Floor

62. Stress Urinary Incontinence Causes

🧠 The Mnemonic: STRESS

Sphincter weakness, Trauma from childbirth, Radiation/surgery history, Estrogen deficiency, Support loss.

63. Urge Urinary Incontinence Overactive Bladder

🧠 The Mnemonic: URGE

Uninhibited detrusor contractions, Rushing to the toilet, Geriatric association, Exclude UTI first.

64. Pelvic Organ Prolapse Quantification Stages

🧠 The Mnemonic: POP-Q

Stage 0: No prolapse.
Stage 1: > 1 cm above hymen.
Stage 2: Between 1 cm above and 1 cm below hymen.
Stage 3: > 1 cm below hymen.
Stage 4: Complete eversion.

Pelvic Masses & Infertility

65. Uterine Leiomyoma Symptoms

🧠 The Mnemonic: FIBROID

Frequency of urination, Infertility, Bleeding heavy, Reproductive losses, Obvious palpable abdominal mass, Iron deficiency anemia, Dysmenorrhea / Pelvic pain.

66. Male Infertility Basic Semen Analysis Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-A-M

Sperm count / Concentration, Anatomy / Morphology, Motility.

67. Tests to Confirm Ovulation

🧠 The Mnemonic: OVULATE

Oh-so-clear Day 21 Progesterone level, Vaginal/Cervical mucus thinning, Ultrasound monitoring, LH surge on urine test kit.

68. Ovarian Hyperstimulation Syndrome Presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: OHSS

Oliguria, Hemoconcentration, Size of ovaries massively increased, Stomach pain.

69. Acute Pelvic Pain Differentials

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-A-I-N

PID / Abscess, Appendicitis, Ischemia (Ovarian torsion / Fibroid degeneration), Normal pregnancy variant or Ectopic.

70. Surgical Checklist for Gynaecological Laparoscopy

🧠 The Mnemonic: SAFE

Stomach/Bowel decompression, Access entry confirmation, Fluid deficit monitoring, Embolism prevention.

Advanced Obstetrics

71. Preterm Labor Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-A-R-T-U-S

Prior history of preterm birth, Amniotic fluid infection, Rupture of membranes, Tobacco/alcohol/substance abuse, Uterine anomalies, Short cervical length.

72. Indications for Induction of Labor

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-P-U-R

Severe pre-eclampsia/gestational hypertension, Post-dates pregnancy, Uterine infection, Restrictions in fetal growth.

73. Bishop Score Criteria for Cervical Ripeness

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-I-S-H-O-P

Baseline Position, Integrity/Consistency, Station of the fetal head, Heightened Cervical Effacement, Opening / Dilation.

74. Mechanical Methods of Cervical Ripening

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-E-C-H

Midline Cook Balloon, Extra-amniotic saline infusion, Cervical Foley catheter insertion, Hygroscopic dilators.

75. Oxytocin/Syntocinon Side Effects

🧠 The Mnemonic: O-X-Y-T-O-C-I-N

Overstimulation, 'X'-tremely low blood pressure, Yielding to water intoxication, Tachycardia, Oxygen deprivation to the fetus, Cerebral edema, Injury / Uterine rupture, Neonatal hyperbilirubinemia.

76. Causes of Fetal Bradycardia

🧠 The Mnemonic: F-E-T-A-L

Failure of placenta, Epidural/spinal anesthesia, Tetanic uterine contractions, Abruptio placentae, Long cord compression/prolapse.

77. Prerequisites for Ventouse / Vacuum Delivery

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-A-F-E-T-Y

Scalp visible, Anesthesia adequate, Fully dilated cervix, Empty bladder, Term gestation (>34 weeks), Your consent.

78. Contraindications to Ventouse Delivery

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-A-B-Y

Breech or face presentation, Age preterm (<34 weeks), Bleeding diathesis or fetal coagulopathy, Your inability to define the fetal position.

79. Risk Factors for Fetal Macrosomia

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-A-C-R-O

Maternal obesity, Advanced maternal age, Conceived post-dates, Raised maternal glucose, Overweight partner / Genetics.

80. Complications of Multiple Gestation

🧠 The Mnemonic: T-W-I-N-S

Twin-to-Twin Transfusion Syndrome, Weight discordance, Increased risk of PPH and pre-eclampsia, Non-cephalic presentation, Spontaneous preterm labor.

81. Twin-to-Twin Transfusion Syndrome Quintero Staging

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-T-A-G-E

Stage I: Oligo/Polyhydramnios sequence.
Stage II: Bladder of donor is not visible.
Stage III: Abnormal Doppler studies.
Stage IV: Hydrops fetalis.
Stage V: Exitus (Death).

82. Causes of Antepartum Haemorrhage - APH

🧠 The Mnemonic: B-L-E-E-D

Borderline low-lying placenta (Placenta previa), Local cervical lesions, Early separation (Abruptio placentae), Extra-fetal vessels (Vasa previa), Determinant unknown.

83. Risk Factors for Placenta Previa

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-R-E-V-I-A

Prior C-section, Raised maternal age, Excess placenta (Multiple gestations), Varying parity, Induced abortion history, Addiction to smoking.

84. Risk Factors for Placental Abruption

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-B-R-U-P-T

Age advanced, Blood pressure high, Rupture of membranes sudden, Use of cocaine/tobacco, Prior history of abruption, Trauma.

85. Placenta Accreta Spectrum Core Depth Layers

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-I-P

Accreta (Attaches to myometrium), Increta (Invades myometrium), Percreta (Penetrates through myometrium into serosa/bladder).

86. Uterine Rupture Risk Factors

🧠 The Mnemonic: U-T-E-R-U-S

Uterine scar, Trauma, Excessive oxytocin usage, Raised parity, Unengaged head with obstructed labor, Shoulder dystocia maneuvers aggressive.

87. Amniotic Fluid Embolism Clinical Triad

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-F-E

Acute hypoxia / Respiratory failure, Failure of hemodynamic stability, Ensuing DIC.

88. Sheehan Syndrome Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: S-H-E-E-H-A-N

Severe PPH history, Hypotension / Shock, Expanded pituitary necrosis, Excretory milk failure, Hormonal collapse, Amenorrhea, No pubic/axillary hair regrowth.

89. Chorioamnionitis Diagnostic Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-H-O-R-I-O

Cervical purulent fluid, High maternal HR, Offspring tachycardia, Raised maternal temperature, Increased WBCs, Odor foul.

90. Postpartum Depression vs. Blues vs. Psychosis

🧠 The Mnemonic: P-P-D

Blues: <2 weeks, Benign.
Depression: Despair/Anhedonia (SSRIs).
Psychosis: Paranoia, hallucinations (Emergency admission).

Advanced Gynaecology & Infectious

91. Causes of Hyperprolactinemia

🧠 The Mnemonic: H-Y-P-E-R

Hypothyroidism, Your medications, Pituitary adenoma, Extraneous chest wall stimulation, Renal failure.

92. Adenomyosis Key Clinical Signs

🧠 The Mnemonic: A-D-E-N-O

Age 35-50 multiparous, Diffusely enlarged globular uterus, Endometrial tissue within myometrium, Tender soft 'boggy' uterus.

93. Ovarian Torsion Presentation

🧠 The Mnemonic: O-V-A-R-Y

Onset sudden sharp pain, Vomiting/nausea, Adnexal mass, Restricted blood flow on Doppler, Yields emergency laparoscopy.

94. PID CDC Treatment Criteria

🧠 The Mnemonic: C-U-A

Cervical motion tenderness, Uterine tenderness, Adnexal tenderness. (Only 1 of 3 required).

95. Müllerian Agenesis vs. AIS Differentials

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-U-L-L-E-R

Müllerian Agenesis: Maternal chromosomes (46, XX), internal organs missing, Malformed kidneys.
Androgen Insensitivity: Androgen receptors broken, Androgen high (XY), Absent body hair.

96. Kallmann Syndrome Features

🧠 The Mnemonic: K-A-L-L-M-A-N-N

Karyotype 46 XX or XY, Anosmia, Low FSH/LH, Midline defects (Cleft palate).

97. Lichen Sclerosus Characteristics

🧠 The Mnemonic: L-I-C-H-E-N

Loss of vulvar architecture, Intense pruritus, Cigarette-paper skin, Hourglass distribution, Epithelial cancer risk, Narcotics/Steroids for treatment.

98. Medical Eligibility Criteria Categories for Contraception

🧠 The Mnemonic: U-K-M-E-C

1: No restriction.
2: Advantages outweigh risks.
3: Risks outweigh advantages.
4: Unacceptable health risk.

99. Emergency Contraception Window Options

🧠 The Mnemonic: E-C-P

Ulipristal acetate (up to 5 days), Levonorgestrel (up to 3 days), Copper IUD (up to 5 days - most effective).

100. Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) Rules

🧠 The Mnemonic: M-E-N-O-P-A-U-S-E

Unopposed estrogen causes endometrial hyperplasia, Progestin must be added if uterus present, Estrogen-only HRT is safe only if hysterectomy performed.
