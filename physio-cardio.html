Chapter 7: Cardiovascular System

The Cardiovascular System consists of the heart, blood vessels, and blood, responsible for transporting oxygen, nutrients, hormones, and waste products throughout the body.

1. Components of the Cardiovascular System

The Mnemonic: HBV

Heart, Blood, Blood Vessels

2. Functions of the Cardiovascular System

The Mnemonic: TOPIC

Transport, Oxygen delivery, Protection (immune cells & clotting), Internal homeostasis, Carbon dioxide & waste removal

3. Layers of the Heart Wall

The Mnemonic: EME

Epicardium, Myocardium, Endocardium

4. Coverings of the Heart

The Mnemonic: FPP

Fibrous pericardium, Parietal serous pericardium, Pisceral (Visceral) serous pericardium (Epicardium)

5. Chambers of the Heart

The Mnemonic: Right Before Left

Right Atrium → Right Ventricle → Left Atrium → Left Ventricle

6. Heart Valves

The Mnemonic: Try Pulling My Aorta

Tricuspid valve, Pulmonary valve, Mitral valve, Aortic valve

7. Atrioventricular (AV) Valves

The Mnemonic: TRI Before BI

Tricuspid → Right side, Bicuspid (Mitral) → Left side

8. Semilunar Valves

The Mnemonic: PA

Pulmonary, Aortic

9. Great Blood Vessels

The Mnemonic: SAPC

Superior vena cava, Aorta, Pulmonary artery, C (Pulmonary veins - Carry blood to the heart)

10. Heart Conduction System

The Mnemonic: Some Angry People Build Very Good Hearts

SA Node, AV Node, Purkinje (Bundle of His), Bundle Branches, Very (Purkinje Fibres)

11. Pacemaker of the Heart

The Mnemonic: SA Starts All

SA Node = Natural pacemaker

12. Coronary Arteries

The Mnemonic: RL

Right Coronary Artery, Left Coronary Artery

13. Branches of Left Coronary Artery

The Mnemonic: LC Gives LAD & Circumflex

Left Coronary gives the LAD (Left Anterior Descending) and Circumflex artery.

14. Types of Blood Vessels

The Mnemonic: AVC

Arteries, Veins, Capillaries

15. Layers of Blood Vessels

The Mnemonic: IAA

Tunica Intima, Tunica Media (A), Tunica Adventitia

16. Arteries vs Veins

The Mnemonic: AV

Arteries: Away from heart, thick wall, high pressure. Veins: Towards heart, thin wall, Valves present.

17. Pulmonary Circulation

The Mnemonic: Right → Lungs → Left

Right ventricle → Pulmonary artery → Lungs → Pulmonary veins → Left atrium. Carries oxygen-poor blood to the lungs and returns oxygen-rich blood to the heart.

18. Systemic Circulation

The Mnemonic: Left → Body → Right

Left ventricle → Aorta → Body → Vena cava → Right atrium

19. Oxygenated Blood

The Mnemonic: Left Loves Oxygen

The Left side of the heart carries oxygen-rich blood.

20. Deoxygenated Blood

The Mnemonic: Right Returns Blood

The Right side of the heart carries oxygen-poor blood.

21. Exceptions in Circulation

The Mnemonic: PA Poor, PV Pure

Pulmonary Artery → Poor (Deoxygenated blood). Pulmonary Vein → Pure (Oxygenated blood).

22. Fetal Circulation

The Mnemonic: DVD

Ductus venosus, Foramen ovale (think "Valve-like opening"), Ductus arteriosus

23. Heart Sounds

The Mnemonic: LUB-DUB

Lub (S1) → AV valves close. Dub (S2) → Semilunar valves close.

24. Cardiac Cycle

The Mnemonic: Fill → Pump → Relax

Ventricular filling → Ventricular systole → Ventricular diastole

25. Blood Pressure

The Mnemonic: 120/80

Normal adult blood pressure ≈ 120/80 mmHg

26. Pulse Points

The Mnemonic: CFBPDPT

Carotid, Femoral, Brachial, Popliteal, Dorsalis pedis, Posterior tibial, Temporal

27. Portal Vein Formation

The Mnemonic: SM + Splenic = Portal

Superior Mesenteric Vein + Splenic Vein form the Portal vein.

28. Blood Vessel Order

The Mnemonic: AACCVV

Artery, Arteriole, Capillary, Venule, Vein

29. Major Veins

The Mnemonic: SIV

Superior vena cava, Inferior vena cava, Pulmonary veins

30. Coronary Veins

The Mnemonic: GMS

Great cardiac vein, Middle cardiac vein, Small cardiac vein

31. Clinical Conditions

The Mnemonic: MASH

Myocardial infarction, Angina, Stroke, Hypertension
